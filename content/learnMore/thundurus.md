## Who is Thundurus?

Thundurus is an Electric/Flying-type Legendary Pokémon from Generation V, one of the Forces of Nature with Tornadus and Landorus. Incarnate form is a swaggering trickster; Therian form a sleek special attacker—both reshaped competitive weather teams for years.

Unovan farmers once cursed Thundurus for lightning strikes until learning it also fertilizes fields. Its mythic role balances destruction and renewal in storm lore.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Thundurus rides lightning bolts between clouds, leaving ozone-scented trails. Pokédex entries describe pranks on humans who disrespect nature, burning barns of poachers specifically.

Therian form sheds mischief for hunting focus, diving at prey faster than sound according to eyewitness pilots.

## Battle Performance

Thundurus-Incarnate runs Prankster Thunder Wave and Taunt, or Nasty Plot with Thunderbolt and Focus Blast. Therian hits harder with 145 Special Attack and Sludge Wave coverage.

Both forms appear in OU; Incarnate disrupts, Therian sweeps. Ground immunity via Flying typing is key, but Ice and Rock hurt.
