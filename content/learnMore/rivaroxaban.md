## What is Rivaroxaban?

Rivaroxaban is a direct oral factor Xa inhibitor anticoagulant that blocks the common pathway converting prothrombin to thrombin. Once-daily dosing is possible for atrial fibrillation and DVT/PE treatment at higher doses; lower doses are used for VTE prophylaxis after orthopedic surgery.

Partial metabolism via CYP3A4 and CYP2J2 and renal excretion (~33% unchanged) require dose adjustment in renal impairment and caution with strong interacting drugs. Andexanet alfa can reverse anti-factor Xa activity in life-threatening bleeding.

Food increases absorption of the 15 mg and 20 mg treatment doses (take with evening meal per labeling).

## Primary Uses

- Nonvalvular atrial fibrillation stroke prevention
- Treatment of DVT and PE
- Reduction in risk of recurrent DVT and PE
- Postoperative VTE prophylaxis after hip or knee replacement

Not for mechanical heart valves or moderate-to-severe mitral stenosis.

## Common Side Effects

- Bleeding (major and minor)
- Bruising
- Nausea, dyspepsia
- Elevated liver enzymes (uncommon)
- **Warnings:** Spinal hematoma with neuraxial anesthesia; discontinue before surgery per timing guidelines; avoid without dose adjustment in advanced CKD for AF indications; drug interactions with strong CYP3A4/P-gp inhibitors and inducers.
