## Who is Gothitelle?

Gothitelle is a Psychic-type Pokémon from Generation V, evolving from Gothorita. Its gothic lolita silhouette and galaxy-tracing ribbons position it as an astronomer oracle in Unova—reading futures in star patterns while trapping foes in battle.

Competitive history remembers Gothitelle less for damage than for Shadow Tag persecution in VGC—a polarizing control piece. Lore-wise, it is a cosmic librarian of fate.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Gothitelle’s psychic power bends space slightly, explaining rare reports of duplicated constellations near its roosts. Pokédex entries claim it can see Pokémon and trainers’ life spans, a grim gift rarely verified ethically.

It builds nests from meteor fragments, humming lullabies that synchronize with planetary rotation—a meditation practice adopted by some psychic gym traditions.

## Battle Performance

Gothitelle with Shadow Tag and Trick Room was a doubles menace, pairing with Perish Song or setup sweepers. Calm Mind and Thunderbolt sets exist in singles with Competitive on Gothorita’s line historically.

Power creep reduced its dominance; Frisk counters Illusion, while Dark-types immune to trapping ignore its plan. Support sets use Heal Pulse and Fake Tears in niche formats.
