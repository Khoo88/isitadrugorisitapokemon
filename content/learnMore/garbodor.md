## Who is Garbodor?

Garbodor is a Poison-type Pokémon from Generation V, evolving from Trubbish. Born from industrial waste in Unova cities, it embodies environmental commentary—a walking landfill with a crooked grin and toxic fumes.

Post-apocalyptic fiction in the Pokémon world often features Garbodor swarms reclaiming abandoned factories. Trainers respect its utility while cities fund Trubbish cleanup programs to prevent evolution outbreaks.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Garbodor compresses trash into toxic sludge pellets fired like cannonballs. Pokédex entries note regional scent differences based on diet—Galar specimens smell sweeter due to bakery waste, a dark culinary footnote.

When two Garbodor clash, they merge refuse into towering heaps that become new breeding grounds for Trubbish, accelerating urban decay cycles.

## Battle Performance

Garbodor gets Garbodor-Gmax and Weak Armor physical sets with Gunk Shot and Drain Punch. Stench and Aftermath provide niche utility.

It competes in lower tiers with Spikes and Toxic Spikes support. Galarian form with Slow Start on evolution line is separate; standard Garbodor remains a bulky physical attacker with Explosion options.
