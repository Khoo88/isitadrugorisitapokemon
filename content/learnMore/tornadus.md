## Who is Tornadus?

Tornadus is a Flying-type Legendary Pokémon from Generation V, the pure Flying member of the Forces of Nature. Incarnate form is a genie-like storm spirit; Therian form a razor-fast hawk, both defining early Gen V weather offense.

Legends blame Tornadus for tornado outbreaks across Unova, though meteorologists note beneficial pollen dispersal follows its seasonal migrations.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Tornadus’s tail spins hurricanes when angry, yet gentle breezes when pleased. Pokédex entries describe it flying at 200 mph, tearing roofs off only from structures built on sacred wind paths.

Therian form molts feathers that conduct electricity, used in traditional grounding rods for villages.

## Battle Performance

Tornadus-Incarnate ran Prankster Rain Dance and Hurricane in eternal rain cores. Therian uses Acrobatics, Superpower, and Tailwind in doubles.

Pure Flying is unique; Stealth Rock and Electric types check it. Defiant sets on Incarnate punish Intimidate in doubles history alongside Thundurus.
