## Who is Guzzlord?

Guzzlord is a Dark/Dragon-type Ultra Beast from Generation VII, codename UB-05 Glutton. Its endless maw and alien proportions embody consumption without satiation—falling through Ultra Wormholes into Alola with catastrophic appetite.

Aether Foundation reports document Guzzlord devouring mountains of waste and wildlife alike. It is horror-comedy and existential threat rolled into one Ultra Beast poster child.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Guzzlord’s mouth leads to a dimensionally suspicious stomach—excretions are never found. Pokédex entries claim constant hunger, yet it never grows fat, violating conservation of mass in unsettling ways.

Roaring sounds like tearing metal echo before raids on scrapyards, where it eats vehicles whole, tires and all.

## Battle Performance

Guzzlord is a slow special wallbreaker with 223 HP and Dragon Hammer, Heavy Slam, and Knock Off. Beast Boost is unavailable; it relies on raw stats.

Trick Room teams use its power under slow speed. Fighting, Fairy, and Ice weaknesses hurt, but it tanks hits that would KO frailer sweepers before firing back with coverage-heavy attacks.
