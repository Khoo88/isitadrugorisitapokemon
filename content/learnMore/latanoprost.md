## What is Latanoprost?

Latanoprost is a prostaglandin F2-alpha analog applied topically to the eye that increases uveoscleral outflow of aqueous humor, lowering intraocular pressure in open-angle glaucoma and ocular hypertension. It is a first-line agent in many glaucoma treatment algorithms.

Systemic absorption is minimal, but iris pigmentation darkening (especially in mixed-color irides), eyelash growth, and periorbital fat atrophy (prostaglandin-associated periorbitopathy) can occur with chronic use. Macular edema risk increases in aphakic patients and those with uveitis.

Once-daily evening dosing is standard; more frequent dosing may reduce efficacy (paradoxical).

## Primary Uses

- Open-angle glaucoma
- Ocular hypertension
- Adjunct when beta-blockers or other agents insufficient

Often first-line monotherapy before combination drops.

## Common Side Effects

- Conjunctival hyperemia
- Iris color darkening (may be permanent)
- Eyelash growth (longer, thicker, darker)
- Ocular burning, stinging
- Blurred vision
- Periorbital fat loss with long-term use
- **Warnings:** Use with caution in uveitis and cystoid macular edema history; preserve single-use vial sterility; contact lenses removed during instillation per product instructions.
