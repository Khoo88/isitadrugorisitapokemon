## Who is Shiinotic?

Shiinotic is a Grass/Fairy-type Pokémon from Generation VII, evolving from Morelull. Its bioluminescent mushroom cap and spore clouds define Alola’s night forests as fairy-ring theaters—beautiful, disorienting, and ecologically vital.

Hikers follow Shiinotic lights believing them safe paths; sometimes they are, sometimes they lead to predator ambushes orchestrated by the colony itself.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Shiinotic communicates via flickering light patterns that induce drowsiness. Pokédex entries describe it draining strength from sleeping prey with root-like feet while shining soothing lights—a sinister nightlight.

Colonies sync flashes during rain, creating aurora-like displays mistaken for Ultra Wormhole activity.

## Battle Performance

Shiinotic is a PU support Pokémon with Strength Sap healing, Moonblast, and Spore or Sleep Powder control. Effect Spore punishes contact with sleep or poison.

Its low Speed requires Trick Room or doubles synergy. Grass/Fairy typing resists Fighting but struggles against Poison and Steel special attackers.
