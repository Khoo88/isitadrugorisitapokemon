## Who is Primarina?

Primarina is a Water/Fairy-type Pokémon from Generation VII, the final evolution of Popplio and Alola’s Moon version starter ace. Its siren seal design conducts sound into destructive bubbles, positioning it as performer and artillery in one.

League champions and contest halls alike revere Primarina for operatic battles where melody becomes hydro cannon. It embodies fairy-type elegance applied to starter narrative stakes.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Primarina’s voice can burst eardrums of prey at close range, yet soothes allies in healing harmonics. Pokédex entries warn that over-singing strains its vocal cords, forcing rest days between major performances.

It crafts bubble choreography that refracts light into rainbows, used in Alolan festivals to signal safe sailing weather.

## Battle Performance

Primarina is a bulky special attacker with Torrent or Liquid Voice Hyper Voice, Moonblast, and Sparkling Aria burn cure. Competitive sets use Assault Vest or Calm Mind.

It checks dragons and dark types in UU; weak to Grass and Electric. Liquid Voice bypasses soundproof, making Hyper Voice a consistent STAB under ability.
