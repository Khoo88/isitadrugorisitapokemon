## Who is Cresselia?

Cresselia is a Psychic-type Legendary Pokémon from Generation IV, associated with peaceful sleep and the dispelling of nightmares. Its crescent wings and aurora trails appear over Sinnoh’s Fullmoon Island in lore tied to Darkrai’s shadow.

Myths describe Cresselia’s feathers granting restful dreams to children plagued by terrors. Hospitals in canalave regions once kept feather charms, blending superstition with genuine calming pheromones.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Cresselia leaves aurora streaks as it flies, particles that soothe neural activity. Pokédex entries claim touching its down induces weeks of serene sleep—overharvesting led to protective ordinances.

It rarely fights directly, preferring to flee while emitting psychic lullabies that pacify aggressors. When cornered, its defensive aura can repel entire packs.

## Battle Performance

Cresselia is a premier special wall with Moonlight, Thunder Wave, and Lunar Dance support. Toxic and Psyshock chip damage while it outlasts sweepers across long battles.

In doubles, Icy Wind speed control and Trick Room reversal are common. Its massive HP and Defense make it a staple in formats allowing restricted legends, though Taunt and Dark-types pressure it.
