## Who is Veluza?

Veluza is a Water/Psychic-type Pokémon from Generation IX, native to Paldea’s coastal waters. Its filet-like body sheds psychic-enhanced flesh to lighten itself for speed bursts—a grotesque yet efficient biology that shocked diners and researchers alike.

Paldean cuisine controversy surrounds Veluza meat, which regenerates and boosts mental clarity when eaten ethically from shed portions, not living tissue.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Veluza discards excess flesh telekinetically, storing it for later reattachment. Pokédex entries describe the shed meat as delicious and focus-enhancing, creating sustainable harvest practices when regulated.

Schools coordinate hunts by sharing psychic maps of prey locations, then reunite to merge flesh after feeding.

## Battle Performance

Veluza has Fillet Away, sacrificing HP for massive Speed and Attack boosts. Aqua Cutter and Psycho Cut benefit from Sharpness.

It is a glass cannon in PU—setup once and sweep, or die trying. Priority and bulk punish Fillet Away turns before they snowball.
