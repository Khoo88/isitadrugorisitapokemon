## Who is Clodsire?

Clodsire is a Poison/Ground-type Pokémon introduced in Generation IX as Paldea’s evolved Wooper form—brown, pudgy, and unmistakably meme-famous for its simple grin. It represents a regional divergence from Johto’s blue Wooper line, adapted to marshy Paldean soils.

Paldean guides joke that Clodsire “survived design scrutiny by being lovable,” yet ecology texts emphasize its toxin-rich mud baths and surprising resilience in toxic wetlands.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Clodsire stores poison in tail glands, releasing clouds when stepped on—accidentally or deliberately. Pokédex entries describe it wallowing in oily bogs that would harm other species, emerging coated in protective film.

Herds create maze-like wallows that trap prey; their stillness makes them resemble mud lumps until predators are ankle-deep.

## Battle Performance

Clodsire is a bulky physical wall with Unaware, ignoring stat boosts while using Earthquake, Poison Jab, and Recover. Spikes or Toxic Spikes support synergize with its defensive role.

Water Absorb grants immunity to Water moves, improving pivoting. It competes in OU as a reliable counter to special sweepers and setup threats when paired with removal.
