## Who is Florges?

Florges is a Fairy-type Pokémon from Generation VI, the final evolution of Flabébé and Floette. Kalos royalty once appointed Florges as garden sovereigns, trusting them to maintain palace grounds for centuries longer than human landscapers could.

Its flower crown color reflects the lifetime devotion to a single garden plot. In the Pokémon universe, Florges symbolizes elegance, territorial plant mastery, and the fairy type’s arrival in modern competitive culture.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Florges draws life force from soil, reviving wilted acres overnight. Pokédex entries describe it repelling invaders with pollen storms that induce sleep, then replanting stolen flowers with punitive precision.

A Florges that abandons its garden wilts within days—a biological bond so strong that trainers must transplant entire plots when relocating specimens.

## Battle Performance

Florges is a special wall with huge Special Defense, Aromatherapy, Wish, and Moonblast. Symbiosis can pass items to allies in doubles.

Offensive sets use Calm Mind with stored power potential in niche formats. It checks dragons and dark types in OU historically, though power creep reduced its dominance while it remains a premier cleric.
