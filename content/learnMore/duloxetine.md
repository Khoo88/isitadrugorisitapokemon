## What is Duloxetine?

Duloxetine is a serotonin-norepinephrine reuptake inhibitor (SNRI) that blocks reuptake transporters for both neurotransmitters, increasing synaptic concentrations and modulating descending pain inhibitory pathways in the spinal cord. Dual mechanism underlies antidepressant and analgesic effects in neuropathic and centralized pain states.

Hepatic metabolism via CYP1A2 and CYP2D6; avoid in substantial liver disease. It is also a mild inhibitor of neuronal nicotinic receptors in vitro, though clinical relevance is uncertain.

Discontinuation syndrome can occur with abrupt stopping—gradual taper recommended.

## Primary Uses

- Major depressive disorder
- Generalized anxiety disorder
- Diabetic peripheral neuropathic pain
- Fibromyalgia
- Chronic musculoskeletal pain (chronic low back pain, osteoarthritis knee pain—regional approvals vary)
- Stress urinary incontinence (selected regions, higher doses)

Often chosen when depression and pain coexist.

## Common Side Effects

- Nausea (common early, often improves)
- Dry mouth, constipation
- Fatigue, somnolence
- Dizziness
- Sweating
- Sexual dysfunction
- Elevated blood pressure (monitor in hypertensive patients)
- Serotonin syndrome (with other serotonergic agents)
- **Warnings:** Avoid in uncontrolled narrow-angle glaucoma and heavy alcohol use with liver disease; hepatotoxicity risk in pre-existing liver disease; suicidality monitoring (antidepressant class); do not combine with MAOIs.
