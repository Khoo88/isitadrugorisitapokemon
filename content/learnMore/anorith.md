## Who is Anorith?

Anorith is a Rock/Bug-type fossil Pokémon revived from the Claw Fossil in Generation III. It represents an ancient marine arthropod lineage predating most modern Bug-types, linking Hoenn’s paleontological past to contemporary ecosystems through resurrection technology.

Within lore, Anorith is studied as a living snapshot of primordial seas—aggressive, armored, and reliant on swift claw strikes. Museums in Rustboro and Pewter display restored specimens alongside Aerodactyl to illustrate divergent prehistoric niches.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Pokédex entries describe Anorith’s dual claws as hydraulic cutting tools, capable of piercing prey shells and carving nests into coral stone. It is said to molt rigid plates as it grows, leaving fossilized shards that confuse researchers about its true size.

Behaviorally, revived Anorith display strong territorial instincts in aquarium habitats, battling Cradily relatives when food is scarce. Some entries suggest it once swam in schools that darkened shallow seas with synchronized molting events.

## Battle Performance

Anorith evolves into Armaldo at level 40, so its battle role is primarily as a swift physical attacker in lower tiers before evolution. It learns Rock Blast, X-Scissor, and Aqua Jet early, giving offensive momentum.

As a standalone fossil stage, Anorith is rarely used competitively except in niche “Little Cup” or draft formats emphasizing ancient typings and Sand Rush synergy after evolution planning.
