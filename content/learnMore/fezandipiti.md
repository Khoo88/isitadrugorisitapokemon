## Who is Fezandipiti?

Fezandipiti is a Poison/Fairy-type Legendary Pokémon from Generation IX, one of the Loyal Three of Kitakami. Its peacock mask and toxic charisma embody tragic folklore—a vain guardian corrupted by resentment after losing to Ogerpon’s ancient hero.

Kitakami festivals retell Fezandipiti’s fall from beloved protector to vengeful poisoner, making it a moral tale about pride more than raw destruction. Its typing and design merge royal court drama with toxic battlefield control.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Fezandipiti’s feathers secrete pheromones that compel admiration, masking lethal toxins in its wing fans. Pokédex entries claim it once healed hearts with dance, now twisting emotions into obsession.

Mask fragments left after battles are treated as cursed relics; shamans bury them in salt to prevent “love sickness” among villagers.

## Battle Performance

Fezandipiti is a fast special attacker with Toxic Chain, badly poisoning on most moves. Moonblast, Sludge Wave, and U-turn form offensive cores; Tera Blast expands coverage.

It competes in OU as a breaker that punishes defensive teams with unavoidable toxic pressure. Fairy/Poison typing resists common moves but struggles against Steel and Psychic threats.
