## Who is Gulpin?

Gulpin is a Poison-type Pokémon from Generation III, evolving into Swalot. Its entire body is a stomach, a living blob that swallows prey larger than itself—a gross-out favorite of Hoenn safari tours and poison-type specialists.

Hoenn comedy clubs joke about Gulpin as nature’s garbage disposal, yet ecologists credit it with controlling pest populations in urban alleys without toxic litter left behind.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Gulpin’s stomach acid dissolves anything organic within hours, including metals at diluted concentrations. Pokédex entries highlight it not producing droppings—all matter converted to energy.

It inflates to intimidate, then deflates through pores—a venting process that smells like fermented berries, attracting prey ironically.

## Battle Performance

Gulpin is a curiosity in Little Cup with Liquid Ooze or Sticky Hold, Pain Split, and Sludge. Encore and Yawn support appear on Swalot more than Gulpin.

Stats are poor; competitive play targets Swalot with Belch, Stockpile, and Gunk Shot. Gulpin’s Amnesia sets are memetic at best in LC.
