## What is Aemcolo?

Aemcolo is a delayed-release formulation of rifamycin SV, a non-absorbed oral antibiotic derived from the rifamycin class. Unlike systemic rifamycins (rifampin), rifamycin SV in this formulation acts locally in the gastrointestinal lumen with minimal systemic absorption, limiting drug interactions and systemic adverse effects.

The delayed-release coating (MMX technology) targets delivery to the colon, where it exerts antibacterial activity against enteric pathogens associated with travelers’ diarrhea. Its mechanism involves inhibition of bacterial RNA synthesis by binding DNA-dependent RNA polymerase in susceptible organisms such as noninvasive strains of Escherichia coli.

Because systemic exposure is low, Aemcolo is distinguished from rifampin for indications requiring high intestinal concentrations without broad systemic antimicrobial effects.

## Primary Uses

- Treatment of travelers’ diarrhea caused by noninvasive strains of Escherichia coli in adults
- Short-course therapy (typically 3 days) initiated when diarrhea occurs during or after travel to endemic regions

It is not indicated for invasive or bloody diarrhea, fever, or suspected pathogens outside the labeled spectrum (e.g., Salmonella, Shigella, Campylobacter), where alternative antibiotics and evaluation are required.

## Common Side Effects

- Generally mild given minimal systemic absorption
- Constipation
- Headache
- Abdominal distension, flatulence
- Nausea
- **Warnings:** Not for use in diarrhea complicated by fever, blood in stool, or systemic toxicity; not studied extensively in children or pregnancy; patients with severe hepatic impairment should use with caution per labeling.
