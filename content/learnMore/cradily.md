## Who is Cradily?

Cradily is a Rock/Grass-type fossil Pokémon from Generation III, revived from the Root Fossil. It resembles a prehistoric sea lily or crinoid raised onto a mobile stalk—an ancient filter feeder turned territorial shoreline guardian.

Hoenn museums pair Cradily with Anorith line exhibits to show divergent prehistoric niches: sessile photosynthesis versus mobile predation. Its calm demeanor belies powerful anchoring strength.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Cradily’s tentacles photosynthesize while rooting into bedrock for minerals. Pokédex entries describe it anchoring in tidal zones, extending vine lashes to capture prey swept by currents.

During full moons, colonies open petal crowns to absorb light, illuminating beaches—a phenomenon mistaken for bioluminescent algae blooms.

## Battle Performance

Cradily is a special wall with Suction Cups preventing phazing and Storm Drain redirecting Water moves. Giga Drain, Ancient Power, and Stealth Rock support stall teams.

Weaknesses to Fighting and Steel hurt, but Recover and Toxic stall out unprepared teams. Specially defensive spreads counter Rain sweepers effectively in niche formats.
