## Who is Camerupt?

Camerupt is a Fire/Ground-type Pokémon from Generation III, evolving from Numel. Its dual volcanic humps and rugged hide make it the backbone of arid-route travel in Hoenn and beyond—often used as a pack Pokémon crossing magma fields.

Volcanic observatories employ Camerupt to predict eruptions, monitoring tremors through hooves sensitive to magma chamber pressure. Trainers respect its stubborn but loyal temperament.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Magma stored in Camerupt’s humps can exceed 10,000°C according to exaggerated Pokédex entries, erupting when angered. It sheds molten rock plates as armor, leaving glassy trails on mountainsides.

Herds migrate along geothermal vents, grazing mineral-rich ash that fuels internal furnaces. Young Numel ride atop adults during ash storms, sheltered between humps.

## Battle Performance

Mega Camerupt was a powerful Trick Room attacker with Sheer Force-boosted Eruptions. Standard Camerupt runs Choice Specs or Assault Vest sets with Eruption, Earth Power, and Flamethrower.

Solid Attack and Special Attack make it a mixed threat, but 4x Water weakness and low Speed outside Trick Room limit its ceiling. Anger Point crit builds remain memetic in casual play.
