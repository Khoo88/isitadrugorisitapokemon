## Who is Ilumise?

Ilumise is a Bug-type Pokémon from Generation III, paired with Volbeat as firefly dancers of Hoenn nights. The “Firefly Pokémon” leads glowstick choreography in swamps, guiding Volbeat formations that astronomers once confused with UFOs.

Ilumise represents cooperative species storytelling—less combat legend than ecological romance. Its standing is cultural, tied to breeding mechanics and berry plot folklore.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Ilumise’s pheromones direct Volbeat to draw geometric light patterns for mating displays. Pokédex entries describe it painting swamps with scent trails visible only to other bug types.

It pollinates night-blooming flowers while dances proceed, linking art and agriculture in Hoenn festival calendars.

## Battle Performance

Ilumise learns Tail Glow, Baton Pass, and Encore support but stats are too low for serious tiers. Prankster is unavailable; it remains a novelty breeder with Wish and Seismic Toss.

Competitive bug dancers favor Volbeat/Illumise doubles gimmicks in casual play. Serious formats ignore both except for egg move chains.
