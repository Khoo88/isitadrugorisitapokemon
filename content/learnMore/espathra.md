## Who is Espathra?

Espathra is a Psychic-type Pokémon introduced in Generation IX, evolving from Flittle. Its ostrich dancer aesthetic and hypnotic leg movements made it a Paldea controversy—stylish, fast, and tied to urban performance culture in Cascarrafa plazas.

Trainers describe Espathra as vain yet disciplined, practicing mirror poses for hours before battle. Its debut generation framed it as a symbol of speed-based psychic offense in a region obsessed with terastal spectacle.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Espathra stores psychic energy in leg tendons, releasing it through rhythmic stomps that induce trance in observers. Pokédex entries warn that prolonged eye contact with its plumage patterns causes dizziness—street performers exploit this for crowd control.

Molted feathers retain psychic residue, sold illegally as “focus charms” until League crackdowns linked them to migraines in children.

## Battle Performance

Espathra dominated early Gen IX with Speed Boost (now banned on it in many formats) and Calm Mind sets running Stored Power or Dazzling Gleam. Even without Speed Boost, it remains a fast special sweeper with Lumina Crash and Shadow Ball coverage.

Its shallow defenses and Dark-type weakness demand momentum; once setup, few Pokémon outspeed and survive a boosted special attack.
