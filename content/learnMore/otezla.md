## What is Otezla?

Otezla (apremilast) is a small-molecule phosphodiesterase-4 (PDE4) inhibitor that increases intracellular cyclic AMP in immune cells, modulating production of proinflammatory cytokines such as TNF-alpha, IL-23, and IL-17. Unlike biologics, it is oral and does not require injection or routine laboratory monitoring for immunosuppression in the same manner as methotrexate.

PDE4 inhibition in the central nervous system may contribute to gastrointestinal and neuropsychiatric side effects. It is not an immunosuppressant in the classic sense but alters inflammatory signaling pathways relevant to psoriasis and psoriatic arthritis.

Titration over the first week reduces gastrointestinal intolerance. No mandatory tuberculosis screening is required before initiation, distinguishing it from TNF inhibitors.

## Primary Uses

- Plaque psoriasis (moderate to severe)
- Psoriatic arthritis
- Behçet disease oral ulcers (approved indication in some regions)
- Oral ulcers in Behçet syndrome

Used when topical therapy is insufficient and patients prefer oral non-biologic systemic therapy or have contraindications to biologics.

## Common Side Effects

- Diarrhea, nausea, vomiting (common during titration)
- Headache
- Weight loss
- Upper respiratory tract infections
- Depression, suicidal ideation (boxed warning—monitor mood)
- **Warnings:** Contraindicated in severe renal impairment (requires dose reduction in moderate impairment); weigh psychiatric history; not studied extensively in pregnancy; discontinue if severe depression develops.
