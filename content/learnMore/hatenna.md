## Who is Hatenna?

Hatenna is a Psychic-type Pokémon from Generation VIII, the first stage of the Hatterene line. It senses emotions through its horn and flees conflict, embodying Galar’s witch-hat fairy tale aesthetic from quiet hamlets to competitive doubles titans.

Galarian nurseries once kept Hatenna to detect lies in courtrooms—a practice banned when privacy concerns arose. Its gentle nature masks an evolution into formidable battlefield control.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Hatenna only approaches trainers with calm hearts, hiding from loud arguments. Pokédex entries describe it amplifying positive emotions in sick rooms, a natural therapy animal with strict consent boundaries.

Its hat-like fur grows with age, eventually covering eyes in later evolutions as psychic focus internalizes.

## Battle Performance

Hatenna learns Magic Powder and Psychic in early levels but lacks stats for serious play. Competitive value arrives with Hatterene’s Magic Bounce and Dazzling Gleam.

In LC, Eviolite sets with Calm Mind are experimental. Mostly a narrative stepping-stone toward one of VGC’s strongest fairy/psychic walls.
