## What is Cyclobenzaprine?

Cyclobenzaprine is a centrally acting skeletal muscle relaxant structurally related to tricyclic antidepressants. It reduces tonic somatic motor activity at brainstem level without direct relaxation of skeletal muscle fibers. Mechanism likely involves increased norepinephine-mediated inhibition of alpha motor neurons.

Sedation is prominent, limiting daytime use in many patients. It is intended for short-term use (typically 2–3 weeks) alongside rest and physical therapy for acute musculoskeletal spasm.

Active metabolites contribute to anticholinergic effects shared with TCA class.

## Primary Uses

- Short-term relief of muscle spasm associated with acute painful musculoskeletal conditions
- Adjunct to rest and physical therapy for neck and back strain (limited evidence for long-term benefit)

Not recommended for elderly patients or as chronic pain management per Beers Criteria concerns.

## Common Side Effects

- Sedation, drowsiness
- Dry mouth
- Dizziness
- Anticholinergic effects (constipation, urinary retention, blurred vision)
- Confusion (especially in elderly)
- **Warnings:** Contraindicated within 14 days of MAO inhibitors; avoid in hyperthyroidism, heart failure, conduction disorders, and acute recovery phase post-MI; serotonin syndrome risk with serotonergic drugs; similar overdose toxicity to tricyclics at high doses.
