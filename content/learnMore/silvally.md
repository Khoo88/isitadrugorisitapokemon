## Who is Silvally?

Silvally is a Normal-type Legendary Pokémon from Generation VII, evolved from Type: Null when friendship breaks its control helmet. Aether Foundation’s weapon project redeemed into customizable guardian, it changes type via held memories.

Gladion’s partner story gave Silvally emotional weight—synthetic chimera becoming true individual. In lore it represents liberation from corporate weapon programs across the Pokémon world.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Silvally’s RKS System rewrites its cellular structure to match memory discs, a painless metamorphosis lasting seconds. Pokédex entries describe it protecting allies at cost of its own safety, contrasting its creation purpose.

Discarded helmets in Aether Paradise became memorials for failed Type: Null subjects, making Silvally evolution a symbol of survivor advocacy.

## Battle Performance

Silvally runs any type with appropriate memory, often with Explosion, Multi-Attack, and U-turn. Battle Factory sets vary by disc.

Stats are spread evenly but unspectacular; it rarely dominates OU, yet type flexibility grants niche roles in formats allowing Legendaries—checking specific threats with correct disc.
