## Who is Kyurem?

Kyurem is a Dragon/Ice-type Legendary Pokémon from Generation V, the abandoned third dragon of Unova’s Tao trio. Its empty, frozen body can fuse with Reshiram or Zekrom into White or Black Kyurem, rewriting power balance in myth and competitive tiers alike.

Giant Chasm folklore paints Kyurem as the dragon that fell when truth and ideals clashed, waiting to complete itself. It is tragedy, weapon, and weather anomaly in one.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Kyurem leaks absolute-zero air from its wings, freezing moisture into permanent snowfields around its lair. Pokédex entries describe it devouring Pokémon and legends to fill its hollow core—a grim hunger narrative.

Fusion forms radiate thermal energy opposite their nature, warping local climates for weeks after separation.

## Battle Performance

Black Kyurem and White Kyurem dominate Ubers with Teravolt/Turboblaze-boosted Fusion Bolt/Bolt Strike and Draco Meteor. Base Kyurem offers Choice Specs Ice Beam spam and Glaciate speed control.

Ice/Dragon typing is offensive heaven, defensively hell. Kyurem forms remain restricted picks whenever allowed, with unparalleled mixed attacking stats.
