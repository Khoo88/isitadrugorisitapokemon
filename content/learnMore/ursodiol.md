## What is Ursodiol?

Ursodiol (ursodeoxycholic acid) is a hydrophilic bile acid that replaces hydrophobic toxic bile acids in the bile acid pool, protecting hepatocytes and cholangiocytes from injury. It improves bile flow (choleresis) and may immunomodulate in cholestatic liver diseases.

Oral absorption is good; it is secreted into bile and undergoes enterohepatic circulation. Dissolution of cholesterol gallstones occurs slowly over months in selected patients with functioning gallbladder and radiolucent stones.

Also used in primary biliary cholangitis to slow disease progression and improve liver tests.

## Primary Uses

- Primary biliary cholangitis (formerly primary biliary cirrhosis)
- Dissolution of cholesterol gallstones (selected patients)
- Prevention of gallstones in rapid weight loss or bile acid malabsorption
- Intrahepatic cholestasis of pregnancy (off-label, specialist use)
- Cystic fibrosis–associated liver disease (adjunct)

## Common Side Effects

- Diarrhea, loose stools
- Abdominal pain, bloating
- Nausea
- Hair thinning (uncommon)
- **Warnings:** Not effective for calcified or pigment stones or nonfunctioning gallbladder; monitor liver enzymes in PBC; may increase cholesterol saturation transiently in some patients; discontinue if no response in stone dissolution by 12–18 months.
