## What is Etanercept?

Etanercept is a fusion protein combining the extracellular domain of the human tumor necrosis factor (TNF) receptor with the Fc portion of IgG1, acting as a soluble decoy receptor that binds TNF-alpha and lymphotoxin, preventing them from activating cellular TNF receptors. This reduces proinflammatory cytokine signaling in rheumatoid arthritis, psoriatic disease, and spondyloarthropathies.

Subcutaneous self-administration weekly or twice weekly depending on indication. It was among the first biologic TNF inhibitors widely adopted in rheumatology and dermatology.

Serious infections, including tuberculosis reactivation, require screening before initiation like other anti-TNF agents.

## Primary Uses

- Rheumatoid arthritis (moderate to severe, often with methotrexate)
- Psoriatic arthritis
- Ankylosing spondylitis and non-radiographic axial spondyloarthritis
- Plaque psoriasis
- Juvenile idiopathic arthritis (polyarticular)

Step therapy through conventional DMARDs is common in insurance protocols.

## Common Side Effects

- Injection-site reactions
- Upper respiratory infections
- Headache
- Serious bacterial, fungal, and opportunistic infections
- Tuberculosis reactivation
- Heart failure exacerbation (caution in NYHA III/IV)
- Demyelinating disorders (rare)
- Malignancy concerns with long-term immunosuppression
- **Warnings:** TB and hepatitis B screening; avoid live vaccines; discontinue for serious infection; monitor for neurologic symptoms.
