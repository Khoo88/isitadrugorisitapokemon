## Who is Solgaleo?

Solgaleo is a Psychic/Steel-type Legendary Pokémon from Generation VII, mascot of Pokémon Sun and evolution of Cosmog into the “beast that devours the sun.” It radiates solar light, opening Ultra Wormholes with roars that shake continents.

Alolan royalty treated Solgaleo as emissary of dawn, partnering with Lunala in cosmic cycles. Its standing is among the most narratively central legendaries of the modern era.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Solgaleo’s mane burns at temperatures rivaling the sun’s surface, yet does not harm chosen riders. Pokédex entries describe it crossing dimensions by running at light speed, leaving plasma trails.

Necrozma fusion into Dusk Mane form merges eclipse symbolism—day swallowed by alien hunger in apocalyptic lore.

## Battle Performance

Solgaleo is a physical tank with Sunsteel Strike (ignores abilities), Zen Headbutt, and Morning Sun. High Attack and bulk suit bulky offensive roles in VGC restricted formats.

Weak to Fire, Ground, Dark, and Ghost in typings; Steel/Psychic resists Fairy. It checks Tapu Lele and sets up sweeps with Cosmog narrative teams in doubles.
