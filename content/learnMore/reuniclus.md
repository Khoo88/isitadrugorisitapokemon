## Who is Reuniclus?

Reuniclus is a Psychic-type Pokémon from Generation V, evolving from Duosion. Its gel-brain inside a protective arm is a living metaphor for unified consciousness—three cells merged into one tactical mind.

Unovan labs study Reuniclus for distributed cognition models, while trainers respect its calm demeanor masking explosive special power in Trick Room metas.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Reuniclus’s arms can detach telekinetically, acting as secondary brains during complex calculations. Pokédex entries describe it increasing alcohol concentration in foes’ blood via psychic fermentation—a bizarre offensive flavor text.

When two Reuniclus merge thoughts, local psychic noise disables electronics for blocks—a phenomenon called “white static Sundays” in Castelia.

## Battle Performance

Reuniclus is a Trick Room sweeper with Magic Guard, Focus Punch or Calm Mind, and Recovery. Life Orb sets fire Psyshock and Signal Beam without recoil damage thanks to Magic Guard.

Slow Speed is an asset under Trick Room. Fighting and Dark types check it; otherwise it tanks hits and sets up unstoppable special sweeps.
