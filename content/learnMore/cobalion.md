## Who is Cobalion?

Cobalion is a Steel/Fighting-type Legendary Pokémon from Generation V, leader of the Swords of Justice alongside Terrakion, Virizion, and Keldeo. It embodies discipline, strategy, and protection of Pokémon harmed by human conflict.

Unovan myth recounts Cobalion teaching early clans defensive warfare without aggression, forging blades from mountain ore. Its horn and cape-like mane evoke a duelist knight of forest realms.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Cobalion’s aura calms wild Pokémon during skirmishes, allowing evacuation of civilians in historical texts. Pokédex entries describe its kicks shattering steel plate, yet it refuses to strike first unless justice demands intervention.

It appears after large-scale habitat destruction, guiding restoration efforts alongside Virizion’s plant growth and Terrakion’s earthworks.

## Battle Performance

Cobalion is a utility pivot with Justified or Steadfast, Taunt, Stealth Rock, and Close Combat. Its typing grants resistances to Fairy, Rock, and common physical moves, though Fire/Fighting pressure hurts.

Swords Dance sets can sweep in lower tiers, but its middling Attack keeps it primarily a defensive role in VGC and singles balanced formats.
