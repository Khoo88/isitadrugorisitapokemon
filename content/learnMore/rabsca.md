## Who is Rabsca?

Rabsca is a Bug/Psychic-type Pokémon from Generation IX, evolving from Rellor. Its dung beetle ball of psychic energy and ancient mask motif tie Paldea to mysterious ruins where spheres roll uphill toward forgotten temples.

Scholars debate whether Rabsca preserves memories inside its orb or creates them—a philosophical Pokémon that became an unexpected doubles support staple in competitive circles.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Rabsca’s ball contains psychically imprinted experiences rolled for generations. Pokédex entries describe it protecting the sphere more than its own life, attacking anyone who touches it.

Desert tracks show perfect circles etched in sand overnight, attributed to Rabsca meditation rituals that sync with planetary alignment in local myth.

## Battle Performance

Rabsca is famous for Revival Blessing, reviving fainted allies once per battle—a game-changing support in VGC. Trick Room, Psychic, and Bug Buzz provide offense.

Slow Speed suits Trick Room teams. Its bulk is moderate; Dark and Ghost types threaten it, but Revival Blessing’s value often outweighs death.
