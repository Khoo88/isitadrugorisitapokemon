## Who is Minior?

Minior is a Rock/Flying-type Pokémon from Generation VII, born as a rocky core wrapped in a fragile meteor shell. It falls from the ozone layer in swarms above Alola, cracking open to reveal a glowing crystalline body when its crust weakens.

Alolan stargazers treat Minior showers as seasonal festivals, though scientists study them as living micrometeorites. Its universe standing blends cute collectible forms with surprising special offensive power once naked.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Minior’s shell absorbs cosmic radiation, hardening until it can no longer contain internal pressure. Pokédex entries describe cores glowing in seven colors based on diet and altitude of origin.

Flocks spiral in the upper atmosphere, feeding on dust; when predators strike, individuals sacrifice shells to dazzle attackers with light bursts—a kamikaze glitter defense.

## Battle Performance

Minior uses Shields Down to become faster and stronger below half HP when shell breaks. Acrobatics, Power Gem, and Shell Smash (via Baton Pass teams historically) define its niche.

At full HP it is bulky; broken form hits hard with doubled Speed. Stealth Rock and Sandstorm chip the shell intentionally in competitive play to trigger transformation early.
