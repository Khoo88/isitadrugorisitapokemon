## Who is Zamazenta?

Zamazenta is a Fighting-type Legendary Pokémon from Generation VIII, Galar’s shield hero paired with Zacian. Crowned form adds Steel typing and Dauntless Shield, raising Defense to tank hits that would obliterate other legendaries.

Legends describe it deflecting Eternatus’s beam with a golden shield, saving the region—a defensive icon as celebrated as Zacian’s offense.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Zamazenta’s shield is living alloy grown from its mane, regenerating chips after battles. Pokédex entries describe it sprinting into disasters to protect civilians, collapsing from exhaustion afterward.

Uncrowned form trains by headbutting mountains, flattening peaks over decades into mesas visible on Galar maps.

## Battle Performance

Zamazenta-Crowned is a physical wallbreaker with Dauntless Shield, Body Press scaling with Defense, and Behemoth Bash. +1 Defense on switch-in makes Body Press lethal.

Choice Band sets exist; it lacks Zacian’s raw speed but breaks stall. Fighting/Steel coverage hits Fairy and Ice teams; Ground and Fire types remain threats.
