## Who is Pheromosa?

Pheromosa is a Bug/Fighting-type Ultra Beast from Generation VII, codename UB-02 Beauty. Its cockroach-ballerina silhouette and impossible Speed made it synonymous with offensive pressure—and Smogon tier bans.

Aether Foundation reports describe Pheromosa blitzing researchers before they could blink, a literal beauty that destroys benchmarks for what “fast” means in the Pokémon universe.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Pheromosa’s legs flick faster than high-speed cameras capture, leaving afterimages. Pokédex entries claim it refuses to touch anything dirty, yet kills prey with sterile precision—a contradictory fastidious predator.

Pheromone trails cause disorientation in mammals; Alolan authorities issued evacuation orders during early Wormhole events.

## Battle Performance

Pheromosa is banned in most formats for good reason: 137 Speed, 137 Attack, U-turn, Ice Beam, and High Jump Kick under Beast Boost snowballing.

Even without boosts, Choice Band or Life Orb sets OHKO large portions of the tier. Only priority, Focus Sash, and sturdy counters survive a well-played Pheromosa turn one.
