## Who is Passimian?

Passimian is a Fighting-type Pokémon from Generation VII, a lemur-like team captain that tosses Berries and allies in coordinated packs. Alola’s Lush Jungle troops mirror real-world primate tactics, with red and blue band variants feuding in game version lore.

It embodies cooperative Fighting-type identity—less solo brawler than rugby captain. Its standing is cultural in Alola and mechanically unique via Defiant and Recycle item synergy.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Passimian leaders wear berry bandoliers, passing fruit to injured packmates mid-battle. Pokédex entries describe formations where every member throws simultaneously, overwhelming predators.

Rival troops steal each other’s berries before full-moon tournaments, a ritual that keeps jungle populations balanced.

## Battle Performance

Passimian runs Defiant with Close Combat, Knock Off, and U-turn, or Choice Scarf revenge killing. Recycle + Iron Ball or Berry strategies appear in doubles gimmicks.

Outclassed in OU by stronger fighters, it thrives in VGC doubles with intimidate counters and Surround-style ally support when paired with Oranguru historically.
