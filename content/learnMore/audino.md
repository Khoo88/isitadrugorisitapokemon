## Who is Audino?

Audino is a Normal-type Pokémon from Generation V, widely known as a gentle healer found on Unovan routes. Its hearing-focused design—large ears and stethoscope-like feelers—made it an iconic roadside companion before trainers even reached the first Pokémon Center in some playthroughs.

Across the Pokémon world, Audino symbolizes medical care and empathy. Nurse Joy partnerships in Unova highlight its role scanning heartbeats and soothing stressed Pokémon, reinforcing its reputation as a living diagnostic tool.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Audino’s feelers detect minute vibrations in air and tissue, mapping emotional states through pulse rhythms. Pokédex entries describe it approaching injured creatures silently, humming frequencies that accelerate cellular repair.

In meadows, Audino herds form listening circles that alert colonies to predators long before visual contact. Mega Audino lore (where referenced) amplifies its defensive fluff into a fortress of healing aura.

## Battle Performance

Audino is a special wall with enormous HP, Regenerator, and access to Wish, Protect, and Heal Bell. Moves like Dazzling Gleam or Flamethrower provide rare offensive pressure, while Toxic and Thunder Wave stall foes.

Mega Audino was a dominant doubles support in past generations; today standard Audino remains niche outside dedicated healing formats or B2B (back-to-back) healing cores.
