## What is Tobramycin?

Tobramycin is an aminoglycoside antibiotic with a mechanism identical to gentamicin—inhibition of bacterial protein synthesis at the 30S ribosomal subunit, producing concentration-dependent bactericidal activity against aerobic gram-negative organisms, especially Pseudomonas aeruginosa.

Inhaled formulations deliver high local concentrations in cystic fibrosis bronchi with limited systemic toxicity when used as directed. IV tobramycin requires therapeutic drug monitoring for nephrotoxicity and ototoxicity.

Poor oral absorption limits systemic use to parenteral and topical/inhaled routes.

## Primary Uses

- Cystic fibrosis pulmonary exacerbations (inhaled and IV)
- Serious gram-negative infections (IV, combination regimens)
- Pseudomonal sepsis and pneumonia in immunocompromised patients
- Topical ophthalmic and otic infections (drops)

Often combined with beta-lactams for synergy in endocarditis and neutropenic fever protocols.

## Common Side Effects

**IV:**
- Nephrotoxicity, ototoxicity
- Neuromuscular blockade

**Inhaled:**
- Cough, bronchospasm, voice alteration
- **Warnings:** TDM for IV use; avoid concurrent nephrotoxins; vestibular and cochlear monitoring with prolonged therapy; inhaled: pre-treat bronchospasm in reactive airway disease if needed.
