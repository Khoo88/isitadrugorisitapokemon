## Who is Feebas?

Feebas is a Water-type Pokémon from Generation III, infamous as the “ugly duckling” of Hoenn rivers before evolving into majestic Milotic. Its shabby appearance hides a genetic rarity that made it a trophy hunt across Route 119’s shifting tile pools.

Hoenn culture uses Feebas as a parable about patience and hidden worth—trainers who walk thousands of steps for one encounter earn respect in fishing guilds. Its standing is less combat fame than legendary transformation narrative.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Feebas thrives only in nutrient-rich, overcrowded waters where competition is fierce—ironically linking beauty evolution to harsh environments. Pokédex entries describe it as the plainest Pokémon, eating scraps and surviving on minimal food.

Princess stories in Sinnoh and Hoenn claim a single Feebas bond can trigger evolution when trust peaks, though modern mechanics also tie evolution to Beauty or Prism Scale trade items.

## Battle Performance

Feebas is nearly nonexistent competitively except in purposefully weak “F.E.A.R.” or novelty LC sets. Its stats are among the worst in the game.

All competitive value lives in Milotic, which inherits no battle relevance from Feebas beyond the journey. Fishermen joke that the real battle is finding one at all.
