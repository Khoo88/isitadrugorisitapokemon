## Who is Inkay?

Inkay is a Dark/Psychic-type Pokémon from Generation VI, evolving into Malamar when leveled up while the console is held upside down—a mechanic that cemented its pop-culture fame. It resembles a squid hat that flashes lights to hypnotize prey.

Kalos children’s games include “Inkay bow” gestures before evolution attempts. Its dual typing inverted expectations by becoming a dark-dominated physical attacker after evolution.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Inkay’s spots flash in patterns that overload prey brains. Pokédex entries warn against staring during hikes—it drains willpower until victims offer food voluntarily.

It skims ship hulls at night, mistaken for kraken juveniles, leaving bioluminescent ink that glows for hours.

## Battle Performance

Inkay can run Trick Room or Foul Play in Little Cup with Contrary unavailable until Malamar. Topsy-Turvy and Superpower define the evolved form.

As Inkay, it uses Hypnosis and Peck; competitive relevance explodes with Malamar’s Contrary boosts reversing stat drops into sweeps.
