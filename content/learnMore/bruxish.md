## Who is Bruxish?

Bruxish is a Water/Psychic-type Pokémon from Generation VII, native to Alola’s vibrant reefs. Its lipstick-bright coloration and gnashing teeth parody triggerfish, while its psychic powers add an unexpected combat dimension.

Alolan divers treat Bruxish as a reef guardian—beautiful but territorial. Tourist guides warn against flashy jewelry near colonies, as light refraction can provoke charging displays.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Bruxish emits psychic pulses from its forehead gem, stunning prey before biting. Pokédex entries claim its skin patterns hypnotize smaller fish, funneling them into feeding zones like living art installations.

During mating season, males flash competing color bands, clashing teeth in non-lethal duels that nonetheless scar coral heads—a spectacle snorkelers record from safe distances.

## Battle Performance

Bruxish’s Dazzling ability prevents priority moves, countering Extreme Speed and Aqua Jet users. Strong Jaw boosts Psychic Fangs and Fishious Rend, making it a hard-hitting physical attacker.

It lacks bulk and speed compared to top tiers, but in Battle Spot or lower tiers it can surprise with Trick Room or Choice Band sets. Coverage moves like Crunch and Ice Fang patch typings.
