## Who is Cacnea?

Cacnea is a Grass-type Pokémon from Generation III, evolving into Cacturne. It thrives in Hoenn’s desert routes, storing water in a spherical body ringed with sharp spines—an emblem of harsh-climate survival.

Desert traders respect Cacnea groves as natural fences: intruders learn quickly that every hug is hostile. Songs from Mirage Tower ruins mention Cacnea spirits guiding lost travelers to oases—if appeased with offerings.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Cacnea blooms once yearly, releasing sweet nectar that attracts prey before spines contract. Pokédex entries note it stands motionless for days, lowering body temperature to avoid detection by predators like Flygon.

Night-blooming flowers glow faintly, drawing misguided moths into spiked embraces. Its rhythmic swaying in sandstorms inspired clockwork toys in Slateport markets.

## Battle Performance

Cacnea learns Spiky Shield, Sucker Punch, and Energy Ball before evolution. In Little Cup it can run offensive or sand-support roles with Sand Veil evasion in sandstorm teams.

Most competitive value arrives with Cacturne’s Dark typing; as Cacnea, it remains a stage-specific pick with exploitable Fire/Ice weaknesses.
