## Who is Hydreigon?

Hydreigon is a Dark/Dragon-type pseudo-legendary from Generation V, evolving from Deino and Zweilous. Three heads argue while it annihilates foes—a Unovan dark dragon that became a special attacking icon in competitive history.

Villain teams in Unova fiction favored Hydreigon imagery for its destructive symbolism, yet trainers praise loyalty when all three heads agree on a trainer’s command.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Each head thinks independently, often snapping at each other over food. Pokédex entries describe it destroying entire villages when hungry, though trained specimens coordinate devastatingly.

Its six wings allow hovering despite bulk, letting it stalk cave systems where Zweilous once bumped walls blindly.

## Battle Performance

Hydreigon uses Levitate, Draco Meteor, Dark Pulse, Fire Blast, and U-turn. Choice Specs and Life Orb sets wallbreak; Roost variants stall in lower tiers.

Fairy-type dominance hurt it, but Steel types fear its coverage. It remains a premier special attacker in OU historically and a strong restricted pick in some VGC formats.
