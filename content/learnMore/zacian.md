## Who is Zacian?

Zacian is a Fairy-type Legendary Pokémon from Generation VIII, hero of Galar’s war legend alongside Zamazenta. Crowned form wields a fairy sword, becoming Fairy/Steel with Intrepid Sword—king of heroes who ended the Darkest Day.

Galarian statues depict Zacian leaping from the mist to strike Eternatus; its mythic standing equals national identity and competitive dominance post-Gen VIII release.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Zacian’s sword is not metal but crystallized fairy energy sharpened by centuries of honor oaths. Pokédex entries describe it running faster than sound, leaving blue trails across battlefields.

Uncrowned form roams wild with sword in mouth, testing worthy trainers before accepting Crowned evolution in ceremony.

## Battle Performance

Zacian-Crowned is among the strongest wallbreakers ever with Intrepid Sword, Behemoth Blade, Close Combat, and Play Rough. Choice Band one-shots much of the tier.

Even uncrowned Zacian pressures with Swords Dance. It is banned in many formats; checks require sturdy Steel-types or Intimidate cycling before it boosts.
