## Who is Stakataka?

Stakataka is a Rock/Steel-type Ultra Beast from Generation VII, codename UB-04 Assembly. It resembles a walking stone wall fortress, appearing on Poni Beach as a living siege engine that crushes anything in its path.

Ultra Beast reports describe Stakataka as architectural mimicry—walls that learned to walk. Its lore is minimal but effective: silent, heavy, unstoppable in Trick Room.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Stakataka’s blocks shift like tectonic plates, sealing gaps when attacked. Pokédex entries claim it destroys landmarks by leaning on them, absorbing minerals to grow taller over centuries.

At night it freezes motionless, mistaken for ruins until dawn migrations flatten sand dunes behind it.

## Battle Performance

Stakataka is a Trick Room sweeper with Beast Boost, Gyro Ball, and Stone Edge. Immense Defense and Attack under Trick Room let it OHKO after boosts.

Outside Trick Room it is useless due to Speed. Gyro Ball power scales with speed difference; stacking Beast Boost on Attack creates late-game sweepers in Ubers.
