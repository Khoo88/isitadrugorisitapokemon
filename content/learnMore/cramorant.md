## Who is Cramorant?

Cramorant is a Flying/Water-type Pokémon from Generation VIII, infamous for Gulping Form and Gorging Form—states where it swallows Arrokuda or Pikachu whole and fires them at foes. Its comedic design masks skilled diving predation.

Galarian coastguards initially dismissed Cramorant as clumsy until documenting its precise plunge fishing. It became a symbol of unpredictable battlefield chaos in competitive memes.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Cramorant’s throat pouch expands dramatically, storing catch for later regurgitation as projectiles. Pokédex entries joke it overestimates swallow capacity, causing occasional choking fits that trainers must remedy.

Despite humor, its dive speed is exceptional, spearing fish from double its height above water with minimal splash.

## Battle Performance

Cramorant’s Gulp Missile ability deals chip damage when hit after surfacing, punishing attacks with Arrokuda or Pikachu payloads. Offensive sets use Surf, Brave Bird, and Roost with high Special Attack investment.

Fragile defenses and predictable forms limit tier placement, but in doubles it can pressure with disruptive chip and strong Water/Flying coverage when built around Gulp triggers.
