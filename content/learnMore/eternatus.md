## Who is Eternatus?

Eternatus is a Poison/Dragon-type Legendary Pokémon from Generation VIII, the extraterrestrial engine behind the Galar region’s Dynamax phenomenon. Its serpentine body channels infinite energy, crashing into the region centuries ago inside a meteor that reshaped power lines and legend alike.

Galarian history treats Eternatus as both catastrophe and power source—Leon’s championship and the Max Lair mythos orbit its corpse-like slumber in the Power Plant. It represents cosmic scale threat unlike terrestrial legendaries.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Eternatus leaks “Galar particles” that cause Pokémon and structures to swell grotesquely when uncontrolled. Pokédex entries describe its core beating like a reactor, absorbing ambient energy until the atmosphere shimmers.

When awakened, its Eternamax form towers over cities, wings of toxic vapor eclipsing the sky—a living reminder that Dynamax is borrowed power, not native biology.

## Battle Performance

Eternatus is a fast special attacker with Pressure or Neutralizing Gas, running Dynamax Cannon, Flamethrower, Sludge Bomb, and Recover. Its 140 base Speed and strong STAB make it a premier restricted pick in VGC.

Poison/Dragon typing grants key resistances, though Ice and Psychic moves hurt. It often leads to set Tailwind or deny opponent terrain with its sheer offensive pressure.
