## Who is Aurorus?

Aurorus is a Rock/Ice-type Pokémon from Generation VI, evolving from Amaura restored from the Sail Fossil. It resembles a crystalline diplodocid sauropod, evoking arctic nights when Kalos was colder and glaciers dominated northern vistas.

Kalosian paleontology treats Aurorus as a gentle giant of frozen plains—herbivorous, herd-oriented, and capable of calling auroras that mesmerize predators. Children’s tales describe its neck lights guiding travelers through blizzards.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Pokédex entries state Aurorus can lower surrounding temperature with crystalline fins, creating ice pathways across tundra. Its collar emits bands of color during geomagnetic storms, possibly a communication channel among herd members.

Fossil beds show communal nesting rings, suggesting parental care lasting years. Revived Aurorus in modern habitats require climate-controlled enclosures to prevent overheating stress.

## Battle Performance

Aurorus offers Refrigerate-boosted Hyper Voice or Blizzard in hail/hyper offense cores, and can set up Light Screen or Reflect with Weak Armor triggers. Its poor defensive typing (4x weak to Fighting and Steel, 2x to Rock) limits viability.

Snow Warning hail teams occasionally use it as a slow special attacker. Freeze-Dry coverage helps against Water-types that otherwise wall its Ice STAB.
