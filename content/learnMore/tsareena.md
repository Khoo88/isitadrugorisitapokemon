## Who is Tsareena?

Tsareena is a Grass-type Pokémon from Generation VII, evolving from Steenee when it knows Stomp. Its crown-kick fighting style and Queenly Majesty ability made it Alola’s martial grass dancer, punishing priority moves with regal disdain.

Trial captains showcase Tsareena as mastery of type disadvantage through technique—kicks that shatter ice and intimidate dark types in narrative battles.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Tsareena’s crown is hardened leaf cartilage sharper than glass. Pokédex entries describe it challenging disobedient Steenee with high kicks until evolution respect is earned—a harsh mentorship culture.

It secretes aromatic oils from feet that mark territory; rivals who cross lines face synchronized kick barrages from the colony.

## Battle Performance

Tsareena blocks priority with Queenly Majesty and hits hard with Power Whip, High Jump Kick, and Rapid Spin. Assault Vest or Choice Scarf sets compete in UU.

Rapid Spin removal is valuable; weaknesses to Fire, Ice, Poison, Flying, and Bug hurt. Triple Axel and U-turn expand coverage in modern sets.
