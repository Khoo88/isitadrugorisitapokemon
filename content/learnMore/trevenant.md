## Who is Trevenant?

Trevenant is a Ghost/Grass-type Pokémon from Generation VI, evolving from Phantump. It possesses ancient trees, binding souls of lost children and forest guardians in Kalos horror stories that are sad rather than malicious.

Route 16 travelers leave offerings at Trevenant roots for safe passage; those who cut its host trees face years of misfortune in superstition backed by actual psychic retaliation in Pokédex canon.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Trevenant controls other trees with root networks spanning acres. Pokédex entries describe it trapping trespassers in living forests until they apologize, then releasing them disoriented but unharmed.

Its body is 90% possessed wood; killing the spirit leaves a normal tree that blooms unusually vibrant each spring.

## Battle Performance

Trevenant has Harvest Sitrus Berry loops with Trick Room, Wood Hammer, and Horn Leech. Natural Cure or Frisk variants support doubles.

Weak to Fire and common offensive types limit tiers. Trick Room physical attacker roles suit its low Speed and high Attack in PU and occasional VGC gimmicks.
