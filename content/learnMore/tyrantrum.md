## Who is Tyrantrum?

Tyrantrum is a Rock/Dragon-type Pokémon from Generation VI, evolving from Tyrunt. Its kingly crest and powerful jaws recreate tyrannosaur majesty in Kalos fossil labs—a prehistoric ruler revived with Strong Jaw ferocity.

Children’s books cast Tyrantrum as cave king, but Pokédex entries describe it crushing prey with 5,000 PSI bites, reminding audiences it is predator first, mascot second.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Tyrantrum’s orange hide reflects royal status in mating displays involving headbutt duels that echo through canyons. Pokédex entries claim only one per territory, enforcing solitary apex rule.

Fossil records show battle scars on every specimen, suggesting constant warfare in ancient Kalos ecosystems.

## Battle Performance

Tyrantrum uses Strong Jaw on Head Smash, Dragon Claw, and Fire Fang with Choice Scarf or Rock Head. Rock/Dragon typing is offensive but defensively shaky.

It wallbreaks in RU; Stealth Rock and Fairy-types check it. Dragon Dance variants surprise when supported by screens.
