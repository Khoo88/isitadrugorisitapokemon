## What is Remdesivir?

Remdesivir is an adenosine nucleotide prodrug that is metabolized intracellularly to a nucleoside triphosphate analog, which inhibits RNA-dependent RNA polymerase of SARS-CoV-2 and other RNA viruses, causing delayed chain termination during viral replication.

Intravenous administration is required; outpatient oral antivirals have largely replaced early outpatient COVID-19 use in many settings. Benefit is greatest when started early in hospitalized patients requiring supplemental oxygen but not yet on high-flow or mechanical ventilation per major trials.

Hepatic transaminase elevations and infusion reactions occur; renal impairment may affect metabolite clearance.

## Primary Uses

- COVID-19 in hospitalized adults and children requiring supplemental oxygen (per FDA labeling and institutional protocols)
- Outpatient IV infusion in high-risk patients when oral agents contraindicated (selected settings)
- Other RNA viruses (Ebola—investigational and compassionate use contexts)

Not recommended for mild COVID-19 without risk factors in many current guidelines when oral agents are available.

## Common Side Effects

- Nausea, elevated liver enzymes
- Infusion-related reactions (hypotension, fever, chills)
- Headache
- **Warnings:** Monitor hepatic function; limited data in severe renal impairment; not a substitute for vaccination and standard supportive care; product availability and variant activity per public health updates.
