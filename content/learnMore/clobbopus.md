## Who is Clobbopus?

Clobbopus is a Fighting-type Pokémon from Generation VIII, evolving into Grapploct. Its rubbery limbs and curious octopus design made it a fan favorite on Galar beaches, where it practices punches against rock pools before mastering grappling techniques.

Fisherfolk describe Clobbopus as mischievous but trainable—stealing bait, then returning it when scolded. Its evolution into a pure grappler reflects a disciplined martial journey.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Clobbopus tests grip strength on everything, leaving sucker marks on dock pilings. Pokédex entries say it smashes berries with rapid jabs, developing muscle memory long before tentacles specialize for holds.

Tide pools with smooth stones often indicate Clobbopus dojos—juveniles line up to spar under watch of a dominant Grapploct elder.

## Battle Performance

Clobbopus learns Octolock only after evolution. As Clobbopus, it runs Fighting-type physical moves like Brick Break and Bind in LC tiers with low bulk.

Competitive relevance arrives with Grapploct’s trapping and Octolock stall-breaking; pre-evolution stages are rarely featured in serious formats.
