## Who is Duraludon?

Duraludon is a Dragon/Steel-type Pokémon from Generation VIII, evolving into Archaludon in Generation IX. Its skyscraper silhouette and alloy hide made it a mascot of Galar industrial routes, grazing on mineral-rich scrap and ore deposits.

Engineers in Hammerlocke study Duraludon exoskeletons for lightweight construction materials, though wild specimens fiercely guard scrap yards.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Duraludon’s body heat melts snow on contact, creating microclimates around nests built from stacked metal. Pokédex entries note territorial laser-like beams fired from dorsal fins, carving warning lines into steel girders.

Rivalry with Tyranitar line species shapes mountain economies—when both appear, construction projects pause until migrations resolve.

## Battle Performance

Duraludon runs Choice Specs or Assault Vest sets with Draco Meteor, Flash Cannon, and Body Press. Stalwart ignores Intimidate; Light Metal reduces weight for Low Kick damage.

Low Special Defense without investment and common Ground/Fighting weaknesses hurt. It can wall Choice Specs Ice attackers and pivot with Thunderbolt coverage in formats before Archaludon power creep.
