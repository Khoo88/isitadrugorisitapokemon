## Who is Naganadel?

Naganadel is a Poison/Dragon-type Ultra Beast from Generation VII, evolved from Poipole. Its syringe-like stinger and draconic wings made it the “Stinger Pokémon” that completes the Ultra Recon Squad’s invasive species narrative.

Falling through wormholes with alien elegance, Naganadel represents poison as cosmic weapon—beautiful, toxic, and unnaturally fast. It stands among the most lethal special sweepers in Ultra Beast lore.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Naganadel injects neurotoxin that alters prey’s brain chemistry before digestion. Pokédex entries claim its saliva is adhesive glue used to build hive spires in other dimensions, hinting at colonial behavior unseen in Alola.

Its cry resonates at frequencies that induce nausea in humans—a defense against capture attempts documented by Aether Foundation field teams.

## Battle Performance

Naganadel is a premier special attacker with Beast Boost, Draco Meteor, Sludge Wave, and Fire Blast. High Speed and Special Attack let it clean teams after one KO.

Frail defenses mean priority and priority-like speed tiers revenge kill it, but in Ubers and VGC restricted formats it remains a top-tier wallbreaker when protected by redirection.
