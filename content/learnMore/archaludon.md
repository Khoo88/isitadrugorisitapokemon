## Who is Archaludon?

Archaludon is a Dragon/Steel-type Pokémon introduced in Generation IX, evolving from Duraludon when exposed to a special magnetic field. It extends the “skyscraper dragon” concept into a heavier, bridge-like organism armored with alloyed scales.

In Paldea’s lore, Archaludon is associated with industrial corridors and mountain quarries where electromagnetic ore veins pulse beneath the soil. Trainers describe it as territorial yet disciplined—more engineer than rampaging beast.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Archaludon’s body channels geomagnetic energy through dorsal fins, hardening its hide into near-industrial tensile strength. Pokédex entries suggest it digs labyrinthine tunnels that double as lightning rods during storms, protecting nearby settlements.

Young Archaludon reportedly align their growth along mineral veins, causing natural “metal ridges” visible from aerial surveys. Its rivalry with Tyranitar line species is a recurring ecological note in Paldean field guides.

## Battle Performance

Archaludon boasts exceptional Defense and Special Attack with abilities like Stamina or Stalwart. It commonly runs Flash Cannon, Dragon Pulse, Thunderbolt, and Body Press, punishing Fairy and Ice types that threaten its pre-evolution.

Its Steel typing grants numerous resistances, though Ground and Fighting attacks remain dangerous. Competitive sets may use Assault Vest or Weakness Policy to lean into tanking or sweeping roles.
