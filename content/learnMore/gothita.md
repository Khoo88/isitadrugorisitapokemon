## Who is Gothita?

Gothita is a Psychic-type Pokémon from Generation V, the first stage of the Gothitelle line. Its ribbon bows and staring eyes made it a cute yet unsettling companion in Unova urban routes, where it people-watches from alley perches.

Psychic researchers use Gothita to study passive telepathy development, noting it calms crying infants with low-frequency pulses—a gentle introduction to a line that ends in battlefield control titans.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Gothita’s red bows amplify psychic focus, twitching when it reads emotions. Pokédex entries say it sleeps clutching loved ones’ belongings, imprinting auras for comfort.

Flocks perch on clock towers, swaying in unison at noon—a phenomenon tourists film, unaware the sway encodes weather predictions.

## Battle Performance

Gothita can run Calm Mind or Trick Room setups in Little Cup with Eviolite and Fake Tears support. Competitive focus shifts to Gothorita and Gothitelle.

As a baby stage, it lacks bulk for higher tiers; Frisk and Shadow Tag arrive later. Gothita’s role is developmental, with niche LC trick room doubling potential.
