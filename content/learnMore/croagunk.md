## Who is Croagunk?

Croagunk is a Poison/Fighting-type Pokémon from Generation IV, evolving into Toxicroak. Its toxic cheek sacs and playful stance made it Team Rocket’s unofficial mascot in Sinnoh anime—yet in the wild it is a cautious marshland ambusher.

Sinnoh marshes feature Croagunk warning calls—distinct croaks that signal poison clouds ahead. Trainers respect its dual typing and surprising agility.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Croagunk inflates poison sacs to appear larger, then strikes with poisoned fingers. Pokédex entries note it never attacks first unless provoked, aligning with its “jester” persona masking defensive strategy.

Mating dances involve rhythmic hopping that disperses diluted toxins, coloring soil purple for seasons—a natural indicator of wetland health.

## Battle Performance

Croagunk learns Anticipation, Dry Skin, or Poison Touch before evolution. In LC it can run Vacuum Wave priority or Swords Dance physical sets with Drain Punch.

Toxicroak gains Dry Skin healing in rain and Sucker Punch priority; Croagunk itself is a stepping-stone with solid Attack but limited bulk at this stage.
