## What is Eszopiclone?

Eszopiclone is a nonbenzodiazepine hypnotic (Z-drug) that acts as a positive allosteric modulator at GABAA receptors containing alpha subunits, particularly those with alpha-2 and alpha-3 subunits, promoting sleep onset and maintenance. It is the (S)-enantiomer of zopiclone, with longer duration than zaleplon and similar mechanism to zolpidem.

Rapid absorption produces sleep within 30 minutes; bitter metallic taste is a distinctive adverse effect. Schedule IV controlled substance with potential for dependence, tolerance, and complex sleep behaviors (sleepwalking, sleep-driving).

Recommended for short-term insomnia treatment; cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia is preferred first-line per sleep medicine guidelines.

## Primary Uses

- Treatment of insomnia characterized by difficulty falling asleep, staying asleep, or early morning awakening
- Short-term use when nonpharmacologic measures are insufficient

Not indicated for chronic nightly use beyond recommended duration without reassessment of underlying causes.

## Common Side Effects

- Unpleasant taste (dysgeusia—metallic or bitter)
- Headache
- Somnolence, dizziness
- Dry mouth
- Complex sleep-related behaviors (parasomnias)
- Next-day psychomotor impairment (especially at higher doses or in elderly)
- **Warnings:** Contraindicated with history of complex sleep behaviors after sedative-hypnotics; caution with opioids and alcohol; reduce dose in elderly and hepatic impairment; avoid abrupt discontinuation after chronic use; pregnancy and lactation considerations.
