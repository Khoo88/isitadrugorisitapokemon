## Who is Gothorita?

Gothorita is a Psychic-type Pokémon from Generation V, the middle stage between Gothita and Gothitelle. It intensifies telekinetic focus, lifting objects twice its weight while preparing to map celestial bodies like its final evolution.

Unova midnight festivals feature Gothorita levitating lanterns, a tradition borrowed from ancient observatories that treated them as living astrolabes.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Gothorita’s bows now orbit its head independently, tracking magnetic north. Pokédex entries describe it punishing those who harm its friends with psychic headaches lasting days—protective, not vengeful by nature.

It arranges pebbles into constellation maps on rooftops, confusing astronomers who discover “new stars” that were rock art.

## Battle Performance

Gothorita carries Frisk and Shadow Tag before Gothitelle’s full trapping kit. In NFE tiers it can run Eviolite Calm Mind with Psychic and Thunderbolt.

Transition stage with improving bulk; competitive relevance peaks after evolution. Trick Room support with Fake Tears appears in dedicated LC and NFE formats.
