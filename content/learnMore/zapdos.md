## Who is Zapdos?

Zapdos is an Electric/Flying-type Legendary Pokémon from Generation I, one of the Kanto birds roaming Power Plant storms. Its crackling wings and territorial lightning made it an early-game myth before Galarian form redefined it as Fighting/Flying martial artist.

Kanto legends tie Zapdos to summer thunderstorms; its capture in classic stories marks a trainer’s ascent to elite status. It remains culturally iconic across all generations.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Zapdos absorbs lightning from clouds, nesting in power plants where it feeds on electrical fields. Pokédex entries describe thunderclaps when it flaps wings, causing localized blackouts.

Galarian Zapdos abandons electricity for kickboxing prowess, running across seas without flying—a regional evolution of lore more than biology.

## Battle Performance

Kanto Zapdos is a defensive pivot with Pressure, Roost, Heat Wave, and Discharge or Thunderbolt. Galarian Zapdos runs Defiant Close Combat and Brave Bird in OU.

Both forms compete differently: Kanto stalls, Galar sweeps. Ground immunity via Flying typing helps Kanto; Galar weak to Fairy and Psychic but pressures Landorus.
