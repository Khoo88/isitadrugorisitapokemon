## What is Risperidone?

Risperidone is an atypical antipsychotic with potent serotonin 5-HT2A and dopamine D2 receptor antagonism, plus alpha-1 and histamine H1 blockade contributing to orthostatic hypotension and sedation. Active metabolite 9-hydroxyrisperidone (paliperidone) has long half-life.

Oral, orally disintegrating, and long-acting injectable formulations support schizophrenia and bipolar maintenance. D2 occupancy at higher doses increases extrapyramidal side effect risk compared with olanzapine or clozapine.

Prolactin elevation is common due to tuberoinfundibular dopamine blockade.

## Primary Uses

- Schizophrenia (acute and maintenance)
- Bipolar I disorder (mania and mixed episodes, maintenance)
- Irritability associated with autistic disorder (pediatric)
- Aggressive behavior in dementia (not recommended—boxed mortality warning)

## Common Side Effects

- Extrapyramidal symptoms (dose-related)
- Hyperprolactinemia (galactorrhea, amenorrhea, sexual dysfunction)
- Weight gain, metabolic changes
- Sedation, orthostatic hypotension
- **Warnings:** Increased mortality in elderly with dementia-related psychosis; neuroleptic malignant syndrome; tardive dyskinesia; monitor metabolic and movement disorder symptoms; QT prolongation at high doses in vulnerable patients.
