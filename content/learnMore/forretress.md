## Who is Forretress?

Forretress is a Bug/Steel-type Pokémon from Generation II, evolving from Pineco. It resembles a living pine cone fortress, rolling through Johto forests and sealing itself against predators with iron-hard shells.

Engineers study Forretress as natural ballistics—its ability to launch spikes inspired early Johto trap designs. It remains a workhorse of defensive teams, unglamorous but indispensable in competitive memory.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Forretress detaches shell plates as explosive spikes, regrowing them over days. Pokédex entries describe it clamping onto trees, sucking sap while camouflaged as bark until prey blunders near.

In winter, colonies roll into snowballs, hibernating as apparent boulders until spring thaws trigger simultaneous hatching of Pineco offspring.

## Battle Performance

Forretress sets Spikes, Stealth Rock, Rapid Spin, and Explosion with Sturdy guaranteeing one layer. Gyro Ball punishes fast foes; Volt Switch pivots in magnet rise teams.

Weak to Fire but resisting Fairy and many physical types, it defined hazard wars for decades. Custap Berry Explosion leads still appear in niche doubles strategies.
