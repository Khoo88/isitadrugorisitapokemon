## What is Trulicity?

Trulicity (dulaglutide) is a long-acting glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptor agonist consisting of two GLP-1 analog chains fused to an immunoglobulin Fc fragment, allowing weekly subcutaneous dosing with slowed renal clearance. It stimulates glucose-dependent insulin secretion, suppresses glucagon, delays gastric emptying, and reduces appetite.

Cardiovascular outcome trials (REWIND) demonstrated reduction in major adverse cardiovascular events in type 2 diabetes. Weight loss is moderate compared with higher-dose semaglutide but clinically meaningful.

Contraindicated in personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma and MEN2. GI side effects are common during dose escalation.

## Primary Uses

- Type 2 diabetes mellitus to improve glycemic control (with diet and exercise)
- Reduction of major adverse cardiovascular events in type 2 diabetes with established cardiovascular disease or multiple risk factors

Not approved for weight management alone at current labeling (unlike higher-dose GLP-1 agents for obesity).

## Common Side Effects

- Nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, abdominal pain
- Decreased appetite
- Injection-site reactions
- Pancreatitis (rare)
- Gallbladder disease
- **Warnings:** Thyroid C-cell tumor boxed warning (rodent data); hold before surgery; delay gastric emptying affects oral drugs; do not use in type 1 diabetes or diabetic ketoacidosis; pregnancy insufficient data.
