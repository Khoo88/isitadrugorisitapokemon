## Who is Vikavolt?

Vikavolt is a Bug/Electric-type Pokémon from Generation VII, evolving from Charjabug only in magnetic areas. Its stag-beetle cannon design and levitation made it Alola’s premier electric sniper on Poni Island trials.

Engineers study Vikavolt discharge for railgun prototypes, while photographers chase its acrobatic aerial duels with Toucannon in rainforest canopies.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Vikavolt fires concentrated plasma from mandibles, propelling itself backward in recoil flight. Pokédex entries describe it hovering motionless for hours, waiting to snipe prey with one shot.

Mating displays involve drawing geometric lightning patterns in storm clouds, visible for kilometers.

## Battle Performance

Vikavolt is slow but hits with Thunderbolt, Bug Buzz, and Energy Ball under Levitate. Trick Room and Choice Specs sets leverage 145 Special Attack.

It lacks Speed outside Trick Room but walls Ground attacks. Sticky Web teams pair historically with Alolan evolution requirements.
