## Who is Garganacl?

Garganacl is a Rock-type Pokémon from Generation IX, evolving from Nacli and Naclstack. Its salt golem physique guards Paldea quarries, crystallizing minerals into fortress-like bodies that shelter smaller Pokémon during sandstorms.

Miners treat Garganacl as living breakwaters—slow, stoic, and capable of repairing walls by excreting hardened salt mortar. It represents Paldea’s earth-tone defensive identity in lore and VGC.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Garganacl’s paws leave salt trails that cure minor wounds in desert travelers. Pokédex entries describe it piling boulders to create oasis dams, reshaping arid geography over centuries.

When angered, it vibrates crystals to produce deafening cracks, shattering glass in nearby towns—a natural earthquake drill for preparedness programs.

## Battle Performance

Garganacl is a premier support tank with Purifying Salt, Salt Cure chip, and Recover. Wide Guard, Stealth Rock, and Body Press make it a doubles staple.

Its Special Defense is enormous; Earthquake and Fighting moves remain checks. Salt Cure wears down Water-types and stall teams, earning VGC usage alongside Amoonguss-style compositions.
