## Who is Virizion?

Virizion is a Grass/Fighting-type Legendary Pokémon from Generation V, the fastest Sword of Justice, embodying protection of Pokémon in threatened forests. Its deer-like grace and swordplay contrast Terrakion’s brute force and Cobalion’s discipline.

Unovan druids depict Virizion teaching Pokémon to evade humans rather than fight, a pacifist counterpoint in the trio’s martial philosophy.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Virizion’s horns slice air currents, accelerating its sprints through dense woods. Pokédex entries describe it appearing where forests burn, planting seeds in ash that sprout within days.

It communicates with plant Pokémon via pollen signals, coordinating ecosystem recovery after disasters.

## Battle Performance

Virizion is a fast physical attacker with Swords Dance, Close Combat, Leaf Blade, and Stone Edge. Justified boosts Special Attack in sun historically, though physical sets dominate.

It struggles in OU power creep but works in UU as a Fighting/Grass check with Taunt and Sacred Sword variants for Tyranitar.
