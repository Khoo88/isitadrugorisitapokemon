## Who is Aromatisse?

Aromatisse is a Fairy-type Pokémon introduced in Generation VI, evolving from Spritzee when traded while holding a Sachet. Its perfumed, flamenco-inspired design reflects Kalos’s emphasis on elegance, fragrance, and performative ritual in Pokémon culture.

In the Kalos region, Aromatisse is associated with salons and aromatherapy guilds that regulate mood in crowded cities. Trainers describe its presence as calming—though rivals warn that its scents can also induce disorientation in battle.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Aromatisse emits customized fragrance molecules that alter emotions—Pokédex entries note it can calm crying children or unsettle foes before duels. Its feathery appendages diffuse oils stored in sacs along its neck, creating colored mists during courtship dances.

Some field journals report symbiotic partnerships with Spritzee colonies, where elders “conduct” group scent harmonies that mark territorial boundaries without physical conflict.

## Battle Performance

Aromatisse competes as a bulky Fairy support with Aroma Veil, blocking Taunt and Encore for allies. Moves like Moonblast, Wish, Heal Pulse, and Trick Room support make it valuable in doubles.

Offensive sets are rare, but Calm Mind variants can sweep unprepared teams. Its mediocre Speed encourages slow-team synergy, especially alongside Trick Room sweepers.
