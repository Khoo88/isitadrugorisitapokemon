## Who is Regidrago?

Regidrago is a Dragon-type Legendary Pokémon from Generation VIII, one of the new Regi titans sealed in Split-Decision Ruins. Its head resembles an ancient draconic golem core, embodying dragon energy crystallized by human worship and fear.

Crown Tundra myth links Regidrago to the Dragon Orb and to Regigigas’s ancient regiment—less ecosystem guardian than symbolic lock on draconic power. Its lore is cryptic, runic, and battle-ready.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Regidrago’s body is a single dragon head orbiting limb joints, channeling dragon energy as plasma. Pokédex entries claim its shape was incomplete when sealed, hinting sculptors ran out of material—a meta joke with terrifying power implications.

When all Regis gather, tremors resonate globally, waking Regigigas in contemporary stories.

## Battle Performance

Regidrago hits with Dragon Energy, scaled by HP, and Dragon Dance or Choice Band sets with Outrage. Dragon’s Maw boosts STAB.

Its defenses are mediocre for Ubers, but wallbreaking power is immense. It pairs with Regieleki on dedicated Regi teams unlocking Regigigas in-game.
