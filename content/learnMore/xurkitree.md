## Who is Xurkitree?

Xurkitree is an Electric-type Ultra Beast from Generation VII, codename UB-03 Lighting. It resembles a humanoid tangle of power cables and sparking limbs, draining city grids when appearing in Alola—a literal blackout beast.

Aether reports document Xurkitree plugging into substations like feeding, its body pulsing with stolen megawatts. It is surreal, invasive, and offensively terrifying in special attack stats.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Xurkitree’s limbs are live wires with no insulation, arcing plasma in rain. Pokédex entries describe it as confused by terrestrial outlets, overcharging until explosions clear blocks.

During festivals, mistaken appearances caused island-wide blackouts blamed on infrastructure failure until Ultra Beast classification.

## Battle Performance

Xurkitree is a special wallbreaker with Beast Boost, Thunderbolt, Volt Switch, and Energy Ball or Dazzling Gleam. Choice Specs or Life Orb sets OHKO after one boost.

Its defenses are paper-thin; Ground and strong special hits kill first. In Ubers it trades blows faster than most, ending games in one turn if unchecked.
