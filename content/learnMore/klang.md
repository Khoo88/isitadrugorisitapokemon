## Who is Klang?

Klang is a Steel-type Pokémon from Generation V, the middle evolution between Klink and Klinklang. Its gear-body rotates in harmonic ratios, generating electromagnetic fields in Unova factories where wild specimens once powered mills illegally.

Engineers mimic Klang gear ratios for clockwork precision, respecting it as living machinery that predates industrial revolution in the region’s lore.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Klang’s gears must rotate synchronously or it collapses from seizure-like spasms. Pokédex entries describe it charging by spinning faster when attacked, converting kinetic energy into stored lightning.

It communicates with Klink colonies via rhythmic clicking audible through walls—early Morse code inspiration in Unovan textbooks.

## Battle Performance

Klang can use Eviolite with Shift Gear in NFE tiers, boosting Speed and Attack before Gear Grind. Clear Body prevents stat drops.

Competitive play targets Klinklang with +2 Shift Gear sweeps. Klang itself is a stepping-stone with solid Defense but outclassed before final evolution’s Speed.
