## Who is Axew?

Axew is a Dragon-type Pokémon introduced in Generation V, native to Unova’s rocky gorges. Before evolving into Fraxure and Haxorus, it is recognized by the tusk protruding from its mouth—used both as a tool and a weapon in territorial skirmishes.

Unovan folklore casts Axew as a symbol of disciplined growth: young dragons hone their tusks on mineral veins, leaving signature gouges geologists use to map populations. Trainers value it as a patient, determined partner.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Axew’s tusk regrows if broken, denser each regeneration cycle. Pokédex entries describe colonies sharing riverbank territories, with older Fraxure teaching juveniles to crack berries and stonefruit without damaging the pulp.

Some caverns glitter with tusk-scratch patterns reflecting light, creating misleading “gem veins” that attract unwary collectors—and predators.

## Battle Performance

As a pre-evolution, Axew appears in Little Cup or NFE formats with Dragon Claw, Outrage, and Aqua Tail. Its Ability Mold Breaker ignores defensive abilities like Levitate, giving early-game utility.

Fully evolved Haxorus carries competitive relevance; Axew itself is a stepping-stone with respectable Attack but fragile defenses at this stage.
