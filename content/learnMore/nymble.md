## Who is Nymble?

Nymble is a Bug-type Pokémon from Generation IX, the first stage of the Lokix line. Its grasshopper skateboarder design and frantic leaps made it a Paldea route mascot of kinetic energy before evolving into a ruthless striker.

Picnics near Mesagoza report Nymble stealing sandwiches with blinding hops—a comic introduction to a line that becomes serious competitive threat upon evolution into Lokix.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Nymble’s hind legs store elastic energy like biological springs, chirping in Morse-like patterns to coordinate swarm raids on berry farms. Pokédex entries describe it practicing jumps on flat stones until they crack.

It sheds exoskeleton plates that farmers grind into fertilizer, a rare beneficial pest relationship in Paldean agriculture.

## Battle Performance

Nymble is a fast Little Cup attacker with Tinted Lens or Swarm, First Impression, and Lunge. It foreshadows Lokix’s priority and pivot prowess.

As a pre-evolution, it dominates dedicated LC formats with high Attack and Speed. Competitive focus shifts to Lokix’s First Impression and U-turn cores in higher tiers.
