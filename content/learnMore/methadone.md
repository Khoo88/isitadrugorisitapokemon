## What is Methadone?

Methadone is a long-acting synthetic opioid agonist at mu-opioid receptors used for opioid use disorder maintenance therapy and chronic pain. Its NMDA receptor antagonism may contribute to efficacy in neuropathic and mixed pain. Highly lipophilic with long and variable half-life (15–60 hours), accumulation occurs over days when titrating.

QT interval prolongation via hERG channel blockade is a serious concern, especially at higher doses or with interacting drugs. Racemic mixture: R-methadone has most mu activity; both enantiomers affect QT.

Schedule II controlled substance; opioid treatment programs provide observed daily dosing for addiction treatment. Drug interactions via CYP3A4, 2D6, and others are extensive.

## Primary Uses

- Opioid use disorder maintenance treatment (reduces withdrawal, craving, and illicit use)
- Chronic severe pain when other opioids are inadequate (specialist pain management)
- Neonatal abstinence syndrome (inpatient protocols)
- Preoperative or perioperative analgesia (limited settings)

Take-home doses in addiction treatment require regulatory compliance and stability assessment.

## Common Side Effects

- Constipation, nausea, sedation
- Respiratory depression (especially with initiation, overdose, or drug interactions)
- QT prolongation, torsades de pointes
- Sweating, weight gain
- Sexual dysfunction
- **Warnings:** Only prescribers with X-waiver/DEA authority for OUD treatment; dangerous with benzodiazepines and alcohol; ECG monitoring at higher doses; prolonged QT drug interactions; neonatal withdrawal if used in pregnancy.
