## Who is Lunatone?

Lunatone is a Rock/Psychic-type Pokémon from Generation III, discovered alongside Solrock during a meteor shower. It drifts without moving its legs, levitating via psychic power while gazing at the moon—a Hoenn oddity tied to Mossdeep observatory lore.

Lunatone and Solrock duos symbolize celestial duality in gyms and puzzles. Its universe standing is puzzle Pokémon elevated by Trick Room and Levitate utility in niche metas.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Lunatone’s red eyes glow brightest during full moons, when it emits psychic pulses that calm nocturnal species. Pokédex entries claim it was born from meteor impact, never sleeping.

Parings with Solrock create artificial eclipses in Sootopolis legends, triggering Rayquaza awakening myths in expanded fanon.

## Battle Performance

Lunatone is a Trick Room setter with Levitate, Stealth Rock, and Explosion. Magic Room and Skill Swap support appear in doubles gimmicks.

Stats are balanced but mediocre; it fits TR teams needing Ground immunity and weatherless rock/psychic coverage. Solrock mirrors physically; Lunatone leans special with Psychic and Power Gem.
