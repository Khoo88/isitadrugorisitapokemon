## What is Ledipasvir?

Ledipasvir is a direct-acting antiviral agent that inhibits the hepatitis C virus (HCV) NS5A protein, a multifunctional protein essential for viral RNA replication, assembly, and secretion. NS5A inhibitors have high barrier to resistance when combined with other agents such as the nucleotide polymerase inhibitor sofosbuvir.

It is available as a fixed-dose combination with sofosbuvir (ledipasvir/sofosbuvir), achieving sustained virologic response in most genotypes 1, 4, 5, and 6 infections with 8–12 weeks of once-daily oral therapy in treatment-naïve and many experienced patients without cirrhosis or with compensated cirrhosis.

Ledipasvir is a substrate of P-glycoprotein and requires acid for absorption; proton pump inhibitors and antacids can reduce exposure if not timed appropriately.

## Primary Uses

- Chronic hepatitis C virus infection (genotypes 1, 4, 5, 6 in combination with sofosbuvir)
- HIV-coinfected patients (with attention to drug interactions with antiretrovirals)
- Selected decompensated cirrhosis regimens (with ribavirin, per specialist protocols)

HCV genotype testing and cirrhosis staging guide duration and ribavirin addition. Direct-acting antiviral regimens have largely replaced interferon-based therapy.

## Common Side Effects

- Generally well tolerated in clinical trials
- Headache, fatigue
- Nausea, diarrhea
- Insomnia
- **Warnings:** Test for hepatitis B before HCV therapy (HBV reactivation risk when HCV suppressed); major interactions via P-gp and breast cancer resistance protein (amiodarone, rifampin, St. John’s wort contraindicated or require avoidance); pregnancy risk with ribavirin if co-prescribed.
