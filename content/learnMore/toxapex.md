## Who is Toxapex?

Toxapex is a Poison/Water-type Pokémon from Generation VII, evolving from Mareanie. Its crown of toxic spines and regenerating core made it the face of stall metagames and Alolan reef defense against invasive Corsola predators.

Marine biologists credit Toxapex with restoring balance after Corsola collapse events, though trainers know it as nearly unkillable wall that outlasts entire teams.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Toxapex can regrow its core if the main body is destroyed, a sea star-like immortality loop. Pokédex entries describe it anchoring to rocks, waiting days for prey to touch venomous spikes.

Colonies form living reefs that filter pollution, turning toxic waste into harmless sediment over years.

## Battle Performance

Toxapex is the ultimate stall mon with Regenerator, Recover, Scald, and Toxic or Haze. Black Sludge and Baneful Bunker variants appear in OU.

It walls special attackers indefinitely unless Taunt, Knock Off, or Electric types break through. Gen VII defined “Pex balance” where teambuilding required multiple answers.
