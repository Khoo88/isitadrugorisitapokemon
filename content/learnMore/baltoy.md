## Who is Baltoy?

Baltoy is a Ground/Psychic-type Pokémon from Generation III, later gaining the Claydol evolution line. Shaped like a spinning clay figurine, it is tied to ancient desert ruins across Hoenn and beyond—often discovered near buried temples.

Archaeologists in the Pokémon world treat Baltoy as a living artifact, linking modern ecosystems to forgotten civilizations that may have carved its likeness into ritual pottery. Its perpetual spin symbolizes time and meditation in desert myths.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Baltoy hovers via psychic repulsion while its base spins for balance, generating faint hums that resonate in sandstone chambers. Pokédex entries say it sleeps upright, embedding itself in soil to absorb minerals overnight.

Desert travelers report circles of Baltoy performing synchronized rotations during eclipses—behavior still unexplained, though some propose geomagnetic alignment rituals.

## Battle Performance

Baltoy can set up Cosmic Power stacks with Recover, becoming a surprisingly durable win condition in lower tiers. Moves like Earth Power, Psychic, and Rapid Spin provide utility before evolution into Claydol.

Its stats are modest, but access to Stealth Rock and status moves makes it a staple in certain draft formats emphasizing hazard control.
