## Who is Glimmora?

Glimmora is a Rock/Poison-type Pokémon from Generation IX, evolving from Glimmet. Its crystal flower cannon aesthetic ties to Paldea’s tectonic lore—living geodes that bloom toxic petals when threatened.

Area Zero researchers link Glimmora to terrestrial energy vents, suggesting it metabolizes minerals into explosive pollen. It became infamous competitively and narratively as a symbol of Paldea’s harsh ecology.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Glimmora stores energy in petal crystals, venting heat through vents on its base. Pokédex entries describe it firing toxic shards like artillery when stepped on, reshaping cave walls into glittering galleries.

Colonies synchronize blooms during earthquakes, releasing spores that crystallize moisture—a natural air purifier with lethal side effects for unprotected hikers.

## Battle Performance

Glimmora is a fast special attacker with Toxic Debris and Mortal Spin hazard removal, or Choice Specs Power Gem and Sludge Wave. Earth Power covers Steel-types.

It excels as a lead that sets Toxic Spikes on switch-out. Fragile physically, but high Special Attack and Speed make it a premier offensive Rock-type in OU.
