## Who is Terapagos?

Terapagos is a Normal-type Legendary Pokémon from Generation IX, the turtle whose crystalline shell inspired Paldea’s Terastal phenomenon. Area Zero lore positions it as geological heart of the region—small in base form, cosmic in Terastal Forme.

Paldean history was rewritten when Terapagos was rediscovered, linking ancient empire collapse to over-terastalization. It is Gen IX’s central mythic organism, cute and world-shaping.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Terapagos’s shell refracts stellar energy into terastal crystals, saturating local ore deposits. Pokédex entries describe it hiding in gem mines for millennia, mistaken for ordinary crystal clusters.

Terastal Forme manifests a floating crown of type gems, symbolizing all eighteen types at once in battle spectacle.

## Battle Performance

Terapagos-Terastal gains Tera Shell, making first hit per battle deal minimal damage at full HP, plus Teraform Zero to suppress weather and terrain. Calm Mind and Tera Starstorm sweep.

It defines Gen IX OU and VGC as setup win condition. Checks require multi-hit or prior chip to break Tera Shell, then focused super-effective assaults.
