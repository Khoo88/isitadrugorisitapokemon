## Who is Duosion?

Duosion is a Psychic-type Pokémon from Generation V, the middle stage between Solosis and Reuniclus. Its split-brain design—two hemispheres communicating—explores themes of cooperation within a single organism evolving toward unified psychic power.

Unovan researchers study Duosion to model distributed computing, noting each half can solve puzzles independently before merging solutions upon evolution.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Duosion’s brains disagree occasionally, causing erratic movement until consensus forms. Pokédex entries describe telepathic fields that double when halves synchronize, repelling predators with mirrored illusions.

They gather in pairs at dusk, four hemispheres linked across two bodies, practicing psychic merges that prelude Reuniclus communal consciousness.

## Battle Performance

Duosion learns Recover, Psyshock, and Thunder in mid-level formats. In Little Cup or NFE tiers it can calibrate special bulk with Eviolite before evolving.

Reuniclus carries competitive weight; Duosion itself is a transitional stage with improving stats but still exploitable Dark and Ghost weaknesses.
