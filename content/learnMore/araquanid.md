## Who is Araquanid?

Araquanid is a Water/Bug-type Pokémon from Generation VII, evolving from Dewpider. It resembles a massive diving-bell spider that carries a shimmering water bubble around its head—an iconic visual that defines Alola’s creek ecosystems.

Trainers in Alola regard Araquanid as both protector and ambusher: gentle to Dewpider broods, ruthless to intruders. Trial Captain lore references its ability to weaponize water surfaces, making it a symbol of adaptation on volcanic islands.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

The bubble on Araquanid’s head is a portable habitat for smaller Dewpider, which ride inside during droughts. Pokédex entries claim it can fire pressurized jets or trap foes inside collapsing bubbles, drowning opponents in staged arenas of its own making.

Its long legs distribute weight across lily pads and wet rock, allowing silent approaches. Some coastal myths describe Araquanid guiding lost Wimpod schools to safe pools before preying on stragglers.

## Battle Performance

Araquanid’s signature Ability Water Bubble halves Fire damage and doubles Water attacks while preventing burns—a unique defensive profile. It often runs Liquidation, Leech Life, Mirror Coat, and Sticky Web support.

In battle formats allowing it, Araquanid can serve as a bulky attacker or trapper with Wide Guard in doubles. Its speed tier is modest, so it relies on typing quirks and priority moves like Aqua Jet when available.
