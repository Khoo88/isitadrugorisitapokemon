## What is Xiidra?

Xiidra (lifitegrast) is a small-molecule lymphocyte function-associated antigen-1 (LFA-1) antagonist that blocks interaction between LFA-1 on T cells and intercellular adhesion molecule-1 (ICAM-1) on antigen-presenting cells and ocular surface epithelium. This inhibits T-cell–mediated inflammation implicated in dry eye disease.

Topical ophthalmic solution is administered twice daily. Unlike cyclosporine ophthalmic emulsion (Restasis), onset of symptom relief may occur within weeks in trials, though individual response varies.

Systemic exposure is minimal with topical use; mechanism is distinct from artificial tears and anti-inflammatory drops.

## Primary Uses

- Signs and symptoms of dry eye disease (keratoconjunctivitis sicca)

Used when lubricants and environmental measures are insufficient; may be tried before or after cyclosporine depending on clinician preference and formulary.

## Common Side Effects

- Ocular irritation, burning, stinging on instillation
- Dysgeusia (unusual taste sensation—common, due to nasolacrimal drainage)
- Blurred vision
- Headache
- **Warnings:** Not for injection; remove contact lenses before instillation per labeling; discontinue if hypersensitivity; safe use in pregnancy not fully established.
