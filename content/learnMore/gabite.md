## Who is Gabite?

Gabite is a Dragon/Ground-type Pokémon from Generation IV, the middle stage between Gible and Garchomp. It hoards gems in desert burrows, shedding scales that merchants prize—behavior that made Sinnoh cave systems economically significant and dangerous.

Cave explorers report Gabite cartwheeling through tunnels at reckless speed, territorial yet glitter-obsessed. It stands as the adolescent powerhouse of Sinnoh’s most iconic pseudo-legendary line.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Gabite’s scales retain heat from underground magma vents, warming nests for Gible siblings. Pokédex entries describe it attacking anything near its gem piles, yet gifting shards to mates during courtship.

Overmining of Gabite hoards collapsed several Oreburgh-adjacent tunnels, prompting conservation laws in modern Sinnoh.

## Battle Performance

Gabite is banned or dominant in many Little Cup formats with Rough Skin, Dragon Claw, and Earthquake under Eviolite. Its stats approach pseudo-legendary levels prematurely.

In standard play it is a brief transition; competitive relevance explodes with Garchomp’s Swords Dance, Choice Scarf, or mixed sets. Gabite itself is feared in dedicated LC tiers.
