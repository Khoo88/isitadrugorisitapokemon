## Who is Toxel?

Toxel is an Electric/Poison-type baby Pokémon from Generation VIII, evolving into Toxtricity at level 30 depending on nature. Its sullen toddler design and drooling electric cheeks made it a Galar nursery icon of punk attitude before evolution.

Daycare centers in Galar stock noise-canceling headphones for Toxel tantrums, which discharge sparks when hungry or jealous of other Pokémon.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Toxel stores poison and electricity in separate cheek sacs, accidentally mixing them when sneezing—creating colorful sparks. Pokédex entries describe it draining energy from adults via contact, a parasitic cute phase.

It headbangs to rhythm even before evolution, hinting at musical combat destiny in Toxtricity forms.

## Battle Performance

Toxel has Rattled and Klutz with Belly Drum or Endeavor gimmicks in LC. It cannot evolve into both Toxtricity forms on one save without nature manipulation.

Competitive play targets Low Key or Amped Toxtricity with Punk Rock and Overdrive. Toxel itself is a novelty baby with Nuzzle and Flail strategies in dedicated formats.
