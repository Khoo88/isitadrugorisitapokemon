## Who is Volcarona?

Volcarona is a Bug/Fire-type Pokémon from Generation V, evolving from Larvesta. Revered as the “Sun Pokémon” in Unovan myth, its blazing wings symbolize rebirth when cast in shadow—worshipped in Relic Castle ruins and feared as apocalyptic moth.

Champions across generations adopt Volcarona as setup sweeper icon, matching its lore as deity of scorching light emerging from darkness.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Volcarona’s scales scatter embers that ignite droughts in legend, yet also hatch fertile ash fields. Pokédex entries describe it as a replacement sun during ice ages, a cosmic responsibility echoed in its 100 base Special Attack stat flavor.

Larvesta cocoons in volcanic ash for years before emergence ceremonies draw pilgrims in desert cultures.

## Battle Performance

Volcarona is a Quiver Dance sweeper with Fiery Dance, Bug Buzz, and Giga Drain. Flame Body punishes contact; Bulk Up variants exist.

Stealth Rock is its nemesis—one layer often decides games. After setup, it sweeps OU teams unless priority or Rock types strike first. Sun teams amplify Fire STAB further.
