## Who is Dracozolt?

Dracozolt is an Electric/Dragon-type fossil Pokémon from Generation VIII, fusing avian upper anatomy with a draconic tail generator. Like other Galar fossils, its existence is debated—yet battlefield success proved its combat viability.

Engineers study Dracozolt’s tail dynamo as inspiration for wireless charging prototypes, albeit scaled down for safety.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Pokédex entries describe Dracozolt storing lightning in its mane while its tail spins like a turbine. It allegedly ran in zigzags across ancient plains, electrocuting herds before feeding—behavior recreated in power grid surge legends.

Museums display animated models showing its clumsy gait, endearing visitors despite predatory history.

## Battle Performance

Dracozolt competes with Bolt Beak and Dragon Claw under sand or rain speed doubling via Slush Rush or Sand Rush. Choice Scarf sets function as wallbreakers.

Bulk is limited, and Ground-type attacks threaten it. In formats without Dracovish dominance, it remains a strong wallbreaker when weather is guaranteed.
