## Who is Arctovish?

Arctovish is a Water/Ice-type fossil Pokémon from Generation VIII, assembled from mismatched ancient parts—specifically a frozen fish head on a carnivorous reptilian body. It embodies Galar’s playful paleontology theme where “restored” species may not have existed naturally.

In-universe, researchers debate whether Arctovish ever swam coherently or merely flopped violently until prey tired. Museum plaques in Circhester treat it as a cautionary tale about genetic splicing without ecological context.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Pokédex entries famously claim Arctovish’s mouth was on the wrong end of its body, causing it to thrash until movement ceased—then it would feed. This absurd biology became a cultural meme among Galarian students.

Despite the joke, its icy cartilage and powerful tail suggest it could deliver crushing bites in cold shallows. Some legends say ancient Arctovish populations startled predators into hypothermic shock before scavenging remains.

## Battle Performance

Arctovish can run Slush Rush doubled speed in hail with Fishious Rend or Icicle Crash for devastating damage. Its Ability Slush Rush makes it a hail-exclusive sweeper when paired with Aurora Veil support.

Defensively it is awkward—weak to Grass, Fighting, Rock, and Steel—but offensive sets can overwhelm slower teams. Trick Room variants occasionally leverage its poor speed inverted into advantage.
