## What is Levocetirizine?

Levocetirizine is the active R-enantiomer of cetirizine, a second-generation H1-antihistamine with minimal sedative and anticholinergic effects at standard doses compared with first-generation agents. It antagonizes histamine H1 receptors peripherally, reducing allergic symptoms without substantial blood-brain barrier penetration at usual doses.

Once-daily dosing and rapid onset make it a mainstay for allergic rhinitis and chronic urticaria. Renal elimination predominates; dose reduction is required in severe renal impairment. It is less sedating than diphenhydramine but may still cause somnolence in sensitive individuals.

No significant cytochrome P450 metabolism reduces drug interaction burden versus first-generation antihistamines.

## Primary Uses

- Seasonal and perennial allergic rhinitis
- Chronic idiopathic urticaria
- Allergic conjunctivitis symptoms (adjunct)
- Angioedema adjunct (with other therapies, not monotherapy for hereditary angioedema)

Available OTC and by prescription depending on region and strength.

## Common Side Effects

- Somnolence (less than first-generation agents)
- Fatigue
- Dry mouth
- Headache
- **Warnings:** Reduce dose in creatinine clearance <50 mL/min; avoid in end-stage renal disease on dialysis per labeling; caution operating machinery if sedation occurs; pregnancy category per labeling (generally considered low risk in guidelines for second-generation agents).
