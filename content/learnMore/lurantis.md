## Who is Lurantis?

Lurantis is a Grass-type Pokémon from Generation VII, evolving from Fomantis. Its orchid mantis disguise and petal-blade arms make it a fashion icon in Alola, where trial captains showcase it as mastery of deceptive beauty.

Lurantis demands precise evolution at day level-up, reinforcing discipline narratives. It punches above its weight aesthetically while struggling competitively without support.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Lurantis colors itself like tropical flowers, striking prey that mistake it for flora. Pokédex entries describe it practicing poses in sunlight to maintain petal sharpness—a daily martial art.

Perfume makers harvest fallen petals illegally; wild Lurantis retaliate with Leaf Blade strikes that scar poachers’ hands as warnings.

## Battle Performance

Lurantis has Contrary with Leaf Storm and Superpower for sweep potential, or Technician Leaf Blade. It lacks Speed and bulk for OU.

Sun teams with Chlorophyll partners help; Defiant/Contrary memes aside, it remains a PU grass attacker needing setup turns. Knock Off and Poison coverage patch common checks.
