## Who is Regieleki?

Regieleki is an Electric-type Legendary Pokémon from Generation VIII, a living lightning bolt golem with the highest base Speed in the franchise. Its tangled cord body stores enough voltage to power countries—a Crown Tundra discovery that rewrote competitive speed tiers.

Legends describe Regieleki as the current that animated ancient golems, now bound in electric lattice. It is less character than force of nature shaped like a plug.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Regieleki’s body cannot be touched without insulation—trainers use rubber suits in mythic art. Pokédex entries warn that removing its rings unleashes uncontrolled storms across valleys.

It hums at 60 Hz in sync with power grids, occasionally causing brownouts near ruins where it sleeps.

## Battle Performance

Regieleki is the fastest Pokémon with Transistor-boosted Thunderbolt, Volt Switch, and Explosion. Choice Specs or Magnet Rise sets wallbreak instantly.

Frail HP means any attack kills if it doesn’t move first. In OU and VGC it functions as a glass cannon pivot or suicide lead that trades itself for massive damage.
