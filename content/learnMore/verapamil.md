## What is Verapamil?

Verapamil is a non-dihydropyridine calcium channel blocker with predominant cardiac effects: slows AV nodal conduction, reduces myocardial contractility, and dilates coronary arteries. It also relaxes vascular smooth muscle, lowering blood pressure.

Used for supraventricular tachycardias, rate control in atrial fibrillation, angina, and hypertension. Constipation is a distinctive adverse effect due to reduced colonic smooth muscle motility.

Extensive CYP3A4 metabolism creates drug interactions (statins, especially simvastatin and lovastatin, cyclosporine, digoxin). Avoid in heart failure with reduced ejection fraction and WPW with atrial fibrillation.

## Primary Uses

- Hypertension
- Chronic stable angina, vasospastic angina
- Atrial fibrillation/flutter rate control
- Supraventricular tachycardia (acute IV and oral maintenance)
- Cluster headache prophylaxis (off-label)
- Migraine prophylaxis (off-label)

## Common Side Effects

- Constipation (very common)
- Bradycardia, heart block
- Hypotension, dizziness
- Peripheral edema (less than dihydropyridines)
- Heart failure exacerbation in susceptible patients
- **Warnings:** Contraindicated with beta-blockers in some AV block settings; do not use IV in WPW with AF; digoxin level increase; grapefruit interaction; avoid in severe LV dysfunction.
