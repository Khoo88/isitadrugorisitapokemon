## Who is Flygon?

Flygon is a Ground/Dragon-type Pokémon from Generation III, evolving from Vibrava. Nicknamed the “Desert Spirit,” its wing beats produce singing sandstorms that guide travelers—or warn them away from nesting dunes.

Hoenn desert tribes revere Flygon as an oasis guardian. Despite not being a pseudo-legendary, its mystique and anime presence elevated it to fan-favorite status across generations, embodying graceful power in arid ecosystems.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Flygon’s red lenses filter sand while its wings create resonant hums audible miles away. Pokédex entries say it never blinks, instead coating eyes with protective film during storms.

Mating pairs spiral above dunes at dusk, carving temporary patterns visible from satellites—mistaken for ancient glyphs by tourists.

## Battle Performance

Flygon offers Levitate immunity to Ground, U-turn pivoting, and Draco Meteor or Earthquake coverage. It lacks raw power compared to true dragons but excels as a scout in UU.

Outclassed in OU by Garchomp and Landorus, it still sees play with Defog support and resistance to Electric. Dragon Dance sets surprise in lower tiers with Outrage flinch risk.
