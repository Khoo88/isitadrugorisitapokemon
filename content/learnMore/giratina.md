## Who is Giratina?

Giratina is a Ghost/Dragon-type Legendary Pokémon from Generation IV, banished to the Distortion World for its violent nature after creation conflicts with Arceus. It guards antimatter reality, serpentine in Origin Forme and regal in Altered Forme within cemeteries.

Sinnoh myth treats Giratina as death’s counterpart to Dialga’s time and Palkia’s space—less evil than exiled balance keeper. Its standing is among the most cosmically significant legends in the franchise.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Giratina’s shadow wings absorb spiritual pollution from battlefields, explaining hauntings near cemeteries. Pokédex entries describe it appearing in ancient ruins, dragging vandals into its realm.

Origin Forme adapts for flight in distorted gravity, while Altered Forme walks the material plane only when reality thins during eclipses.

## Battle Performance

Giratina-Origin is an offensive behemoth with Levitate, Shadow Force, Draco Meteor, and Defog on dedicated teams. Altered Forme is a premier special wall with Pressure, Will-O-Wisp, and Dragon Tail.

Both forms shape restricted formats. Origin hits harder but is frailer; Altered stalls and removes hazards. Ghost/Dragon typing is excellent offensively with few safe switches.
