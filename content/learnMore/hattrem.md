## Who is Hattrem?

Hattrem is a Psychic-type Pokémon from Generation VIII, evolving from Hatenna into Hatterene. It develops stronger emotional filtering, crushing objects that disturb its meditation with telekinetic clamps—a teenage moodiness phase of the line.

Galarian hikers learn to whisper near Hattrem groves; one shout can trigger synchronized psychic squeezes on camping gear. It bridges cute empathy and witch-like severity.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Hattrem’s braids act as antennae, severing psychic links to noisy creatures. Pokédex entries state it will not tolerate strong emotions nearby, even joyful ones, until it evolves greater control.

It arranges stones in spiral patterns that amplify calm fields, used by monks who seek Hattrem-assisted retreats.

## Battle Performance

Hattrem learns Brutal Swing and Dazzling Gleam mid-evolution but sees little competitive use outside NFE formats. Magic Bounce and Healer arrive on final evolution.

Eviolite Calm Mind sets are rare; trainers rush to Hatterene for Trick Room and Magic Bounce cores in VGC.
