## Who is Cacturne?

Cacturne is a Grass/Dark-type Pokémon from Generation III, evolving from Cacnea. Its scarecrow silhouette and jack-o’-lantern grin evoke desert folklore about straw effigies that move when the moon is full.

Hoenn myths describe Cacturne packs trailing caravans silently, offering help by day and pilfering supplies by night—stories used to teach children about trusting appearances in the desert.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Cacnea’s spines harden into dark-tipped needles capable of injecting irritants. Pokédex entries say it follows travelers for weeks without sleep, waiting for exhaustion to create openings for ambush.

During sandstorms, colonies link arms via vine threads, forming living fences that redirect wildlife away from buried eggs—a rare cooperative behavior for such ominous Pokémon.

## Battle Performance

Cacturne is a slow mixed attacker with Sucker Punch, Seed Bomb, and Knock Off. Sand Rush doubles speed in sandstorm, enabling sweeps with Swords Dance or Nasty Plot if weather is active.

Its frailty and numerous weaknesses keep it in lower tiers, but it can punish passive teams with priority and status immunity to Prankster tricks when built carefully.
