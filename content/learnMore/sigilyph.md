## Who is Sigilyph?

Sigilyph is a Psychic/Flying-type Pokémon from Generation V, resembling a Nazca Lines bird come to life. It guards desert ruins across Unova and beyond, floating in geometric patterns that archaeologists map to ancient constellations.

Unovan conspiracy forums link Sigilyph to extraterrestrial cartography; League researchers insist it is a natural psychic sentinel species predating human civilization in the region.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Sigilyph’s eyes never blink, constantly processing psychic telemetry of trespassers. Pokédex entries describe it attacking anyone damaging ruins, pursuing them miles beyond site boundaries.

Flocks paint identical glyphs in sand overnight, glyphs that match pottery from 3,000-year-old digs—evidence of cultural transmission or mimicry debated hotly.

## Battle Performance

Sigilyph is a Magic Guard special attacker with Cosmic Power, Stored Power, and Psyshock, or offensive sets with Air Slash and Heat Wave. It cannot be hurt by indirect damage under Magic Guard.

Sturdy variants use Explosion or Reflect types in doubles. Offensive pressure is moderate, but setup Stored Power sweeps surprise in lower tiers when bolstered.
