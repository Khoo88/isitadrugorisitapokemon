## Who is Ferroseed?

Ferroseed is a Grass/Steel-type Pokémon from Generation V, evolving into Ferrothorn. It clings to cave ceilings like a spiked pinecone, dropping on unsuspecting prey in Unova’s Chargestone Cave and later appearing across Kalos and Galar tunnels.

Miners once cleared Ferroseed clusters with magnets, unaware the species absorbs iron to harden its shell. It embodies slow, patient predation in the Pokémon world’s underground ecosystems.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Ferroseed’s spikes are hardened moss needles infused with metal filings. Pokédex entries say it absorbs minerals from cave walls, glowing faintly when ore veins run nearby—prospectors follow them at their peril.

When threatened, it curls tighter, becoming a living caltrop that rusts attackers’ claws over hours of contact.

## Battle Performance

Ferroseed is a premier Little Cup and NU defensive piece with Eviolite, Leech Seed, Gyro Ball, and Spikes. Iron Barbs punishes contact before evolution.

It foreshadows Ferrothorn’s elite stall role; as Ferroseed, it stacks hazards and drains teams despite low Speed, making it a staple in dedicated LC formats.
