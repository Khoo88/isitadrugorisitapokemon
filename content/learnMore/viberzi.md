## What is Viberzi?

Viberzi (eluxadoline) is an oral mixed mu-opioid receptor agonist and delta-opioid receptor antagonist that modulates GI motility and visceral pain pathways, reducing symptoms of irritable bowel syndrome with diarrhea (IBS-D). Mu agonism decreases colonic transit and secretion; delta antagonism may reduce pain signaling.

It is contraindicated without a gallbladder due to increased risk of pancreatitis in post-cholecystectomy patients in post-marketing reports. Sphincter of Oddi dysfunction may contribute to pancreatitis risk.

Schedule IV controlled substance in the United States due to opioid receptor activity; abuse potential is lower than full agonists but caution applies.

## Primary Uses

- Irritable bowel syndrome with diarrhea (IBS-D) in adults

Not for IBS with constipation or unclassified IBS without diarrhea predominance.

## Common Side Effects

- Constipation (most common—can be severe)
- Nausea, abdominal pain
- Vomiting
- Pancreatitis (especially without gallbladder—contraindicated)
- Sphincter of Oddi spasm (abdominal pain, elevated liver enzymes)
- **Warnings:** Contraindicated if no gallbladder; avoid in alcoholism, pancreatitis history, severe liver impairment; avoid with other opioids causing constipation; may cause sphincter of Oddi dysfunction symptoms.
