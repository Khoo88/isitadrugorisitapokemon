## What is Nirmatrelvir?

Nirmatrelvir is an oral protease inhibitor that blocks the SARS-CoV-2 main protease (Mpro/3CLpro), preventing cleavage of viral polyproteins essential for replication. It is co-packaged with ritonavir, which inhibits CYP3A4 metabolism of nirmatrelvir, boosting plasma levels to therapeutic concentrations.

Treatment must begin within 5 days of symptom onset in high-risk outpatients with mild to moderate COVID-19 to reduce progression to severe disease, hospitalization, and death. Extensive drug-drug interactions via ritonavir require medication review and often temporary hold of interacting drugs.

Renally cleared active moieties require dose adjustment in moderate renal impairment; not recommended in severe impairment per labeling.

## Primary Uses

- Treatment of mild to moderate COVID-19 in outpatients at high risk of progression (age, comorbidities, immunocompromise per guidelines)
- Not for pre-exposure or post-exposure prophylaxis as sole standard (evolving guidelines)
- Not for hospitalized patients requiring oxygen (benefit limited in late disease)

EUA/approval criteria and variant susceptibility should be checked against current public health guidance.

## Common Side Effects

- Dysgeusia (metallic or bitter taste—very common)
- Diarrhea, nausea
- Hypertension (transient)
- **Warnings:** Major CYP3A4 interactions (statins, immunosuppressants, some anticoagulants, amiodarone, etc.); hepatic impairment caution; rebound symptoms reported rarely; HIV patients need regimen adjustment to avoid protease inhibitor resistance if unsuppressed.
