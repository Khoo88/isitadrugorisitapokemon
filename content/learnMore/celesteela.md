## Who is Celesteela?

Celesteela is a Steel/Flying-type Ultra Beast codenamed “Launch,” introduced in Generation VII. It resembles a bamboo rocket—an invasive organism that blurs the line between plant, machine, and alien weapon.

Aether reports describe Celesteela as gentle when feeding on tree sap, yet capable of explosive takeoffs that flatten forests. Alolan conservationists classify it among the most destructive Ultra Beast landings.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Celesteela stores flammable gas in hollow stalks, igniting controlled burns for propulsion. Pokédex entries claim it absorbs nutrients from soil via metallic roots while airborne, leaving scorched launch pads in meadows.

Witnesses compare its vertical ascent to spacecraft tests, inspiring engineering prototypes for lightweight alloys—though replication remains unsafe.

## Battle Performance

Celesteela is a defensive behemoth with Beast Boost, Leech Seed, and recovery moves like Roost. It can wall many threats while boosting Defense or Special Attack after KOs, then sweep with Heavy Slam or Air Slash.

Weaknesses to Fire and Electric are notable, but Ground immunity aids switching. Special sets run Flamethrower and Grass Knot for coverage in restricted formats.
