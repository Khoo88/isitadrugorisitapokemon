## Who is Pikachu?

**Pikachu** is an Electric-type Pokémon and the global mascot of the franchise. It evolves from Pichu and into Raichu (with a regional Alolan form).

### Why it sounds like a drug

Marketing-friendly, rhythmic names appear in both pharmacies and Pokédexes. Pikachu's simplicity is memorable — just like many brand-name medications.

### Quick facts

- Type: Electric
- Debut: Pokémon Red & Blue (1996)
- Signature move: Thunderbolt
