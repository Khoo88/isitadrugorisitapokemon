## What is Pradaxa?

Pradaxa (dabigatran) is a direct oral anticoagulant (DOAC) that reversibly inhibits thrombin (factor IIa), blocking conversion of fibrinogen to fibrin in the coagulation cascade. It is a prodrug (dabigatran etexilate) converted to active dabigatran after oral absorption.

Unlike warfarin, routine INR monitoring is not required. Specific reversal agent idarucizumab (Praxbind) is available for life-threatening bleeding. Dabigatran has the highest renal elimination among DOACs (~80%), mandating dose reduction or avoidance in advanced chronic kidney disease.

Gastrointestinal upset is more common than with factor Xa inhibitors. Storage requires intact bottle to protect from moisture once opened.

## Primary Uses

- Stroke and systemic embolism prevention in nonvalvular atrial fibrillation
- Treatment and prevention of deep vein thrombosis and pulmonary embolism
- Postoperative venous thromboembolism prophylaxis after hip replacement (selected regimens)

Not for mechanical heart valves or moderate-to-severe mitral stenosis.

## Common Side Effects

- Dyspepsia, nausea, abdominal pain
- Bleeding (major and minor)
- Diarrhea
- **Warnings:** Contraindicated in mechanical valves; adjust or avoid in renal impairment; hold before surgery per protocol; idarucizumab for emergency reversal; increased bleeding with antiplatelet agents and NSAIDs.
