## Who is Klinklang?

Klinklang is a Steel-type Pokémon from Generation V, final form of the Klink line. Three gears orbit a red core, accelerating into drill-like momentum that symbolizes Unova’s industrial fairy tale aesthetic.

Factory legends claim a Klinklang core once powered an entire ship across the ocean—a myth debunked yet celebrated in Castelia tech museums. It remains a cult favorite for Shift Gear sweepers.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Klinklang’s red core spikes Speed when gears align at maximum RPM. Pokédex entries say it launches mini gears as shrapnel when threatened, regrowing them within hours.

Overclocking causes overheating steam vents—a safety valve trainers must monitor during long battles.

## Battle Performance

Klinklang uses Shift Gear plus Gear Grind or Return with Magnet Rise coverage. Clear Body protects setup; Electric immunity with Magnet Rise catches opponents off guard.

Outclassed in higher tiers by faster sweepers, but in NU it cleans teams after one setup. Ground moves remain lethal without Magnet Rise active.
