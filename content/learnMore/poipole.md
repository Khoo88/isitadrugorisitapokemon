## Who is Poipole?

Poipole is a Poison-type Ultra Beast from Generation VII, the juvenile form of Naganadel gifted to champions who thwarted Ultra Necrozma. Its playful alien design belies a creature raised in another world’s hive structures before bonding with trainers.

Ultra Megalopolis lore treats Poipole as ambassador species—cute face of invasive biology. Its standing is narrative reward and stepping stone to one of the strongest special dragons in competitive play.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Poipole squeaks in ultrasonic harmonics that stimulate plant growth accidentally, confusing botanists. Pokédex entries describe it learning affection by mimicking trainer expressions, a rare emotional intelligence among Ultra Beasts.

It stores venom in tail buds, playfully squirting dilute toxin during games that can numb fingers for hours.

## Battle Performance

Poipole learns Dragon Pulse and Toxic only after evolution to Naganadel. In LC or story modes it runs Poison Jab and Charm.

Competitive relevance is entirely post-evolution with Beast Boost.special sweeping. Poipole itself is a trophy mon with middling stats outside dedicated novelty tiers.
