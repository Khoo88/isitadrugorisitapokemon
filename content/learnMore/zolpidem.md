## What is Zolpidem?

Zolpidem is a nonbenzodiazepine hypnotic (Z-drug) that selectively binds the benzodiazepine site on GABAA receptors containing alpha-1 subunits, promoting sleep onset with less muscle relaxant and anxiolytic effect than traditional benzodiazepines at hypnotic doses.

Immediate-release, extended-release, and sublingual formulations target sleep onset and maintenance. Short half-life limits next-day sedation but complex sleep behaviors (sleepwalking, sleep-driving) occur as class warnings.

Schedule IV controlled substance with dependence potential; recommended for short-term insomnia treatment with cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia as first-line per guidelines.

## Primary Uses

- Short-term treatment of insomnia characterized by difficulty with sleep initiation (and maintenance with CR formulation)
- Sleep onset insomnia in adults

## Common Side Effects

- Drowsiness, dizziness
- Headache
- Complex sleep-related behaviors (parasomnias)
- Next-day impairment (especially CR and in women—lower doses recommended)
- Nausea, diarrhea
- **Warnings:** Contraindicated with history of complex sleep behaviors on sedative-hypnotics; lowest effective dose; avoid alcohol and other CNS depressants; caution in elderly (FDA reduced dose recommendations); rebound insomnia on discontinuation; pregnancy not recommended.
