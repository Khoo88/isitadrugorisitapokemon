## Who is Landorus?

Landorus is a Ground/Flying-type Legendary Pokémon from Generation V, guardian of fields and brother to Tornadus and Thundurus. It descends to punish those who damage crops, earning farmer reverence across Unova and beyond.

Incarnate and Therian forms split swaggering power from tactical pivoting. Landorus defined OU for years as the quintessential offensive pivot—mythic, regulatory, and mechanically dominant.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Landorus’s tail stores fertilizing energy released in storms that restore soil. Pokédex entries describe it destroying those who pillage nature, then planting seeds in crater footprints.

Therian form sacrifices showmanship for hunting efficiency, diving from jet streams like a living missile.

## Battle Performance

Landorus-Therian is an OU staple with Intimidate, U-turn, Earthquake, and Stone Edge. Landorus-Incarnate runs Sheer Force Sludge Wave, Earth Power, and Rock Slide with no immunities to Ground.

Both forms wallbreak and pivot. Misty Terrain and strong water types check them, yet momentum teams still slot Landorus-T into nearly every generation’s meta.
