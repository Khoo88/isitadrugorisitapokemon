## Who is Dragalge?

Dragalge is a Poison/Dragon-type Pokémon from Generation VI, evolving from Skrelp. Its seaweed dragon design and venomous spines make it an ambush predator of Kalos and Alola reefs—elegant, toxic, and patient.

Divers respect Dragalge kelp forests as beautiful no-go zones unless equipped with antidotes. Marine biologists document its symbiotic camouflage with coral polyps.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Dragalge injects poison through spines disguised as kelp fronds. Pokédex entries claim its secretions corrode metal hulls, explaining shipwrecks near breeding grounds.

Courtship involves exchanging toxin cocktails that strengthen offspring immunity—a rare example of poison as parental investment.

## Battle Performance

Dragalge’s Poison Touch and Adaptability (hidden) enable deadly Sludge Wave and Draco Meteor hits. It can run defensive sets with Toxic Spikes and Dragon Tail phazing, or Choice Specs special offense.

Its Speed is low, but typing grants Fairy resistance when airborne. Adaptability-boosted attacks break cores unprepared for special poison damage.
