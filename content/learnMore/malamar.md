## Who is Malamar?

Malamar is a Dark/Psychic-type Pokémon from Generation VI, evolving from Inkay through the famous upside-down level-up. Its inverted squid tyrant design and Contrary ability made it a setup sweeper icon and meme simultaneously.

Kalos coastal villages feared Malamar hypnotizing sailors; trainers reframed it as a strategic prodigy that punishes Intimidate and stat-lowering moves by reversing them into buffs.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Malamar’s spots flash in complex spirals that rewrite prey’s sense of direction. Pokédex entries describe it as the overturned evolution of Inkay—literally and figuratively—a dark mastermind pulling strings from tide pools.

It wraps foes in tentacles, draining resolve before delivering finishing blows—a behavior mirrored in competitive Contrary sweeps.

## Battle Performance

Malamar runs Contrary with Superpower, Night Slash, and Rest or Psycho Cut after Topsy-Turvy alternatives. One Superpower grants +2 Attack and +2 Defense.

It dominated formats weak to Dark/Psychic coverage until priority and Fairy-types adapted. Trick Room Malamar still appears, laughing at Intimidate users who accidentally boost it.
