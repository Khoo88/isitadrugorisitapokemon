## Who is Lunala?

Lunala is a Psychic/Ghost-type Legendary Pokémon from Generation VII, mascot of Pokémon Moon and evolution of Cosmog into bat-winged guardian of night. It absorbs light into midnight wings, traversing dimensions as the “beast that calls the moon.”

Alolan myth pairs Lunala with Solgaleo as cosmic emissaries, guiding spirits and trainers across Ultra Wormholes. Its mournful cry defines Alola’s celestial identity.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Lunala stores thousands of spirits in its wings, releasing them as light spears when hunting Necrozma-class threats. Pokédex entries describe it quietly observing sleeping towns, feared as death omen yet protective against Ultra Beast incursions.

Moondust from its wings causes vivid dreams—studied for therapy, regulated for abuse potential.

## Battle Performance

Lunala is a bulky special attacker with Shadow Shield (Multiscale equivalent), Moongeist Beam, Psyshock, and Roost. Weak to Ghost and Dark but checks many setup sweepers.

It competes in VGC restricted formats as a defensive win condition. Necrozma fusion forms alter role; base Lunala stalls and breaks with unmatched neutral coverage on Moongeist Beam.
