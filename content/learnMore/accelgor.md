## Who is Accelgor?

Accelgor is a Bug-type Pokémon introduced in Generation V, evolving from Shelmet when traded for a Karrablast. It resembles a sleek, ninja-like sea slug encased in a folding organic cloak, built for speed rather than defense. In the Unova region’s folklore of opposing evolution lines, Accelgor embodies agility and offense—the counterpart to Escavalier’s armored knight motif.

Within the broader Pokémon world, Accelgor is often depicted as a specialist striker: lightweight, fragile, but difficult to pin down. Its design draws from gastropods and shinobi aesthetics, signaling a creature that wins through momentum and precise strikes instead of prolonged brawls.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Pokédex entries emphasize Accelgor’s mucous membranes, which it uses both for hydration and as a slippery barrier against attackers. It is said to lose moisture rapidly outside humid environments, forcing it to wrap itself in many layers of protective film—hence its billowing scarf-like mantle in artwork.

Behaviorally, Accelgor is portrayed as hyperactive and evasive, darting through wetlands and ruins at speeds that leave afterimages. Some entries suggest it fires pressurized liquid or “ninja spit” to harass foes before closing distance, reinforcing its hit-and-run biology.

## Battle Performance

Accelgor competes as a fast special attacker with access to moves like Bug Buzz, Focus Blast, Energy Ball, and Spikes. Its Hidden Ability Unburden doubles speed after losing an item, making it a terrifying late-game sweeper when paired with White Herb or consumables.

Defensively it falters due to poor bulk and a weakness to Stealth Rock, but in rain or under Tailwind it can outspeed much of the metagame. Competitive sets often lean on Sticky Web support or all-out offense, trading longevity for immediate pressure.
