## Who is Jirachi?

Jirachi is a Steel/Psychic-type Mythical Pokémon from Generation III, awakening once per millennium to grant wishes written on notes attached to its head. Hoenn’s Millennium Festival and global events treat it as hope incarnate—a star child sleeping in crystalline cocoon.

Its universe role blends fairy tale and tactical threat: Serene Grace iron heads made it beloved and feared. Jirachi symbolizes rare benevolence among Mythicals tied to cosmic cycles.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Jirachi hibernates in a comet-like shell, dreaming futures into probability. Pokédex entries claim it wakes seven days per thousand years, answering one wish truly while others manifest as fortune’s ambiguity.

Dust from its awakening forms gemstones said to bring luck—black market trade is heavily prosecuted by international Pokémon authorities.

## Battle Performance

Jirachi is a paraflinch menace with Serene Grace Iron Head and Body Slam, Wish + Protect, and Calm Mind sets. Fire Punch covers Steel-types; U-turn pivots.

It was banned in many formats for luck-based control. Defensive typing grants numerous resistances; Heatran and Ground types check it reliably when immune to flinch tactics.
