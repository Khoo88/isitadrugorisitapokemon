## Who is Duskull?

Duskull is a Ghost-type Pokémon from Generation III, evolving into Dusclops and Dusknoir. Its hollow-eyed mask and night-wandering habits made it a classic spooky companion of Hoenn’s early routes—mischievous yet oddly loyal.

Folktales describe Duskull leading lost travelers astray or safely home depending on mood. Its single red eye peering through a rotating body became iconic in ghost-type marketing.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Duskull’s body is 99% hollow, absorbing impacts while it phases through walls. Pokédex entries claim it loves children’s crying (playfully mischievous framing), yet also rescues souls of lost Pokémon—a dual nature explored in anime lore.

At midnight, groups orbit streetlamps, creating flickering halos mistaken for will-o’-wisps.

## Battle Performance

Duskull is a premier Eviolite tank in Little Cup with phenomenal defenses, Will-O-Wisp, and Night Shade. Pain Split and status moves let it stall far above its stage.

Evolution into Dusclops and Dusknoir brings Trick Room and tank roles; as Duskull, it defines LC ghost-type metagames with frustration value.
