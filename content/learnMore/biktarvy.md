## What is Biktarvy?

Biktarvy is a fixed-dose combination antiretroviral tablet containing bictegravir (integrase strand transfer inhibitor), emtricitabine (nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitor), and tenofovir alafenamide (tenofovir prodrug). Bictegravir blocks HIV integrase, preventing viral DNA integration into host chromosomes. Emtricitabine and tenofovir alafenamide inhibit reverse transcriptase after intracellular phosphorylation, terminating viral DNA chain elongation.

Tenofovir alafenamide delivers tenofovir diphosphate to lymphocytes with lower plasma tenofovir exposure than tenofovir disoproxil fumarate, reducing renal and bone toxicity risk while maintaining antiviral efficacy. Biktarvy is administered once daily without food restrictions for most patients.

Resistance requires multiple mutations; baseline genotype is recommended in treatment-experienced patients or when non-adherence is suspected.

## Primary Uses

- Complete regimen for treatment-naïve adults and adolescents with HIV-1 infection
- Switch therapy in virologically suppressed patients without known resistance to components
- HIV-1 treatment when a single-tablet, integrase-based regimen is appropriate

Not for pre-exposure prophylaxis (PrEP) as formulated; separate tenofovir/emtricitabine products are used for PrEP.

## Common Side Effects

- Generally well tolerated
- Headache
- Diarrhea, nausea
- Fatigue
- Small weight gain (class effect with integrase inhibitors in some cohorts)
- Renal and bone effects (lower than older tenofovir disoproxil formulations but monitoring still required)
- **Warnings:** Test for hepatitis B before initiation (hepatitis flare if HBV co-infected and therapy stopped); monitor renal function and HIV RNA; drug interactions primarily via bictegravir and organic cation transporter pathways (consult interaction references).
