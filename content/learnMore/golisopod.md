## Who is Golisopod?

Golisopod is a Bug/Water-type Pokémon from Generation VII, evolving from Wimpod. Its armored samurai crustacean design and cowardly pre-evolution created a narrative of bravery earned under pressure—famous for Emergency Exit memes and anime heroics.

Alolan docks hire Golisopod pairs as living cranes, trusting their First Impression ambushes only against pests. It stands as Alola’s premier physical bug warrior despite comedic retreat habits.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Golisopod’s claws can cleave ocean currents, yet Wimpod instincts linger—when injured, it flees reflexively. Pokédex entries praise its six-armed blade style, each limb independently gripping prey.

Shell polish rituals at dawn attract fish schools, turning harbors into feeding frenzies ecologists study for sustainable fishing partnerships.

## Battle Performance

Golisopod’s First Impression and Emergency Exit enable hit-and-run pivots with Liquidation and Leech Life. Assault Vest variants tank special hits.

Emergency Exit can backfire by forcing switches at half HP, but in doubles First Impression threatens frail leads. It competes in UU as a priority physical attacker with Swords Dance options.
