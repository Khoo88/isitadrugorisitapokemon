## Who is Necrozma?

Necrozma is a Psychic-type Legendary Pokémon from Generation VII, the Prism Pokémon that devours light and fuses with Solgaleo or Lunala into Dusk Mane and Dawn Wings forms, or becomes Ultra Necrozma in its ultimate state.

Alolan myth frames Necrozma as a fallen light thief from another world, crashing into Megalo Tower and warping the region’s relationship with Z-Moves and aura. It is cosmic horror wrapped in crystalline geometry.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Necrozma’s body refracts light into weaponized lasers; when starved, it turns feral and raids Solgaleo/Lunala energy. Pokédex entries describe it weeping black light when wounded—a visual poets compare to eclipses.

Ultra form sheds all armor, becoming a dragon of pure photonic rage that reshapes battlefields in story climaxes.

## Battle Performance

Ultra Necrozma dominates with Neuroforce, Photon Geyser, and insane mixed stats. Dusk Mane and Dawn Wings blend Steel/Ghost or Psychic/Ghost typings with boosted stats.

Base Necrozma sets Stealth Rock and Trick Room in niche play. All forms are restricted staples; Prismatic Laser and Sunsteel/Moongeist coverage define Gen VII–IX power levels.
