## Who is Noivern?

Noivern is a Flying/Dragon-type Pokémon from Generation VI, evolving from Noibat. Its speaker-wing design and ultrasonic screeches made it Kalos’s night guardian of cliffs and caves—elegant, loud, and faster than most dragons when trained.

Challenger caves feature Noivern as apex aerial predators that map terrain through echolocation, inspiring sound-based gym puzzles and horror stories about disorienting booms in fog.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Noivern’s ears can shatter boulders when it roars at full power, yet it whispers lullabies to Noibat young at subsonic levels. Pokédex entries warn that prolonged exposure to its cries causes migraines and memory gaps.

It drinks flower nectar for energy, linking dragon majesty to surprisingly delicate dietary habits.

## Battle Performance

Noivern offers Infiltrator Hurricane and Boomburst sets with Choice Specs or Life Orb. High Speed lets it break substitutes and screens.

Competitive usage is niche in UU; Dragon/Flying typing grants Ground immunity via Levitate on Noibat line—Noivern has Infiltrator instead. Flamethrower and Draco Meteor round coverage against Steel and Ice threats.
