## What is Sofosbuvir?

Sofosbuvir is a nucleotide analog inhibitor of hepatitis C virus (HCV) NS5B RNA-dependent RNA polymerase, incorporated into viral RNA and causing chain termination. It is a prodrug metabolized to the active uridine triphosphate form with pangenotypic activity across HCV genotypes when combined with other direct-acting antivirals.

High cure rates (>95%) with 8–12 weeks of oral therapy in most patients replaced interferon-based regimens. Formulations include combinations with ledipasvir, velpatasvir, and voxilaprevir.

Minimal drug interactions compared with protease inhibitors; P-gp inducers (rifampin, St. John’s wort) reduce efficacy.

## Primary Uses

- Chronic hepatitis C infection (all genotypes, in combination regimens)
- Decompensated cirrhosis (with ribavirin in some regimens, specialist management)
- Post-liver transplant HCV recurrence (specialist protocols)
- HIV coinfection (with attention to antiretroviral interactions in combined products)

Test for hepatitis B before treatment—HBV reactivation risk if HCV cleared.

## Common Side Effects

- Generally well tolerated
- Fatigue, headache
- Nausea, insomnia
- **Warnings:** HBV screening mandatory; review combined product interactions; use with ribavirin adds anemia and teratogenicity constraints; not monotherapy—always combined per labeling.
