## Who is Elgyem?

Elgyem is a Psychic-type Pokémon from Generation V, resembling a small grey alien with luminous fingers. It arrived in Unova folklore alongside UFO sightings near Route 14, where trainers report telepathic greetings before the creature vanishes into crop circles.

Scholars debate whether Elgyem is extraterrestrial or a terrestrial species that evolved mimicry of “visitor” myths. Either way, it stands as Unova’s most discussed psychic enigma—curious, not malevolent, and obsessed with human technology.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Elgyem’s fingers flash in binary-like patterns, allegedly downloading memories from nearby devices. Pokédex entries describe it controlling minds with a steady gaze, though field studies show it prefers swapping short-term memories for harmless pranks.

Flocks gather at radio towers during solar storms, synchronizing finger pulses that disrupt reception for minutes—a behavior mistaken for hacking attacks in rural news reports.

## Battle Performance

Elgyem learns Telepathy and Analytic, boosting damage when moving last. It can run Choice Specs with Psychic, Thunderbolt, and Signal Beam in NU tiers before evolving into Beheeyem.

Its middling Speed and bulk limit OU relevance, but Trick Room teams sometimes use Beheeyem’s superior stats; Elgyem itself shines in Little Cup with Eviolite and Thunder Wave support.
