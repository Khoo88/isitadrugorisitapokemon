## Who is Zarude?

Zarude is a Dark/Grass-type Mythical Pokémon from Generation VIII, a jungle ape with vine whips and pack loyalty introduced in the Zarude movie. It defies human technology, living in dense Galar-adjacent forests with tribal social structures.

Mythical distribution made Zarude rare in games, but lore emphasizes found-family bonds between outcast Zarude and human children—a protector of isolated ecosystems.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Zarude’s vines synthesize healing sap for packmates and weapons for intruders. Pokédex entries describe it rejecting Poké Balls instinctively, viewing capture as betrayal of the pack.

It swings tree-to-tree at 50 mph, leaving vine bridges for smaller Pokémon to flee fires.

## Battle Performance

Zarude is a fast physical attacker with Jungle Healing support, Power Whip, Knock Off, and Close Combat. Choice Band and Life Orb sets wallbreak in UU when Mythicals allowed.

Grass/Dark typing is awkward defensively; Bug, Fairy, Ice, Fire, and Fighting hurt. Jungle Healing grants rare team recovery in formats permitting Mythicals.
