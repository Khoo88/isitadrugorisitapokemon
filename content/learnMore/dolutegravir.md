## What is Dolutegravir?

Dolutegravir is an integrase strand transfer inhibitor (INSTI) that blocks HIV integrase, preventing viral DNA integration into host chromosomal DNA. High barrier to resistance and potent viral suppression characterize the class; dolutegravir has a long half-life allowing once-daily dosing.

Metabolism involves UGT1A1 glucuronidation with some CYP3A contribution. It is available alone or in fixed combinations (e.g., with abacavir/lamivudine or emtricitabine/tenofovir alafenamide). Rapid viral load decline occurs in treatment-naïve patients.

Neural tube defect signal in Botswana cohort led to caution in conception; updated guidance balances risks with benefits of viral suppression.

## Primary Uses

- HIV-1 infection in treatment-naïve and experienced patients (as part of combination antiretroviral therapy)
- Post-exposure prophylaxis (selected regimens, per CDC)
- Component of single-tablet regimens (Triumeq, Dovato, Biktarvy)

Requires resistance testing in treatment-experienced patients with prior integrase inhibitor exposure.

## Common Side Effects

- Generally well tolerated
- Insomnia, headache
- Fatigue
- Gastrointestinal upset (nausea, diarrhea—mild)
- Hypersensitivity reactions (rare, with abacavir-containing combos)
- Weight gain (class observation with INSTIs)
- **Warnings:** Test for hepatitis B when combined with tenofovir/emtricitabine; drug interactions via UGT and renal transporters (metformin dose adjustment sometimes needed); pregnancy planning counseling per current guidelines.
