## Who is Skrelp?

Skrelp is a Poison/Water-type Pokémon from Generation VI, evolving into Dragalge. It disguises itself as seaweed to ambush prey in Kalos reefs, a humble predator that becomes a toxic dragon kelp lord upon evolution.

Divers learn to spot Skrelp by tail twitching—stillness otherwise perfect. It represents the poison-water continuum in coastal ecosystems contested by Clauncher and Mareanie lines.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Skrelp’s camouflage includes adopting rotten kelp scent to avoid predators. Pokédex entries describe it spraying poison when touched, turning clear bays murky in seconds.

Juveniles ride currents clinging to driftwood, colonizing new reefs until Dragalge maturity lets them claim territory with acidic secretions.

## Battle Performance

Skrelp can run defensive Eviolite sets in Little Cup with Poison Point and Sludge Bomb. Competitive relevance peaks at Dragalge with Adaptability-boosted attacks.

As Skrelp, it lacks power for higher tiers; Toxic Spikes and Dragon Tail appear only after evolution. LC players use its typing to wall other waters.
