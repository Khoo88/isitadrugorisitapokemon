## What is Meloxicam?

Meloxicam is an enolic acid NSAID that preferentially inhibits cyclooxygenase-2 (COX-2) over COX-1 at low doses, aiming for anti-inflammatory and analgesic effects with relatively less gastrointestinal platelet inhibition than nonselective NSAIDs—though COX-1 inhibition still occurs at higher doses.

It is used once daily for osteoarthritis and rheumatoid arthritis. All NSAIDs carry cardiovascular and renal risks; meloxicam is not risk-free for GI bleeding, especially in elderly patients or those on anticoagulants.

Highly protein bound; hepatic metabolism via CYP2C9 and CYP3A4; active metabolites excreted renally.

## Primary Uses

- Osteoarthritis
- Rheumatoid arthritis
- Juvenile rheumatoid arthritis (selected ages, per labeling)

Shortest effective duration is recommended per osteoarthritis guidelines.

## Common Side Effects

- Dyspepsia, nausea, diarrhea
- GI ulceration and bleeding
- Edema, hypertension
- Renal impairment
- Rash
- Cardiovascular thrombotic events (chronic use, class effect)
- **Warnings:** Avoid in active GI bleed, advanced renal disease, high cardiovascular risk when alternatives exist; caution with anticoagulants and corticosteroids; lowest dose for shortest time.
