## Who is Dracovish?

Dracovish is a Water/Dragon-type fossil Pokémon from Generation VIII, combining a fish head with a raptor-like body—another “impossible” Galar reconstruction. Despite absurd biology, it dominated competitive scenes with Fishious Rend.

Circhester paleontologists use Dracovish as a teaching tool about ecological mismatch, while popular media embraced it as a triumphant underdog monster.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Pokédex entries claim Dracovish’s legs outlasted its aquatic metabolism, forcing it to beach itself to hunt—then strike prey with powerful jaw snaps. The contradiction fuels comedy and horror alike.

Coastal fossils show bite marks inconsistent with known species, hinting ancient predators may have resembled Dracovish more than modern science admits.

## Battle Performance

Dracovish’s Fishious Rend doubles damage when moving first, paired with Strong Jaw and Choice Scarf or Sand Rush in rain/sand. It was banned or restricted in many formats at its peak.

Defensively awkward, it relies on sheer damage output. Without speed control, faster threats revenge-kill easily—yet at its height, few outspeed under rain.
