## Who is Salazzle?

Salazzle is a Poison/Fire-type Pokémon from Generation VII, evolving only from female Salandit. Its toxic pheromone control over male harems made it infamous in Alola for gender politics and battlefield Nasty Plot sweeping alike.

Volcano cave societies revolve around Salazzle queens, with males competing for approval—a rare matriarchal structure celebrated and feared in trainer documentaries.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Salazzle emits gas that enslaves Salandit males chemically, coordinating hunts with eerie precision. Pokédex entries describe flames mixed with poison, burning prey from inside after inhalation.

Its tail flame color indicates toxicity level; jewelers once prized shed scales until poisoning incidents ended the trade.

## Battle Performance

Salazzle is a fast special attacker with Corrosion ignoring Steel immunity to poison, Nasty Plot, Flamethrower, and Sludge Wave. Focus Sash leads set up against slower teams.

Frail but deadly after setup; priority and Psychic types revenge kill. It dominated early Gen VII NU and remains a potent wallbreaker with Encore support options.
