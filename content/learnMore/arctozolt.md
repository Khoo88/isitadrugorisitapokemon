## Who is Arctozolt?

Arctozolt is an Electric/Ice-type fossil Pokémon from Generation VIII, combining a furry prehistoric hindquarters with a bolt-generating avian front half. Like other Galar fossils, it may never have existed as a cohesive species—yet it thrives when revived through advanced technology.

Galarian lore frames Arctozolt as a “mistake that learned to run,” gaining mythic status in snowbound routes where its sparks illuminate blizzards. Trainers find it simultaneously comical and intimidating.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Pokédex entries describe Arctozolt storing static in its plumage while its tail acts as a grounding counterweight—an unstable pairing that causes it to stumble, then discharge electricity in bursts. This clumsy gait allegedly confuses predators.

In alpine caves, revived Arctozolt have been observed leaving frozen footprints charged with ions, briefly stunning smaller Pokémon. Biologists study it to understand how separate extinct lineages might hybridize under laboratory conditions.

## Battle Performance

Arctozolt is a hail-speed sweeper with Slush Rush, Bolt Beak, and Icicle Crash. When hail is active, it outspeeds many threats and deals doubled damage with Bolt Beak if moving first.

Its fragility and reliance on weather keep it niche, but on dedicated hail teams it can dismantish unprepared offenses. Low Kick and Superpower coverage address Steel-types that wall its STAB moves.
