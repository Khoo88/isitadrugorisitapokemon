## Who is Armaldo?

Armaldo is a Rock/Bug-type Pokémon from Generation III, the evolved form of Anorith restored from the Claw Fossil. It represents a prehistoric apex predator that transitioned from aquatic hunter to shoreline raider, wielding twin claws like geological chisels.

Hoenn lore positions Armaldo as a relic species studied alongside Cradily—one aggressive and mobile, the other sessile and photosynthetic. Together they illustrate ancient reef ecosystems recreated in modern battle facilities.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Pokédex entries claim Armaldo can curl into a wheel-like shape to chase prey across tidal flats, then extend steel-hard claws to pierce shells. Its armored carapace sheds annually, producing fragments mistaken for native rock strata.

Some fossil beds show claw marks on granite outcrops, fueling theories that Armaldo once scaled cliffs to raid bird Pokémon nests. Revived specimens remain fiercely territorial in containment ponds.

## Battle Performance

Armaldo is a physical attacker with Swift Swim for rain teams or Battle Armor to block critical hits. It learns Stone Edge, X-Scissor, Earthquake, and Aqua Jet, offering strong coverage when weather or support enables it.

Low Speed limits its tier, but under rain it can outspeed and sweep. Weakness to Water, Steel, and Rock in the meta keep it niche, though Swords Dance sets can break dedicated walls.
