## Who is Genesect?

Genesect is a Bug/Steel-type Mythical Pokémon from Generation V, revived from a 300-million-year-old fossil and modified by Team Plasma with a cannon on its back. It bridges paleontology and cyberpunk warfare in Unovan myth.

Plasma’s goal to create the ultimate battling machine backfired ethically, making Genesect a symbol of scientific hubris. Its universe standing is that of a weaponized relic seeking its lost forest home in films and side stories.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Genesect’s drive cannons fire Techno Blast typed by held discs, a fusion of ancient chitin and modern alloy. Pokédex entries describe it as the apex hunter of prehistoric forests, now confused by industrial skies.

Fossil beds show mass extinction layers matching Genesect migration patterns, hinting its return may have ecological consequences if colonies reawaken.

## Battle Performance

Genesect is a fast pivot with Download boosting Attack or Special Attack, U-turn, Ice Beam, Flamethrower, and Techno Blast. Choice Band and Special variants wallbreak.

Its movepool and typing made it banned in many formats. Fire-resistant steels and priority users check it, but momentum loss from constant U-turn pressure defines matches it enters.
