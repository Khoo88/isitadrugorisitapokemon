## Who is Heliolisk?

Heliolisk is an Electric/Normal-type Pokémon from Generation VI, evolving from Helioptile. Its frilled collar converts sunlight into lethal voltage, evoking desert lizards scaled up for Kalos solar farms and Route 9 racing folklore.

Engineers partner with Heliolisk at power plants as living regulators, though wild specimens sprint on hind legs faster than Olympic sprinters—a blur of yellow and sass.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Heliolisk can generate enough electricity to power a skyscraper for a day after basking. Pokédex entries describe its frills as solar panels that also signal mates via color shifts invisible to humans.

When storms approach, colonies discharge excess energy into sand, creating fulgurite sculptures sold as art illegally.

## Battle Performance

Heliolisk hits hard with Dry Skin or Sand Rush synergy in weather, Thunderbolt, Hyper Voice, and Volt Switch. High Special Attack and Speed make it a UU special attacker.

Fragile defenses require careful switching. Choice Scarf sets outspeed sweeper metas; Life Orb variants wallbreak with minimal setup.
