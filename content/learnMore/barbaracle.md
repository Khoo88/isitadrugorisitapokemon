## Who is Barbaracle?

Barbaracle is a Rock/Water-type Pokémon from Generation VI, evolving from Binacle when multiple symbiotic individuals fuse into one coordinated body. Its design evokes barnacles turned into a humanoid collective—a striking image on Kalos and Alola coastlines.

Coastal lore describes Barbaracle as a “committee predator,” where each limb-thinker votes on when to strike. Trainers find it aggressive but fascinating, embodying emergent intelligence from simple organisms.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Each Barbaracle limb can detach briefly to scout crevices, reattaching via calcified cement. Pokédex entries note they share a nervous network through the central core, allowing synchronized ambushes on Crustacean prey.

Tidal pools with unusually smooth stone often hide Barbaracle gardens—colonies that look like rock until prey steps near.

## Battle Performance

Barbaracle’s Tough Claws ability boosts contact moves like Razor Shell and Stone Edge. It can run Shell Smash sets for sweeping or Choice Band physical offense with Liquidation coverage.

Speed remains mediocre even after boosts, and Grass/Electric/Fighting weaknesses hurt. Still, it can break stall teams when supported with rain or Sticky Web.
