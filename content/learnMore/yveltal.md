## Who is Yveltal?

Yveltal is a Dark/Flying-type Legendary Pokémon from Generation VI, the Destruction Pokémon whose wings absorb life energy when it awakens. Kalos legend says it turns forests to ash, sleeping in cocoon form after centuries of devastation.

Its rivalry with Xerneas frames the franchise’s life-death duality in Gen VI, with Yveltal embodying necessary entropy—not evil, but cosmic clearance of overgrown order.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Yveltal’s Oblivion Wing drains vitality into red aura visible from orbit in myth. Pokédex entries describe its cocoon as regenerative hibernation after absorbing enough life force from regions.

Where it roosts, plants wither in spirals matching wing patterns—geographers map these as warning zones in Kalos atlases.

## Battle Performance

Yveltal is a Ubers staple with Dark Aura, Oblivion Wing recovery, and Sucker Punch. Foul Play punishes setup; Choice Scarf sets revenge kill.

It tanks special hits well and checks Ghost types. Fairy and Rock attacks threaten it, but offensive pressure and longevity make it a defining restricted Pokémon.
