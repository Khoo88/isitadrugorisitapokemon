## What is Paxlovid?

Paxlovid is a co-packaged antiviral regimen combining nirmatrelvir, a SARS-CoV-2 main protease (3CLpro) inhibitor, with ritonavir, a strong CYP3A4 inhibitor included as a pharmacokinetic booster. Nirmatrelvir prevents cleavage of viral polyproteins required for replication; ritonavir slows nirmatrelvir metabolism, maintaining therapeutic plasma concentrations.

Treatment must begin within 5 days of symptom onset in outpatients with mild to moderate COVID-19 who are at high risk for progression to severe disease. Ritonavir component causes extensive drug-drug interactions requiring medication reconciliation and often temporary discontinuation of interacting drugs.

Renal dose adjustment is required for moderate renal impairment; not recommended in severe renal failure per labeling.

## Primary Uses

- Treatment of mild to moderate COVID-19 in high-risk outpatients within 5 days of symptom onset
- Reducing hospitalization and death in eligible patients per current FDA authorization/approval and CDC guidance

Not indicated for hospitalized patients requiring supplemental oxygen for COVID-19 (limited benefit in late disease) or pre-exposure prophylaxis as standard care.

## Common Side Effects

- Dysgeusia (metallic or bitter taste—very common)
- Diarrhea, nausea
- Hypertension
- **Warnings:** Major CYP3A4 interactions (statins, immunosuppressants, amiodarone, some anticoagulants); hepatic impairment caution; HIV patients on certain regimens need specialist adjustment; rebound symptoms reported rarely after completion.
