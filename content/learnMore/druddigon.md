## Who is Druddigon?

Druddigon is a Dragon-type Pokémon from Generation V, inhabiting Unova’s Mistralton Cave. Its rough-hewn stone appearance and red-head mohawk evoke a gargoyle carved by tectonic forces rather than born from eggs traditionally.

Unovan miners consider Druddigon a territorial spirit of ore veins—harmless if respected, ferocious if pickaxes strike nest walls. Its design celebrates raw, non-serpentine dragon diversity.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Druddigon absorbs sunlight through facial crystals, warming its blood before activity. Pokédex entries describe it painting cave murals with pigment from oxidized metals—a behavior linking art and geology.

It hibernates in geodes, waking when gems form nearby, suggesting sensitivity to crystal growth vibrations.

## Battle Performance

Druddigon has Mold Breaker, Rough Skin chip, and access to Dragon Claw, Earthquake, and Glare. It can set Stealth Rock with superior typing versus Fairies compared to other dragons.

Very slow and vulnerable to Ice and Fairy attacks, it fits niche roles as a hazard setter or tank in lower tiers. Gunk Shot coverage punishes Fairy-types.
