## Who is Kartana?

Kartana is a Grass/Steel-type Ultra Beast from Generation VII, codename UB-04 Blade. It resembles a living origami samurai, slicing steel skyscrapers in Poni Meadow reports with absurd cutting prowess despite tiny mass.

Ultra Beast research classifies Kartana as paper-thin yet harder than diamond—a contradiction that fascinates materials scientists and terrorizes Alolan construction unions.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Kartana’s body is folded cellulose sharper than observatory lenses. Pokédex entries describe it cleaving opponents in single strokes, leaving clean cross-sections as if laser-cut.

Wind can blow it off course—a vulnerability hidden by offensive speed that makes it seem unstoppable in battle footage.

## Battle Performance

Kartana is an OU and Ubers staple with Beast Boost, Leaf Blade, Smart Strike, and Swords Dance. Choice Scarf sets revenge kill; sash variants lead.

Paper-thin defenses mean any Fire or Fighting hit OHKOs, but only if you survive its +2 Attack sweep. It defines hyper offense in many Gen VII formats.
