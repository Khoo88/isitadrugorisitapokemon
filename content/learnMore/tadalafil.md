## What is Tadalafil?

Tadalafil is a long-acting phosphodiesterase type 5 (PDE5) inhibitor that increases cyclic GMP in vascular and smooth muscle, producing vasodilation. Long half-life (approximately 17 hours) allows daily low-dose use for erectile dysfunction and benign prostatic hyperplasia urinary symptoms, or on-demand dosing at higher strength.

PDE11 inhibition may contribute to back pain and myalgia more than with sildenafil. Metabolism primarily via CYP3A4.

Nitrates are absolutely contraindicated due to severe hypotension risk.

## Primary Uses

- Erectile dysfunction (on-demand or daily)
- Benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH) lower urinary tract symptoms (daily Cialis labeling)
- Pulmonary arterial hypertension (Adcirca dosing)
- BPH and ED coexisting (daily tadalafil)

## Common Side Effects

- Headache
- Dyspepsia, back pain, myalgia
- Flushing, nasal congestion
- Hypotension
- Priapism (rare)
- **Warnings:** Contraindicated with nitrates and guanylate cyclase stimulators (riociguat); caution with alpha-blockers for BPH (orthostatic hypotension); non-arteritic optic neuropathy and sudden hearing loss (rare class effects); max frequency guidance for on-demand dosing.
