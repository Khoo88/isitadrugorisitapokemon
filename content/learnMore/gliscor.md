## Who is Gliscor?

Gliscor is a Ground/Flying-type Pokémon from Generation IV, evolving from Gligar. It glides on night winds with vampire-adjacent fangs, staking out Sinnoh cliffs and desert mesas as an apex aerial ambusher.

Its Poison Heal stall archetype made it legendary in competitive circles, while Pokédex horror entries describe it waiting silently for prey to weaken before striking—a patient sky predator.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Gliscor’s tail is a balancing rudder coated in barbs; it produces a soundless glide by editing wing membrane tension. Pokédex entries note it clips prey with tail pincers, never needing to land during hunts.

It licks minerals from rock faces with acidic saliva, hardening its hide—a grooming ritual mistaken for blood drinking in folk tales.

## Battle Performance

Gliscor with Poison Heal, Toxic Orb, Roost, and Protect is a stall icon, gaining recovery each turn while Toxic wears foes down. Earthquake, Facade, and Knock Off provide pressure.

Immune to Electric with Ground/Flying typing, weak to Ice. Swords Dance variants surprise; it defined BW OU stall and remains viable with Taunt and Stealth Rock support sets.
