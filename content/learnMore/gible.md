## Who is Gible?

Gible is a Dragon/Ground-type Pokémon from Generation IV, the first stage of the Garchomp line. It burrows in sinkholes, occasionally biting steel towers mistaken for prey—a comic yet destructive habit that put Wayward Cave on travel advisories.

Sinnoh children’s tales warn of “land sharks” that eat anything shiny. Gible’s goofy appetite masks the lineage that would define offensive metagames for a generation.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Gible’s jaw can crush boulders, yet it often trips while running due to short legs. Pokédex entries describe it forming packs that accidentally undermine roads, requiring League relocation programs.

It hoards round objects in nests, rolling them like toys before evolution teaches gem discipline in Gabite stage.

## Battle Performance

Gible is terrifying in Little Cup with Rough Skin chip, Dragon Claw, and Rock Tomb under Eviolite. Sand Veil variants historically annoyed opponents in sand teams.

Standard progression rushes to Garchomp; Gible alone defines LC dragon dominance with stats far above peers when evolved forms are banned.
