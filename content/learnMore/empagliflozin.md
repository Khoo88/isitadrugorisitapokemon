## What is Empagliflozin?

Empagliflozin is a sodium-glucose cotransporter 2 (SGLT2) inhibitor that blocks reabsorption of filtered glucose in the proximal renal tubule, producing glucosuria and lowering plasma glucose independent of insulin secretion. Caloric loss and osmotic diuresis contribute to modest weight and blood pressure reduction.

Cardiovascular outcome trials demonstrated reduced heart failure hospitalization and renal endpoints in patients with type 2 diabetes and established cardiovascular disease; benefits extend to heart failure with reduced ejection fraction even without diabetes in dedicated trials.

Increased genital mycotic infections result from glycosuria. Euglycemic diabetic ketoacidosis is a rare but serious risk, especially perioperatively or during illness.

## Primary Uses

- Type 2 diabetes mellitus (glycemic control and cardiovascular/renal risk reduction)
- Heart failure with reduced ejection fraction (guideline-directed therapy, with or without diabetes)
- Chronic kidney disease in diabetic and selected nondiabetic populations (per labeling and guidelines)

Often combined with metformin or other agents when additional therapy is needed.

## Common Side Effects

- Genital yeast infections (vulvovaginal candidiasis, balanitis)
- Urinary tract infections
- Increased urination, polyuria
- Hypotension, dehydration
- Weight loss
- **Warnings:** Risk of ketoacidosis (monitor ketones if ill); hold before surgery per protocols; necrotizing fasciitis of perineum (Fournier gangrene—rare); monitor renal function; avoid in type 1 diabetes (off-label use requires specialist oversight).
