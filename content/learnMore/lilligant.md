## Who is Lilligant?

Lilligant is a Grass-type Pokémon from Generation V, evolving from Petilil. Unovan nobility held Petilil-to-Lilligant evolution as a rite of passage, crowning dancers with flower tiaras that never wilt in loyal hands.

Its elegant design and Sleep Powder strategies made it a singles annoyance and doubles supporter. Hisuian Lilligant adds Fighting flair in later lore, but Unovan form remains the grass dancer icon.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Lilligant’s petals release aroma that pacifies foes during ritual dances. Pokédex entries claim only the most graceful trainers evolve Petilil successfully—others produce wilted forms in superstition, not biology.

It leads Sawsbuck herds through spring migrations, pollen trails marking safe paths across seasons.

## Battle Performance

Lilligant uses Chlorophyll Sleep Powder in sun, Quiver Dance, and Solar Beam. Own Tempo prevents confusion from Outrage after Petilil stage.

Hisuian form gets Victory Dance and Fighting coverage; standard Lilligant is a sun sweeper fragile to Fire and Flying. Competitive viability shifts by format generation.
