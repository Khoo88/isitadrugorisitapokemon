## Who is Magearna?

Magearna is a Steel/Fairy-type Mythical Pokémon from Generation VI, a soul-hearted automaton built 500 years ago to comfort a king’s daughter. Its rabbit-ballerina design hides apocalyptic Soul-Heart cannon lore from the Volcanion film.

Kalos treats Magearna as cultural treasure—living history bridging steampunk craftsmanship and fairy energy. Its competitive Soul-Heart sweeps matched its narrative as a weapon of last resort.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Magearna’s Soul-Heart stores emotions of its creator, glowing when empathizing with suffering. Pokédex entries describe color shift between Original Forme gold and standard red when awakening true power.

It slept in Pokéballs disguised as crowns until disturbed, a fairy tale sleeping beauty with steel defenses.

## Battle Performance

Magearna is a premier Fairy Heatran-like special attacker with Soul-Heart boosts on KO, Fleur Cannon, Aura Sphere, and Shift Gear. Assault Vest and Calm Mind variants exist.

Its typing and movepool made it banned in many formats. Ground and Fire types threaten it, but +1 or +2 Special Attack sweepers end games before checks arrive.
