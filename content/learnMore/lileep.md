## Who is Lileep?

Lileep is a Rock/Grass-type fossil Pokémon from Generation III, revived from the Root Fossil’s counterpart—the Claw Fossil line shares Hoenn’s prehistoric theme with Cradily’s predator-prey dynamic as Anorith’s counterpart ecosystem.

Lileep resembles a sea lily anchored to reefs, filter-feeding in ancient oceans recreated in Rustboro labs. It embodies passive prehistoric beauty before becoming aggressive Cradily.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Lileep’s tentacles wave like flowers to lure fish, then constrict with surprising force. Pokédex entries describe it rooted for life on one rock, slowly migrating only by uprooting during typhoons.

Fossil beds show Lileep colonies forming garden reefs that sheltered smaller species—a paleo-ecological hotspot in Hoenn museum tours.

## Battle Performance

Lileep is a Little Cup staple with Eviolite, Recover, Ancient Power, and Leech Seed. Storm Drain redirects water moves even at this stage.

It evolves into Cradily’s defensive wall role. As Lileep, it stalls physical attackers and sets up for late-game wins in dedicated LC formats.
