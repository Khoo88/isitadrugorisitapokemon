## Who is Cascoon?

Cascoon is a Bug-type cocoon stage evolving from Wurmple into Dustox. Unlike Silcoon’s clean silk shell, Cascoon’s hardened purple casing anchors it to tree branches while it undergoes metamorphosis—a vulnerable yet essential forest phase.

Hoenn bug catchers study Cascoon clusters as indicators of seasonal humidity. Forest rangers protect Cascoon groves during festivals, believing harming them brings misfortune to harvests.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Cascoon hardens its shell with collected toxins from leaves, deterring predators via bitter secretions. Pokédex entries describe it using silk threads to sense vibrations, alerting nearby Dustox colonies to threats before emergence.

Some trees host dozens of Cascoon simultaneously, creating “purple bark” silhouettes that photographers flock to document each spring.

## Battle Performance

Cascoon has virtually no offensive presence, learning Harden and Poison Sting at best. Competitive play ignores this stage except in gimmick “only cocoon” challenges.

Its value is entirely narrative and evolutionary, leading to Dustox’s special attacking and status utility in lower tiers.
