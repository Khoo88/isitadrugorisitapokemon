## Who is Toxtricity?

Toxtricity is an Electric/Poison-type Pokémon from Generation VIII, with Amped and Low Key forms based on nature. Its guitar-punk aesthetic and concert-scale sound attacks embody Galar’s music culture war against silence.

Rocker gym leaders and Spikemuth tourism built economies around Toxtricity battles, where Overdrive deals damage from sound moves boosted by Punk Rock ability.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Toxtricity’s chest organ generates 15,000-volt discharges synced to strumming finger claws. Pokédex entries warn that mosh pits near wild specimens cause heart arrhythmia in humans lacking ear protection.

Aamped forms shout; Low Key forms croon—same species, different sonic weapon frequencies.

## Battle Performance

Toxtricity runs Punk Rock Boomburst or Overdrive with Shift Gear or Choice Specs. G-Max Stun Shock poisoned electric damage dominated doubles briefly.

Volt Switch pivots and Sludge Wave coverage handle Fairies. Ground types and faster sweepers revenge kill, but +1 Shift Gear cleans teams in UU.
