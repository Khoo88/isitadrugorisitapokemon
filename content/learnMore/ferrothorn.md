## Who is Ferrothorn?

Ferrothorn is a Grass/Steel-type Pokémon from Generation V, the evolved form of Ferroseed and a defining wall of competitive history. Its spiked vine tentacles and iron-laced bark made it synonymous with stall teams across generations V through VIII.

Unovan caverns treat Ferrothorn as immovable guardians—legends say they outlive the caves they inhabit, slowly migrating only when ore deposits deplete. In the wider universe, it is respected and reviled as the ultimate patient defender.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Ferrothorn anchors with retractable roots, absorbing lightning to strengthen spikes. Pokédex entries describe it swinging vine lashes at 80 mph, pinning prey until moss digests them over days.

During thunderstorms, colonies conduct electricity into the ground, preventing cave collapses—a mutual benefit miners once celebrated before overharvesting angered local populations.

## Battle Performance

Ferrothorn runs Iron Barbs with Leech Seed, Power Whip, Gyro Ball, and Spikes or Stealth Rock. Assault Vest variants check special attackers; Rocky Helmet stacks chip with Iron Barbs.

Immense physical Defense and resistances define formats, though Fire and Fighting types pressure it. It remains a benchmark for hazard-stacking stall in OU and VGC support roles.
