## Who is Carvanha?

Carvanha is a Water/Dark-type Pokémon from Generation III, evolving into Sharpedo—the “Bully of the Sea.” In packs, Carvanha are relentless scavengers and ambush hunters of river routes and coastal shallows.

Hoenn sailors tell stories of red fins circling boats, a sign to leave waters immediately. Alone, Carvanha are timid; in groups, they become famously aggressive, defining oceanic danger for early trainers.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Carvanha’s jaws can strip prey in minutes, with blood-sensitive vision that triggers feeding frenzies. Pokédex entries note they cannot sustain aggression when separated from the pack, hiding in reeds until numbers return.

Some rivers install Carvanha deterrent fields—underwater vibrations that disrupt pack coordination—protecting ferry lanes without exterminating native species.

## Battle Performance

Carvanha is a fast physical attacker in Little Cup with Rough Skin chip damage. Moves like Aqua Jet, Crunch, and Poison Fang preview Sharpedo’s offense.

Its defenses are poor, and it relies on speed tiers in LC formats. Upon evolution, Sharpedo gains Speed Boost and competitive relevance.
