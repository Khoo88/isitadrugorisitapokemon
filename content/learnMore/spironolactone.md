## What is Spironolactone?

Spironolactone is a potassium-sparing diuretic and aldosterone receptor antagonist that competes with aldosterone in the distal nephron, reducing sodium reabsorption and potassium excretion. Antiandrogenic effects from structural similarity cause gynecomastia and are exploited in acne and hirsutism treatment.

It reduces mortality in heart failure with reduced ejection fraction and treats resistant hypertension, primary hyperaldosteronism, and ascites in cirrhosis. Metabolite canrenone contributes to long duration of action.

Hyperkalemia is the principal risk, especially with ACE inhibitors, ARBs, or renal impairment.

## Primary Uses

- Heart failure with reduced ejection fraction (guideline-directed therapy)
- Resistant hypertension
- Primary hyperaldosteronism (adjunct or diagnostic)
- Cirrhotic ascites with edema
- Hypokalemia prevention on thiazides or loop diuretics
- Acne, hirsutism, female pattern hair loss (antiandrogen doses)

## Common Side Effects

- Hyperkalemia
- Gynecomastia, breast tenderness (men)
- Menstrual irregularities
- Dizziness, hyperkalemia-related arrhythmia risk
- GI upset
- **Warnings:** Monitor potassium and renal function; avoid potassium supplements and salt substitutes; contraindicated in anuria, significant hyperkalemia, and Addison disease; may cause fetal harm—contraception in women of childbearing potential.
