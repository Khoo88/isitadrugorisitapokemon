## Who is Heatran?

Heatran is a Fire/Steel-type Legendary Pokémon from Generation IV, dwelling in Stark Mountain’s magma chambers. Its cross-shaped body and molten blood made it Sinnoh’s industrial fire spirit—less creator god than apex volcanic organism.

Sinnoh miners once worshipped Heatran as forge patron until eruptions linked to its mating seasons forced relocation. It remains a restricted-format staple and geological wonder.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Heatran’s steel shell floats on magnetic fields above lava, never touching ground directly. Pokédex entries describe blood boiling like magma, venting through claws that carve tunnels.

Pairs compete for volcanic vents, their courtship raising regional temperatures measurably on weather satellites.

## Battle Performance

Heatran is a special wall with Flash Fire, Stealth Rock, Magma Storm trapping, and Earth Power. Air Balloon variants dodge Ground; Assault Vest tanks special hits.

It checks Fairy and Ice teams while threatening Steel-types. Common in VGC and Ubers as a pivot that chips and traps simultaneously.
