## Who is Ludicolo?

Ludicolo is a Water/Grass-type Pokémon from Generation III, evolving from Lombre with a Water Stone. Its sombrero and festive dance embody Hoenn rainforest carnival culture—joyful, rhythmic, and deceptively deadly in rain.

Surfers near Lilycove report Ludicolo leading rain dances that end droughts locally—a myth blending meteorology with Chlorophyll teams in competitive fan jokes.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Ludicolo’s body rhythm syncs with music, gaining energy when it hears drums. Pokédex entries describe it dancing when hearing festive songs, drawing crowds that become hunting opportunities.

Its lily pad hat stores rainwater, doubling as solar still during dry weeks for desert travelers who befriend specimens.

## Battle Performance

Ludicolo is a rain abuser with Swift Swim, Hydro Pump, Giga Drain, and Ice Beam. Special Attack doubles in rain while Grass STAB heals with Giga Drain.

Outclassed by Kingdra and Pelipper cores in modern rain, but niche mixed Ludicolo with Fake Out appears in doubles history. Weak to common special types limits OU play.
