## Who is Cofagrigus?

Cofagrigus is a Ghost-type Pokémon from Generation V, evolving from Yamask with a sarcophagus motif. It rules desert ruins as a “king of coffins,” sealing intruders in golden bands that mimic ancient burial artistry.

Unovan archaeologists treat Cofagrigus as both guardian and hazard—its presence protects tombs from looters, yet revived specimens complicate modern excavations.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Cofagrigus animates gold filaments to trap prey, shrinking them into symbolic effigies. Pokédex entries claim it devours greed, targeting thieves specifically—a poetic justice narrative popular in desert folklore.

Its body is hollow, hosting smaller spirits or Yamask apprentices learning ritual sealing techniques passed through generations.

## Battle Performance

Cofagrigus is a defensive Ghost with Mummy, spreading ability lock on contact. Moves like Shadow Ball, Nasty Plot, and Trick Room support make it a bulky setup threat in doubles.

Physical Defense is enormous, but Dark-type attackers and status wear it down. Will-O-Wisp and Pain Split extend its stalling prowess in lower tiers.
