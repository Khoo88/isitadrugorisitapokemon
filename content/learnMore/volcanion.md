## Who is Volcanion?

Volcanion is a Fire/Water-type Mythical Pokémon from Generation VI, the only Pokémon with this typing. It expels steam explosions from arms like cannons, guarding Kalos’s isolated crater where it was filmed in the Volcanion movie.

Steam burst mythology positions Volcanion as living geyser deity—rare, territorial, and capable of ending wars between human factions by flooding battlefields with scalding mist.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Volcanion’s body maintains boiling internal water without harming itself, releasing hyperheated steam that melts steel gates. Pokédex entries describe it as explosive temper when its home volcano is disturbed.

Its footprints leave mineral-rich water that heals burned soil, a restorative cycle after destructive outbursts.

## Battle Performance

Volcanion uses Water Absorb and Steam Eruption’s 30% burn chance with Fire Blast and Sludge Bomb. Assault Vest or Choice Specs sets wallbreak in UU and restricted formats.

Fire/Water typing resists Fire, Steel, and Ice; Grass and Electric threaten it. Steam Eruption’s power and accuracy make it a premier special wallbreaker when allowed.
