## Who is Tinkaton?

Tinkaton is a Fairy/Steel-type Pokémon from Generation IX, evolving from Tinkatuff. Its giant hammer and habit of bashing Corviknight mid-flight made it an instant meme and genuine Fairy/Steel tank in competitive play.

Paldean children’s stories cast Tinkaton as smith fairy forging weapons while riding social media fame for anti-bird violence in Pokédex flavor text.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Tinkaton collects scrap metal to forge its hammer, which can exceed its body weight. Pokédex entries describe it swinging 100 times daily to maintain strength, occasionally flattening roadside signs.

It builds nests from stolen gears, creating clockwork playgrounds that confuse engineers inspecting power lines.

## Battle Performance

Tinkaton has Pickpocket, Knock Off, Gigaton Hammer, and Stealth Rock. Huge HP and solid typing provide OU defensive presence with Encore and Thunder Wave support.

Gigaton Hammer cannot be used twice in a row, requiring move sequencing. It checks dragons and dark types while threatening setup with Knock Off removal.
