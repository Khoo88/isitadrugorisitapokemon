## Who is Blacephalon?

Blacephalon is a Fire/Ghost-type Ultra Beast introduced in Generation VII, codenamed “Fireworks.” Its clown-like silhouette and detachable explosive head embody invasive species from another dimension—beautiful, unsettling, and destructive.

The Aether Foundation classified Blacephalon as a organism that turns celebration into predation, luring prey with light displays before detonating. Alolan media sensationalized it as a walking festival hazard.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Blacephalon’s head can separate briefly to pursue fleeing targets, trailing sparks that burn chemical trails in the air. Pokédex entries describe its body as hollow, storing volatile gases ignited through percussion strikes like drumming on its own torso.

Witnesses report synchronized light shows near Blacephalon sightings—possibly mating displays, though reproduction in our dimension remains unobserved.

## Battle Performance

Blacephalon is a glass cannon special attacker with Beast Boost raising its best stat after KOs. Moves like Fire Blast, Shadow Ball, and Trick make it a devastating late-game cleaner on hyper offense teams.

Its frail defenses and Stealth Rock weakness demand careful timing. Choice Scarf sets exploit its high Special Attack and Speed to revenge-kill weakened teams.
