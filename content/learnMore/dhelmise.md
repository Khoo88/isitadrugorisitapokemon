## Who is Dhelmise?

Dhelmise is a Ghost/Grass-type Pokémon from Generation VII, resembling an anchor wrapped in kelp possessed by vengeful ocean spirits. It haunts shipwrecks off Alola, blending nautical horror with seaweed ecology.

Sailors’ logs describe Dhelmise as both protector and predator—guiding vessels through reefs by day, dragging rusted hulls deeper by night. Its design anchors Alola’s ghost-type maritime myths.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Dhelmise’s kelp strands regenerate from ocean nutrients, anchoring to rusted metal for camouflage. Pokédex entries say it swings its heavy core like a pendulum, smashing prey against reef rocks before absorbing released spirits.

Ship graveyards with dense Dhelmise populations become no-dive zones, marked by swirling kelp patterns visible from aerial drones.

## Battle Performance

Dhelmise hits enormously hard with Steelworker-boosted Anchor Shot and Power Whip, trapping opponents. Assault Vest or Choice Band sets leverage high Attack, while Leech Seed variants stall.

Its Speed is slow, and Fire/Ice/Flying weaknesses hurt. Trick Room teams appreciate its damage output, and Anchor Shot punishes defensive pivots.
