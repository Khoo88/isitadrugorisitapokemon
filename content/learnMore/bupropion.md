## What is Bupropion?

Bupropion is an atypical antidepressant that inhibits reuptake of norepinephrine and dopamine (NDRI) without significant serotonin reuptake inhibition at therapeutic doses. Exact antidepressant mechanism involves downstream effects on noradrenergic and dopaminergic neurotransmission and possible nicotinic receptor modulation relevant to smoking cessation.

It is metabolized to active metabolites (hydroxybupropion) via CYP2B6. The drug lowers seizure threshold, especially at high doses or in predisposed individuals. Extended-release formulations allow once-daily dosing and smoother plasma levels.

Unlike many antidepressants, bupropion is not associated with sexual dysfunction or weight gain and may promote modest weight loss; it is contraindicated in eating disorders with seizure risk.

## Primary Uses

- Major depressive disorder
- Seasonal affective disorder (extended-release)
- Smoking cessation aid
- Attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder (off-label in adults, per specialist guidance)

Sometimes added to SSRIs for residual fatigue or sexual side effects (augmentation).

## Common Side Effects

- Insomnia, agitation, anxiety
- Dry mouth
- Headache
- Nausea
- Weight loss or decreased appetite
- Tinnitus (uncommon)
- Seizure (dose-related, ~0.1–0.4% at therapeutic doses; higher with overdose)
- Hypertension (especially with nicotine replacement or in smoking cessation)
- **Warnings:** Contraindicated in seizure disorders, bulimia, anorexia nervosa, abrupt alcohol/benzodiazepine withdrawal; do not exceed maximum daily dose; may interact with MAO inhibitors; psychiatric worsening/suicidality class warning for antidepressants.
