## Who is Glalie?

Glalie is an Ice-type Pokémon from Generation III, evolving from Snorunt (alternate to Froslass). Its hockey-mask face and ability to freeze its body into an ice shell earned it the title “Face Pokémon” in Hoenn’s northern routes.

Hoenn folklore calls Glalie a winter spirit that rolls through blizzards without melting. Mega Glalie’s jaw cannon cemented its reputation as explosive offense wrapped in grim design.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Glalie can suspend its digestive processes while frozen solid, entering pseudo-hibernation. Pokédex entries claim it never rolls its eyes, instead using horn vibrations to sense prey through ice sheets.

It devours prey whole by unhinging its jaw—a trait exaggerated in Mega form where thermal energy builds catastrophic pressure.

## Battle Performance

Glalie gets Inner Focus or Ice Body, with Spikes and Frost Breath for crit-happy sets. Mega Glalie packs Refrigerate-boosted Hyper Voice and explosion-like Special Attack.

Standard Glalie is niche in NU; Mega was a wallbreaker in ORAS. Without mega, it mainly serves novelty Sheer Cold or Taunt roles with mediocre Speed.
