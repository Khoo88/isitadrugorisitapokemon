## Who is Xerneas?

Xerneas is a Fairy-type Legendary Pokémon from Generation VI, the Life Pokémon whose antlers shine with eternal rainbow light. Kalos myth pairs it with Yveltal in cycles of creation and destruction, sleeping as a tree for a thousand years between awakenings.

Its geomancy pose in battle mirrors druidic rituals across Kalos, where forests regrow overnight after Xerneas sightings—a symbol of hope against Yveltal’s entropy.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Xerneas shares life energy by touching horns to dying species, reviving ecosystems at cost of its own mobility afterward. Pokédex entries describe its tree form as dormant conservation mode, roots linking entire woodlands.

Feathers shed during battle glow for decades, used in illegal rejuvenation tonics hunted by Rangers.

## Battle Performance

Xerneas is an Ubers titan with Fairy Aura-boosted Geomancy, Moonblast, and Thunder. One Geomancy often ends games, boosting Special Attack, Special Defense, and Speed.

Steel and Poison types resist Fairy, but few survive +2 Moonblast. It defines restricted formats whenever allowed, with Precipice Blades checks in dual restricted.
