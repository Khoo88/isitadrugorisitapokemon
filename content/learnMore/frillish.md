## Who is Frillish?

Frillish is a Water/Ghost-type Pokémon from Generation V, evolving into Jellicent. Its frilled bell shape and gender dimorphism evoke Victorian jellyfish fashion, haunting Unova coastlines with polite menace.

Sailors distinguish blue “princes” and pink “princesses” in folklore, each luring ships differently—one with storms, one with mirages. Frillish introduced the ghostly ocean lord archetype that Jellicent would later dominate competitively.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Frillish’s veil is 80% water, stinging prey with diluted toxins. Pokédex entries claim it sinks ships that pollute its habitat, though researchers attribute losses to rogue currents paired with opportunistic feeding.

Juveniles practice camouflage by floating motionless beside buoys, startling dockworkers when they blink visible eyes.

## Battle Performance

Frillish can run defensive Water Absorb sets in Little Cup with Will-O-Wisp and Night Shade. Cursed Body threatens lock on switch-ins.

Most competitive value appears in Jellicent with Water Absorb, Strength Sap, and blocking. Frillish itself is a stepping-stone with solid special bulk on Eviolite.
