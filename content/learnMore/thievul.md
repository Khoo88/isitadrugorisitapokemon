## Who is Thievul?

Thievul is a Dark-type Pokémon from Generation VIII, evolving from Nickit. Its masked fox design and Stakeout ability made it Galar’s premier thief archetype—stealing from Team Yell and trainers alike in route comedy and competitive punishes.

Galarian folk tales warn campers to hide berries at night; Thievul tracks scent trails for miles, returning loot to dens as status symbols.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Thievul’s tail brush wipes footprints while its mask filters scent particles. Pokédex entries describe it taunting victims after theft, a behavioral cruelty rare among Pokémon.

Pairs bond by exchanging stolen goods; the largest hoard wins leadership of urban alley packs near Motostoke.

## Battle Performance

Thievul uses Stakeout to double damage on switched-in foes, with Knock Off, U-turn, and Nasty Plot special sets. Faster than many tanks, slower than top sweepers.

It occupies NU as a pivot and trap punisher. Libero-less Dark coverage is valuable, but low bulk limits higher-tier play without Stakeout mind games.
