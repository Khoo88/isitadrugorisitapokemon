## Who is Nihilego?

Nihilego is a Rock/Poison-type Ultra Beast from Generation VII, codename UB-01 Symbiont. It resembles a jellyfish made of glass and toxin, drifting through Wormholes to possess trainers and Pokémon with obsessive behavior in canon stories.

Aether Paradise incidents cemented Nihilego as psychological hazard as much as physical—an alien parasite aesthetic that unsettled audiences and competitive players alike with its special bulk and Speed.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Nihilego’s tentacles inject toxins that amplify neurotransmitters, creating addiction-like loyalty in hosts. Pokédex entries describe it as beautiful yet deadly, floating silently until prey touches its stingers.

Beachcombers find translucent fronds washed ashore after storms—samples that hum faintly, prompting quarantine protocols across Alolan islands.

## Battle Performance

Nihilego is a fast special tank with Beast Boost, Power Gem, Sludge Wave, and Grass Knot. It often runs Choice Scarf or Assault Vest, punishing setup with Toxic Spikes on switch-in sets.

Its 4x weakness to Ground is fatal, but otherwise it pressures teams with mixed coverage and snowballing Speed or offenses after KOs in Ubers and high-level VGC.
