## Who is Beldum?

Beldum is a Steel/Psychic-type Pokémon introduced in Generation III, famous for having only one natural move—Take Down—until it evolves into Metang and Metagross. Its levitating, eyeless magnet creature design evokes alien technology embedded in meteor craters.

Hoenn’s Mossdeep narratives link Beldum swarms to meteor showers, suggesting extraterrestrial origin or mineral-rich impact sites. Trainers describe raising Beldum as training a living compass that communicates through magnetic pulses.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Beldum propels itself via magnetic repulsion, consuming iron filings and ore dust for growth. Pokédex entries claim groups align into geometric lattices at night, possibly downloading geomagnetic maps of the region.

Some scientists propose Beldum colonies “remember” ore vein locations across generations, guiding human mining operations—ethically debated in modern regulation.

## Battle Performance

Beldum is iconic in Little Cup for spamming Take Down with Clear Body preventing stat drops, or holding Choice Scarf for reckless early damage. Its absurd movepool limitation defines gimmick sets.

Upon evolving, Metagross becomes a meta-defining Pokémon; Beldum itself is a novelty unless formats restrict levels to its stage.
