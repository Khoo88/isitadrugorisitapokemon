## Who is Cranidos?

Cranidos is a Rock-type fossil Pokémon from Generation IV, revived from the Skull Fossil in Sinnoh. It represents a headbutting ceratopsian predator—aggressive, thick-skulled, and emblematic of ancient competitive ramming displays.

Oreburgh Mining Museum exhibits Cranidos charging holograms, teaching visitors about paleo-ecology where only the hardest skulls survived mating contests.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Cranidos lacks defensive instinct, charging until knocked down—a behavior revived specimens retain. Pokédex entries describe skulls denser than steel, capable of shattering boulders to reach lichen.

Fossil beds show healed fractures, suggesting frequent duels. Juveniles practice on hollow logs, producing rhythmic echoes mistaken for mining blasts.

## Battle Performance

Cranidos is a premier Little Cup physical attacker with Rock Head preventing recoil on Head Smash. Sky-high Attack and moves like Zen Headbutt make it a glass cannon.

It evolves into Rampardos, carrying the same philosophy at higher power tiers. As Cranidos, speed control and priority moves are its main checks.
