## What is Rosuvastatin?

Rosuvastatin is a synthetic hydrophilic HMG-CoA reductase inhibitor with high potency for LDL cholesterol reduction at low doses. Minimal CYP metabolism (some CYP2C9) reduces certain drug interactions compared with lipophilic statins, though renal excretion of unchanged drug requires caution in advanced kidney disease.

It upregulates hepatic LDL receptors, lowering atherogenic lipoproteins and stabilizing plaques in cardiovascular disease. High-intensity rosuvastatin is guideline-recommended for secondary prevention in many patients with ASCVD.

Asian populations may have higher plasma levels at a given dose; lower starting doses are recommended in labeling for some groups.

## Primary Uses

- Primary hyperlipidemia and mixed dyslipidemia
- Primary and secondary prevention of atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease
- Familial hypercholesterolemia (heterozygous and homozygous, high-dose)
- Hypertriglyceridemia (modest effect; not first-line for severe hypertriglyceridemia)

## Common Side Effects

- Myalgia, muscle weakness
- Rhabdomyolysis (rare)
- Elevated hepatic transaminases
- Headache, nausea
- Proteinuria at high doses (historical monitoring)
- New-onset diabetes (class effect with long-term use)
- **Warnings:** Avoid in active liver disease; pregnancy contraindicated; adjust dose in severe renal impairment; cyclosporine increases exposure significantly.
