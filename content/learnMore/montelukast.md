## What is Montelukast?

Montelukast is a leukotriene receptor antagonist (LTRA) that blocks cysteinyl leukotriene receptors (CysLT1) in airways, reducing bronchoconstriction, mucus secretion, and eosinophilic inflammation triggered by allergens and exercise. It is an oral non-biologic controller for asthma and allergic rhinitis.

It is less potent than inhaled corticosteroids for asthma control and is add-on or alternative therapy per guidelines. FDA added boxed warning for serious neuropsychiatric events (agitation, depression, suicidal thinking) in 2020; risk-benefit should be discussed with patients.

Metabolism via CYP2C8 and 3A4; few clinically significant interactions.

## Primary Uses

- Mild persistent asthma (alternative or add-on to inhaled corticosteroids)
- Exercise-induced bronchoconstriction prophylaxis
- Allergic rhinitis (seasonal and perennial)
- Aspirin-exacerbated respiratory disease (adjunct)

Not for acute bronchospasm relief.

## Common Side Effects

- Headache
- Abdominal pain, nausea, diarrhea
- Mood and behavior changes (agitation, anxiety, depression, suicidal ideation—boxed warning)
- Eosinophilic conditions including Churg-Strauss vasculitis (rare, historically associated with steroid withdrawal)
- **Warnings:** Educate patients and caregivers on neuropsychiatric symptoms; discontinue if behavior changes occur; not first-line monotherapy for moderate-severe asthma.
