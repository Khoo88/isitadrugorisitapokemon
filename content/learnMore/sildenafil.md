## What is Sildenafil?

Sildenafil is a phosphodiesterase type 5 (PDE5) inhibitor that prevents breakdown of cyclic GMP in smooth muscle, enhancing nitric oxide–mediated vasodilation. In the corpus cavernosum, this facilitates penile erection with sexual stimulation; in pulmonary arterial hypertension, it reduces pulmonary vascular resistance.

Hepatic metabolism via CYP3A4; food delays absorption. Concomitant nitrates cause profound hypotension and are absolutely contraindicated. Alpha-blockers and other vasodilators increase hypotension risk.

Also sold under Revatio branding for pulmonary hypertension at different dosing.

## Primary Uses

- Erectile dysfunction
- Pulmonary arterial hypertension (Revatio dosing)
- Raynaud phenomenon (off-label)

Take on demand for ED typically 30–60 minutes before activity; not more than once daily.

## Common Side Effects

- Headache
- Flushing
- Dyspepsia, nasal congestion
- Visual color changes (blue tinge—uncommon)
- Hypotension, dizziness
- Priapism (rare emergency)
- **Warnings:** Absolute contraindication with nitrates and riociguat; caution within 24–48 hours of nitrate use; cardiovascular risk assessment for sexual activity; non-arteritic anterior ischemic optic neuropathy (rare); hearing loss (rare).
