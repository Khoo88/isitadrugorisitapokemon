## Who is Runerigus?

Runerigus is a Ground/Ghost-type Pokémon from Generation VIII, a Galarian evolution of Yamask when it passes under a stone arch with low HP. Its sand body inscribed with cursed engravings embodies archaeological horror—history literally chasing intruders.

Galarian museums display Runerigus fragments beside real runestones, warning that desecrating tombs invites possession by collective ancestral rage.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Runerigus’s markings record names of those who disturbed graves, allegedly shortening lifespans in superstition. Pokédex entries describe it dragging prey into murals, trapping them in two-dimensional torment.

Sandstorms near desert ruins sometimes form its silhouette briefly—a mirage or migration, debaters still argue.

## Battle Performance

Runerigus has Wandering Spirit, swapping abilities on contact, plus high Defense and Will-O-Wisp. Body Press scales with Defense; Shadow Claw hits Ghosts.

It walls physical attackers in UU, but Water and Grass moves hurt. Mummy-like strategies pair with Trick Room or hazard stacking for chip.
