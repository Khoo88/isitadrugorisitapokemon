## What is Venlafaxine?

Venlafaxine is a serotonin-norepinephrine reuptake inhibitor (SNRI) that blocks SERT and NET, increasing synaptic serotonin and norepinephrine. At low doses serotonin effects predominate; norepinephrine reuptake inhibition increases at higher doses. Active metabolite desvenlafaxine (Pristiq) contributes to activity.

Extended-release formulation allows once-daily dosing and smoother adverse effect profile. Discontinuation syndrome can be severe with abrupt cessation—gradual taper over weeks is recommended.

Blood pressure may increase at higher doses; periodic monitoring is advised.

## Primary Uses

- Major depressive disorder
- Generalized anxiety disorder
- Social anxiety disorder
- Panic disorder
- Vasomotor symptoms of menopause (off-label/low-dose XR)

## Common Side Effects

- Nausea, dry mouth, constipation
- Sweating
- Insomnia or somnolence
- Sexual dysfunction
- Elevated blood pressure (dose-related)
- Serotonin syndrome (with other serotonergic agents)
- Discontinuation syndrome (dizziness, brain zaps, irritability)
- **Warnings:** Suicidality boxed warning; taper slowly; caution in uncontrolled hypertension; may increase bleeding with NSAIDs; avoid MAOIs within 14 days.
