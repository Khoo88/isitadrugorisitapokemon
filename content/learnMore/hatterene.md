## Who is Hatterene?

Hatterene is a Psychic/Fairy-type Pokémon from Generation VIII, evolving from Hattrem. Its witch silhouette and scythe-like braids enforce a “quiet please” doctrine—punishing loud emotions and physical attackers alike in lore and VGC.

Galarian tales warn children not to shout near moors where Hatterene broods, lest they be lifted telekinetically into fog. It became a defining Trick Room attacker and supporter post-release.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Hatterene emits psychic waves from its “hat” that silence noise within hundreds of meters. Pokédex entries describe it destroying loud machinery near nests—a metaphor for intolerance of chaos turned literal.

It reads surface thoughts to preempt attacks, explaining competitive Magic Bounce synergy as biological, not merely ability-based flavor.

## Battle Performance

Hatterene excels in Trick Room with Magic Bounce, Dazzling Gleam, Psychic, and Draining Kiss. Life Orb Mystical Fire sets punish Steel-types.

Its low Speed becomes advantage under Trick Room. Contact moves trigger optional Life Dew support sets in doubles. Dark and Steel pressure it, but redirection partners mitigate threats.
