## What is Zubsolv?

Zubsolv is a sublingual tablet combining buprenorphine, a partial mu-opioid agonist, and naloxone, an opioid antagonist included to deter intravenous misuse (naloxone is inactive when taken sublingually as directed but causes withdrawal if injected). Buprenorphine reduces craving and withdrawal in opioid use disorder while ceiling effects limit respiratory depression compared with full agonists.

High bioavailability sublingual formulation allows smaller tablet burden than some generic buprenorphine/naloxone films. Schedule III controlled substance; prescribing requires waiver/training per jurisdiction (U.S. DATA 2000 and subsequent legislative updates).

Must be initiated when patient is in mild to moderate withdrawal to avoid precipitated withdrawal from naloxone component if misused or from buprenorphine affinity displacing full agonists.

## Primary Uses

- Maintenance treatment of opioid use disorder (medication-assisted treatment)
- Induction and maintenance when transitioning from full opioid agonists under clinician supervision

Part of comprehensive addiction treatment including psychosocial support.

## Common Side Effects

- Headache, nausea, constipation
- Insomnia, sweating
- Tongue pain, mouth numbness (sublingual)
- Precipitated withdrawal if started too early
- Respiratory depression (especially with benzodiazepines or other CNS depressants)
- **Warnings:** Risk Evaluation and Mitigation Strategies; neonatal withdrawal in pregnancy; hepatic impairment dosing; avoid unsupervised benzodiazepine combination; store securely to prevent diversion; counseling on proper sublingual technique.
