## What is Varenicline?

Varenicline is a partial agonist at alpha-4 beta-2 nicotinic acetylcholine receptors, reducing withdrawal symptoms and craving while blocking nicotine binding to diminish reinforcement from smoking. It is among the most effective pharmacologic smoking cessation aids in clinical trials.

Titration over the first week reduces nausea. Neuropsychiatric adverse events (mood changes, agitation, suicidal ideation) were historically boxed; updated labeling reflects no significant increase in neuropsychiatric events versus placebo in post-marketing studies, though monitoring remains prudent.

Eliminated renally with minimal metabolism; dose reduction in severe renal impairment.

## Primary Uses

- Smoking cessation aid (12-week course, often with behavioral support)
- Maintenance or repeat courses for relapse prevention (per labeling)

Start 1 week before quit date for many protocols.

## Common Side Effects

- Nausea (most common, dose-related)
- Abnormal dreams, insomnia
- Headache
- Constipation, flatulence
- Mood changes, depression, suicidal ideation (monitor)
- **Warnings:** Counsel on mood and behavior changes; cardiovascular safety improved in studies versus prior concerns; not established in pregnancy; may interact with alcohol tolerance (reduced pleasurable effects reported).
