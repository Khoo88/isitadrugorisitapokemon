## Who is Zeraora?

Zeraora is an Electric-type Mythical Pokémon from Generation VII, a bipedal thundercat that runs at lightning speed without flying. It appeared in Ultra events and films as heroic figure who channels electricity through claws rather than storing it internally.

Alolan myth calls Zeraora the lightning that chose a body, protecting islands when Tapu conflict escalates. Its standing is action-hero Mythical rather than ancient god.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Zeraora emits electric claws by ionizing air, not storing charge in organs—unique among Electric types. Pokédex entries describe it helping power plants during crises, then vanishing before thanks.

Its fur conducts without shocking allies, allowing pack grooming rituals that strengthen social bonds.

## Battle Performance

Zeraora is a high-speed physical attacker with Plasma Fists (Electric move that neutralizes Ground immunity temporarily), Close Combat, and Knock Off. Volt Switch pivots in OU when allowed.

Fragile but fast; Ground types still threaten unless Plasma Fists applies. It competes in VGC as anti-tapu tech and burst damage dealer in restricted formats.
