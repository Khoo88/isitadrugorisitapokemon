## Who is Terrakion?

Terrakion is a Rock/Fighting-type Legendary Pokémon from Generation V, one of the Swords of Justice alongside Cobalion, Virizion, and Keldeo. It embodies brute force applied to protect Pokémon harmed by human expansion.

Unovan epics depict Terrakion shattering castle walls to liberate captured forest species, leaving hoofprints in bedrock still visible in Victory Road tours.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Terrakion’s horns can penetrate steel plating; its charge creates shockwaves that uproot trees. Pokédex entries describe it teaching Pokémon to fight back against oppressors—a revolutionary figure in myth.

It rarely speaks to humans, communicating instead through earth tremors partners interpret as battle plans.

## Battle Performance

Terrakion is a physical wallbreaker with Swords Dance, Stone Edge, Close Combat, and Rock Polish. High Speed and Attack threaten offensive teams in OU and Ubers.

Weak to Ground, Fighting, Water, Grass, Steel, and Psychic—but coverage and STAB power often trade favorably. Choice Band sets remain popular for immediate pressure.
