## Who is Deoxys?

Deoxys is a Psychic-type Mythical Pokémon introduced in Generation III, born from a mutated space virus. Its ability to shift forms—Normal, Attack, Defense, and Speed—makes it an alien tactician unlike any earthly species.

Hoenn’s Mossdeep incident established Deoxys in modern lore as a reminder of cosmic influence on the planet. Researchers study its DNA reconfiguration as a model for adaptive extremophiles.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Deoxys rewrites its limb structure in response to threat assessment, a process visible as aurora-like pulses along its torso. Pokédex entries claim it originated on an asteroid, arriving during meteor showers that altered ozone readings globally.

It communicates via electromagnetic bursts that interfere with devices—trainers must use shielded balls when approaching wild specimens.

## Battle Performance

Deoxys-Attack is a hyper offense staple with huge Attack and Speed, running Psycho Boost, Superpower, and Extreme Speed. Deoxys-Speed sets webs and spikes before suicide leading; Deoxys-Defense walls with absurd physical bulk.

Each form competes in formats allowing Mythicals. Normal form is flexible but overshadowed by specialized forms. Hazards and priority are universal checks.
