## Who is Pecharunt?

Pecharunt is a Poison/Ghost-type Mythical Pokémon from Generation IX, tied to Kitakami’s Mochi Mystery. Its peach-shaped mask and toxic confectionery theme cast it as a trickster spirit that binds people through cursed mochi in legend.

The Loyal Three narrative retroactively frames Pecharunt as manipulator behind Fezandipiti, Okidogi, and Munkidori’s corruption—a Mythical puppeteer rather than brute force legend.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Pecharunt’s toxins induce obsession with its gifts, mimicking affection while draining vitality. Pokédex entries describe mochi that tastes divine yet poisons slowly—a fairy tale warning about strangers bearing sweets.

Mask fragments found in shrines are sealed in lacquer boxes; priests perform annual rites to prevent “gratitude sickness” among pilgrims.

## Battle Performance

Pecharunt is a fast special attacker with Poison Puppeteer, corrupting poisoned foes’ moves. Malignant Chain and Shadow Ball form offensive cores; Nasty Plot boosts sweeping potential.

It competes in OU as a status-centric breaker. Steel and Dark types resist its plan; removal of poisoned partners requires careful switching to avoid self-sabotage from Puppeteer.
