## What is Phentermine?

Phentermine is a sympathomimetic amine structurally related to amphetamine that suppresses appetite primarily by increasing norepinephrine release in the hypothalamus, reducing hunger signals. It is a Schedule IV controlled substance with potential for dependence and cardiovascular stimulation.

Short-term use (typically ≤12 weeks) is approved as adjunct to caloric restriction and exercise for obesity; longer-term combinations (e.g., with topiramate as Qsymia) extend duration under different labeling. Tolerance to appetite suppression may develop.

Contraindicated with MAO inhibitors, cardiovascular disease, hyperthyroidism, and glaucoma. It is not a substitute for lifestyle modification and comprehensive obesity management.

## Primary Uses

- Short-term weight reduction adjunct in obesity (BMI ≥30, or ≥27 with comorbidities)
- Component of combination obesity pharmacotherapy (with topiramate in approved combination product)

Reserved for patients who have not achieved adequate weight loss with lifestyle alone and lack contraindications.

## Common Side Effects

- Insomnia, restlessness, anxiety
- Dry mouth, unpleasant taste
- Elevated heart rate and blood pressure
- Constipation, diarrhea
- Palpitations
- **Warnings:** Risk of primary pulmonary hypertension and valvular heart disease (historical fenfluramine combination concern—phentermine alone has lower risk but cardiovascular monitoring required); dependence potential; contraindicated in pregnancy; avoid late-day dosing due to insomnia.
