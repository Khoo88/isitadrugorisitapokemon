## Who is Claydol?

Claydol is a Ground/Psychic-type Pokémon evolving from Baltoy, shaped like a twin-faced clay idol brought to life. It guards desert ruins across Hoenn, often found patrolling ancient corridors in synchronized pairs.

Mythology links Claydol to extraterrestrial clay dolls animated by psychic energy—explaining its levitation and geometric patterns. Archaeologists treat wild Claydol as living security systems for forbidden chambers.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Claydol emits psychic beams from its arms to carve tunnels and levitate boulders. Pokédex entries state it has existed for millennia, predating many human civilizations, and communicates via resonant hums that induce dizziness in intruders.

Pairs rotate in opposite directions during rituals at solstice, aligning with star maps etched into ruin floors—behavior still studied by astronomers.

## Battle Performance

Claydol offers Rapid Spin removal, Stealth Rock, and Cosmic Power boosting alongside Recover. Its balanced defenses let it serve as a spinner on balanced teams in lower tiers.

Offensive sets can use Earth Power and Psychic with Choice Specs, but mediocre Speed and common Dark/Ghost/Ground threats limit prominence.
