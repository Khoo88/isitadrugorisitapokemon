## Who is Dialga?

Dialga is a Steel/Dragon-type Legendary Pokémon from Generation IV, master of time within Sinnoh creation myth. Its metallic plates and crystalline crest evoke a clockwork dragon guarding temporal stability across the Pokémon universe.

Myths with Palkia and Giratina describe Dialga’s roar as the tick of cosmic order—without it, timelines fracture. Mount Coronet shrines depict it as the arbiter preventing paradox catastrophes.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Dialga can accelerate or slow localized time fields, aging metal to rust or restoring wilted plants in seconds per legend. Pokédex entries emphasize its heart beats once per millennium, each pulse resetting certain geological processes.

Temporal anomalies near Spear Pillar often coincide with Dialga sightings, researched under strict League oversight.

## Battle Performance

Dialga is a bulky special attacker with pressure or telepathy, running Draco Meteor, Flash Cannon, Fire Blast, and Thunder. Assault Vest variants wall rain teams; Choice Scarf sets surprise sweepers.

Its resistances are exceptional, though Fighting and Ground moves threaten it. In restricted formats, Dialga remains a defining defensive pivot and late-game cleaner.
