## Who is Cryogonal?

Cryogonal is an Ice-type Pokémon from Generation V, resembling a living snowflake hexagon that drifts through alpine storms. It lacks evolution but embodies crystalline precision—beautiful, cold, and mathematically symmetrical.

Unovan mountaineers treat Cryogonal sightings as blizzard omens. Photographers chase them during whiteouts, capturing geometric light refraction that inspires art installations in Icirrus City.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Cryogonal forms from atmospheric moisture flash-frozen around mineral nuclei. Pokédex entries describe it melting when temperatures rise, teleporting via molecular reassembly in colder air—a pseudo-immortality cycle.

It freezes prey with beam attacks, then consumes microscopic nutrients from ice shavings. Swarms create audible harmonic ringing during wind shear events.

## Battle Performance

Cryogonal is a fast special attacker with high Special Defense but abysmal physical Defense and HP. It runs Freeze-Dry, Ice Beam, Hidden Power Fire, and Rapid Spin for hazard removal unique among Ice-types.

Glass cannon builds need screens or snow support. Its spin utility and ability to hit Water-types super-effectively with Freeze-Dry give niche value on dedicated hail teams.
