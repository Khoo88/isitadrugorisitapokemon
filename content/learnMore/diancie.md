## Who is Diancie?

Diancie is a Rock/Fairy-type Mythical Pokémon from Generation VI, a sudden mutation of Carbink bearing royal jewel aesthetics. It can compress carbon into diamonds, earning titles like “Royal Pink Princess” in Kalos high society.

Diancie’s movie lore portrays it as a guardian of hidden sanctuaries, creating crystal gardens to shelter endangered Pokémon. Its rarity makes it a symbol of geological miracle.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Diancie’s core emits waves that rearrange carbon atoms into diamond lattices. Pokédex entries describe it blessing allies with crystalline armor during crises, then vanishing into caverns to sleep for decades.

Courtiers in fictional Kalos tales seek Diancie blessings for engagements, though real specimens resist captivity, teleporting via flash blindness.

## Battle Performance

Mega Diancie was a terrifying mixed sweeper with Diamond Storm and Moonblast. Base Diancie offers Stealth Rock, Moonblast, and Diamond Storm with Clear Body preventing stat drops.

Its mediocre Speed and weaknesses to Steel and Ground limit OU presence, but in formats allowing Mega Evolution or Mythicals, it can break stall with powerful special coverage and setup potential.
