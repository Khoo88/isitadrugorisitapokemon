## Who is Altaria?

Altaria is a Dragon/Flying-type Pokémon introduced in Generation III, evolving from Swablu. Before Mega Evolution was retired from standard play, Altaria was famous for its cloudlike plumage and gentle songbird persona—earning it the nickname “Humming Pokémon” across Hoenn.

In setting lore, Altaria nests on high mountain peaks above the clouds, where it is said to ride warm updrafts for hours without flapping. Trainers describe it as affectionate and melodious, contrasting sharply with fiercer dragons like Salamence that share its homeland.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Altaria’s cottony wings are packed with hollow filaments that trap heat and soften impacts. Pokédex entries claim its singing harmonizes with wind resonance in valleys, producing melodies that calm aggressive Pokémon during territorial disputes.

Mega Altaria lore (where still referenced) describes its feathers becoming meteor-like streaks of cloud, temporarily amplifying its draconic ancestry. Even in base form, stories tell of Altaria grooming smaller Pokémon beneath its wings during storms.

## Battle Performance

Historically, Mega Altaria was a bulky Dragon dancer using Dragon Dance, Pixilate-boosted Hyper Voice, and Heal Bell support. In modern generations without Mega Evolution, standard Altaria functions as a niche special attacker or support Pokémon with Cotton Guard, Roost, and Perish Song tricks.

Its typing grants Ground immunity when airborne but leaves it vulnerable to Ice and Fairy attacks. Competitive use is limited, though Dragon Dance sets can surprise unprepared teams in lower tiers.
