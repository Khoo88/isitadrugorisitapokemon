## Who is Gorebyss?

Gorebyss is a Water-type Pokémon from Generation III, evolving from Clamperl via Deep Sea Scale trade. Its slender eel form and draconic fins contrast Huntail’s brute aesthetic, embodying deep-sea elegance in Hoenn mythology.

Divers associate Gorebyss with warm currents and coral cathedrals, where schools spiral in mating dances mistaken for sea serpents. It is the graceful half of Hoenn’s most mysterious branched evolution.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Gorebyss’s mouth probe drains fluids from prey with surgical precision. Pokédex entries describe it inserting its snout into rock crevices, harvesting nutrients without disturbing reef structure—a keystone predator role.

Pink scales reflect bioluminescent symbionts that flare during storms, guiding lost ships away from shoals in sailor gratitude tales.

## Battle Performance

Gorebyss is a high Special Attack sweeper with Swift Swim in rain, Ice Beam, Surf, and Hidden Power Electric. Deep Sea Scale boosts Special Defense when held pre-evolution.

Rare in modern tiers due to frailty and outclassed water sweepers, but rain hyper offense still experiments with +2 Speed under Swift Swim. Huntail fills physical niche separately.
