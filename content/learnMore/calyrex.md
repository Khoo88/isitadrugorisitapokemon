## Who is Calyrex?

Calyrex is a Psychic/Grass-type Legendary Pokémon from Generation VIII’s Crown Tundra, known as the King of Galar who united humans and Pokémon in ancient times. Its regal, stag-like design and crown of foliage tie it to agriculture, prophecy, and rulership myths.

Galarian history texts portray Calyrex as a benevolent sovereign who could see future harvests, later fading from memory until revived in modern legend quests. It bonds uniquely with Glastrier or Spectrier through fusion forms.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Calyrex communicates via telepathic pollen clouds that induce vivid visions of possible futures. Pokédex entries describe it planting seeds that sprout overnight into fields, earning loyalty from farming clans who treated it as divine.

Without its steed, Calyrex is frail but wise; lore insists it willingly surrendered power to teach humility, hiding in Crown Tundra ruins until worthy trainers arrived.

## Battle Performance

Base Calyrex is niche, but Ice Rider and Shadow Rider forms are top-tier. Ice Rider offers Nuclear Glue offense with Glacial Lance; Shadow Rider provides special sweeping with Astral Barrage and Nasty Plot.

Support sets can use Pollen Puff healing in doubles. Choice Scarf Shadow Rider remains a defining meta threat in formats allowing restricted legends.
