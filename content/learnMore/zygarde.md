## Who is Zygarde?

Zygarde is a Dragon/Ground-type Legendary Pokémon from Generation VI, guardian of ecosystem balance composed of Zygarde Cells and Cores. Its 10%, 50%, and Complete Formes represent escalating response to environmental threat—Kalos and Alola lore’s cellular immune system.

Team Flare’s experiments and Necrozma-era crises awakened Complete Forme, a kaiju serpent that restores order when the planet’s cells cry out in unison.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Zygarde monitors ecosystems via microscopic cells in soil and water, assembling forms when pollution or Ultra Beast incursions spike. Pokédex entries describe Complete Forme as judge and executioner of natural law.

Cells resemble green gelatin; crushing one alerts others miles away, creating swarm intelligence without central brain until cores merge.

## Battle Performance

Zygarde-Complete is a bulky setup sweeper with Power Construct, Thousand Arrows (grounds Flying types), and Extreme Speed. 50% form runs Dragon Dance and Coil in UU.

Thousand Waves traps; Thousand Arrows breaks Levitate meta. Complete Forme dominates Ubers when allowed, with Coil boosting Attack, Defense, and accuracy for unstoppable sweeps.
