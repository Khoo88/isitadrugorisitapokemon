## Who is Groudon?

Groudon is a Ground-type Legendary Pokémon from Generation III, embodiment of land and drought in Hoenn creation myth. Its slumber beneath magma beds shapes continents; awakening triggers sun-scorched deserts across the globe in legend.

Teams Magma’s historical crusade and Primal Groudon’s Desolate Land made Groudon a metagame-defining deity. It stands beside Kyogre and Rayquaza as weather sovereign of the Pokémon world’s geological order.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Groudon’s body absorbs sunlight into magma veins, expanding landmass by volcanic eruption when active. Pokédex entries describe it as the Pokémon who fought Kyogre for eons, only Rayquaza quelling their clash.

Cave paintings in Hoenn depict Groudon’s hide as living basalt, plants growing in its footprints centuries later—a fertility myth for agrarian cultures.

## Battle Performance

Primal Groudon dominates Ubers with Desolate Land, Precipice Blades, Fire Punch, and Rock-type coverage. Base Groudon sets Stealth Rock, Thunder Wave, and phazing in older formats.

Ground/Fire Primal typing is offensively terrifying; Water types fail under sun. Checks include Dragon Tail phazing and strong water moves only in rare neutralizing weather scenarios.
