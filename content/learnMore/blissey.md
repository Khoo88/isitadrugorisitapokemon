## Who is Blissey?

Blissey is a Normal-type Pokémon evolving from Chansey, renowned as the ultimate supportive healer in competitive history. Its egg-shaped silhouette and nurturing demeanor made it a staple of Pokémon Centers long before it became a battle format icon.

Across regions, Blissey represents compassion and endurance—carrying eggs that restore vitality and refusing to flee from sick companions. Elite trainers respect it as a cornerstone of stall and doubles healing cores.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Blissey’s eggs are nutrient-dense, adapting flavor and minerals to the recipient’s needs. Pokédex entries describe it sharing egg fragments only with those it trusts, while secreting calming pheromones that reduce aggression in entire rooms.

Its fluffy coat absorbs impact, explaining extraordinary HP pools. Some hospitals employ Blissey in rehabilitation wings, monitoring patients overnight with soft humming.

## Battle Performance

Blissey walls special attackers with colossal HP, Soft-Boiled recovery, and Seismic Toss or Toxic for damage. Natural Cure clears status on switch-out, while Heal Bell supports teams.

It fears physical attackers and Ghost-types, so pivoting is essential. Eviolite Chansey sometimes outclasses it in raw special bulk, but Blissey’s movepool and healing output remain legendary in stall metas.
