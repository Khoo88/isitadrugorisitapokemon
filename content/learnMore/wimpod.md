## Who is Wimpod?

Wimpod is a Bug/Water-type Pokémon from Generation VII, evolving into Golisopod. Its Wimp Out ability and constant anxiety made it a relatable meme—cowardly scavenger that becomes armored berserker upon evolution.

Alolan beaches feature Wimpod skittering from shadows, cleaning scraps while avoiding any eye contact. Trainers joke that raising one builds patience before payoff.

## Biological Quirks & Lore

Wimpod’s antenna detect danger at 100 meters, triggering flight before predators see it. Pokédex entries describe it as fastidiously clean, eating only fresh debris—a picky janitor of tide pools.

Shed shells are translucent and collectible, used in craft markets until allergies to residue closed the trade.

## Battle Performance

Wimpod has Wimp Out, switching at half HP—useless offensively but enabling pivot strategies into Golisopod with First Impression.

In LC it can run Spikes before fleeing. Competitive relevance is entirely post-evolution; Wimpod itself is fodder except for emergency switches.
